### Behaviour that consumers rely on

- **Index stability.** The geometry and sampling of every vessel depend only on the genome and the ostia, not on lesions or stents. A tree rebuilt with different lesions has the same vessels, the same sample arrays (the very same objects) and the same `pathMm` and `param`. AngioPci's `pciGeom(tree, refTree)` and `pciDevices` index one tree's radii with another tree's indices, so this matters.
- **Side.** Every branch has its parent's `side`. With `anomalous-lcx-right-sinus` the whole circumflex system (LCx, marginals, and in left or codominant dominance the PDA, posterolateral and AV-nodal branches) is `side: "right"`, because it opacifies with a right-sinus injection. SYNTAX labels follow the parent artery, not the side: a PDA from that LCx is still segment 15. Calibre scaling follows the arterial system (the circumflex keeps its own scale).
- **Naming**
  - Named vessels keep the old names: `LM`, `LAD`, `LCx`, `RCA`, `CONUS`, `RV`, `AM`, `PDA`, `PLV`, `PL2`, `D1`, `D2`, `S1`…`Sn`, `OM1`, `OM2`, `RAMUS`.
  - New named vessels: `D3`, `D4`, `OM3`, `OM4`, `RV2`, `RV3`, `PL3`, `SAN`, `SAN2`, `AVN`, and septals up to `S12`.
  - Space-filling branches are called `<host>-twig<k>`. AngioPci excludes them from wire navigation because the name contains "twig".
  - The LM is the first left vessel in the array, so xray.test's tube lookup keeps working.
- **Origin spacing.** Named side-branch origins on one parent are at least 3.5-4 mm apart. Two branches leaving at one point would make the wire's choice ambiguous (angioPci.test).

## 2. What was built

### Motion field (heartMotion.ts)

The displacement of a material point is the sum of six terms:

- **Base descent towards a nearly fixed apex.** The annular amplitude varies round the ring, from Ochs 2017: lateral tricuspid annulus, where the RCA runs, 26 mm; mitral lateral 16; septal 13; anterior aortic 14.
- **Twist about the long axis.** The apex turns anticlockwise by +9.7° and the base clockwise by -5.4°, seen from the apex. These are the means of Cheng 2017 and Halcsik 2026; Buckberg 2011 gives the sign and the order of untwisting.
- **Radial thickening.** Amplitude 0.12 of the radius, fitted to the library (`fit_motion.ts`). That is about twice a physiological epicardial inward motion; it also stands in for the whole-heart rotation, which is not modelled.
- **Whole-heart translation** towards the left, inferior and anterior (Shechter 2006).
- **RV free-wall (acute-margin) swing.**
- **RV phase lead** of 30 ms.

The timing is in seconds from the `ClockState`:

1. An onset delay (45 ms at RR 1 s, scaled with the square root of RR like the rest of systole).
2. Ejection up to the clock's Bazett-scaled end-systole.
3. Isovolumic relaxation, during which the twist unwinds first.
4. Rapid filling with a fast return.
5. A diastasis drift, which vanishes at high heart rates as relaxation and rapid filling compress.
6. An atrial kick that completes the return at R.

`samplePos` applies the field to the cached rest position, so the per-frame cost is one displacement per sample.

The aortic root descends with the base. That descent is attenuated with height, because the ascending aorta tethers it. With `motionAt`, samples fixed in the aortic frame (ostia, LM, proximal RCA) move with the root, and the blend samples pass smoothly from root motion to heart motion. With a legacy `{contraction}` motion they stay still, so nothing separates from the catheter until sceneBuilder applies `rootDisplacement` to the catheter too.

`bridgeCompression` is 0 in diastole and 1 at end-systole.

### Heart shape and silhouette

The heart surface is the smooth union (3 mm blend) of an LV ellipsoid and a right-anterior RV ellipsoid. The anterior and posterior interventricular grooves are where the two meet, tabulated per heart; the scan ranges are wide enough for the sampled roll, and holes in a table are filled from their neighbours. The groove fat fills the crease. Offsets follow the true surface normal, so septal perforators run into the septum.

`heartShapes` fits the LV and RV ellipsoids to the deformed surface every frame. It uses an affine least-squares fit of control points moved by the same field, then an eigen-decomposition. It adds a 3 mm epicardial fat shell (carved), the RA, LA, LAA, RVOT, a cardiophrenic fat pad and the root.

### Genome

`genomeForCase(c)` is seeded by the case id. The case's dominance and variants always win; the genome fills in what the case does not say.

**Cited priors.** The ramus prior is used only when no variant list is given, because the ramus is a case-visible vessel.
- Dominance (Knaapen 2012).
- Ramus (Medrano-Gracia 2017).
- Number of diagonals and marginals (Manpoong 2023).
- Wrap-around LAD (Kobayashi 2015; a selected anterior-STEMI cohort, bracketed by Manpoong and Ilia).
- Sinoatrial nodal artery origin, including duplication (Vikse 2016).
- Myocardial bridging, on the LAD in 82% (Hostiuc 2018).
- LM length and bifurcation angle (Medrano-Gracia 2016).
- LM diameter, with the sex and LVH calibre factors (Dodge 1992).
- Near-ostial diameters of the first diagonal, the obtuse marginal and the ramus (Divia Paul 2018, Table 2, as ratios to the cohort's own left main, per sex).
- Septal perforator count, 6-12, mean 9 (Kesieme 2024).

**Model parameters:**
- Share of women (0.38; it equals the Manpoong cohort mix but is not a population prior).
- RV and posterolateral branch counts.
- Tortuosity index.
- Heart size, axis and length. The mean axis (azimuth 50°, elevation 30°, roll -8°) and the base-apex length factor 1.22 come from `fit_axis.ts` (section 3); the RV size factor 0.9 keeps the RCA's ostium-to-crux course at 125-175 mm.

### Growth

**Named vessels:**
- The LAD runs in the anterior groove. Its entry is always below and to the left of the bifurcation. It continues to or around the apex.
- The LCx runs in the left AV groove under the appendage, ending by dominance.
- The RCA leaves its ostium for the nearest point of the right AV groove at least 2 mm below it, runs to the crux, then gives the posterolateral continuation in right dominance.
- Branches:
  - PDA in the posterior groove.
  - Posterolateral branches.
  - AV-nodal artery turning up at the crux.
  - Sinoatrial nodal artery from the RCA (about 45-55 mm) or the circumflex (70-85 mm), ending at the SVC-right atrial junction. The course and length are model parameters; no opened source gives an angiographic length.
  - Conus.
  - RV branches.
  - Acute marginal.
  - Diagonals.
  - Septals: S1 the longest (about 50 mm), later ones shorter.
  - Obtuse marginals, fanning over the lateral and inferolateral wall.
  - Ramus.

**Proximal courses.** These are cubic Beziers that leave along the ostial direction and arrive along the groove path's own tangent. Every proximal sample carries the surface coordinates of its own rest point, so there is no kink at the junction. The heart is seated per genome so that the top of the anterior groove lies below the LM bifurcation, which means the LAD never climbs.

**Tortuosity.**
- Three octaves (3.5-8, 8-20 and 20-60 mm) in the local surface frame.
- A minimum radius of curvature of 2.2 mm.
- The undulation starts from zero where the surface course begins.
- It is damped at the apex singularity.
- Epicardial heights are kept between 0.6 and 6 mm.
- Corkscrew and loop phenotypes are available.

**Space colonisation.** Three generations of small branches grow into the largest gaps of a coverage-distance field and stop near other territories, so there are no parallel combs. The third generation reaches about 0.4 mm radius. The first generation is long and steered apically (up to 90 mm). The apical third of the LAD gives no first-generation branches, because it is the LAD's own territory.

**Territory.** Each patch of epicardium is drained by the nearest vessel, with an order offset: the LAD wins patches within 4.5 mm of being nearest along its course and within 10 mm over the apical quarter (the apex is LAD territory in most hearts). Every terminal tip owns 50 mm² beyond its end. Septal and nodal arteries carry territory per mm. The first septals carry the most (S1 ×2).

**Calibres.**
- Calibres follow D ∝ Q^(3/7) (Huo-Kassab, Gong 2016). Q is the territory drained downstream; the RV free wall has weight 0.4.
- The flow constant is set so that proximal LAD = genome LM × 3.7/4.5 (Dodge).
- The RCA system and the circumflex system are each pulled towards the Dodge mean for the dominance. 30% of the territory-driven log deviation is kept, then clamped at ±1.5 SD. The territories alone over-size both against the LAD.
- The first diagonal, the first marginal and the ramus get at least their Divia Paul near-ostial diameters. If the surface territory near the base gives them less, the missing flow is spread along the branch, which also feeds the parent upstream.
- The first diagonal and first marginal are at least ρ × the largest later branch of their series, with ρ drawn per patient from U(0.8, 1.1). This is a model rule: the first of a series is the larger in most patients, not all. Without it, the territory near the base made OM1 smaller than OM2 in 30 of 34 genomes.
- Every terminal tip tapers to 0.12 mm over 4 mm, with an iodine fade.

**SYNTAX labels** follow the syntaxscore.org definitions: 1-16c. Septals, RV, acute marginal, conus and nodal arteries are not scored and are labelled null. Between OM1 and the last OM the circumflex is labelled 13, as a convention.

### Lesions and stents (coronaryTree.ts)

Radii, occlusion, calcium, thrombus and collaterals are applied per build on the cached topology. Descendants of an occluded vessel get no antegrade contrast. Vessels with a lesion or stent get `fine`: samples spaced at most 0.25 mm within ±(lesion half-length + 1 mm) and ±1.5 mm around stent edges, with an exact sample at every lesion centre.

### Variants as geometry

- **Separate ostia:** the LCx comes from `leftOstium2` and there is no LM.
- **Shepherd's crook:** a superior proximal loop.
- **Tortuous RCA:** index ×1.8 and long-wavelength loops.
- **Myocardial bridge:** a 10-30 mm segment 3.8 mm under the epicardium, with systolic narrowing through `vesselRadiiAt`.
- **`anomalous-rca-left-sinus`:** the ostium is in the left sinus, higher than the LM, with an interarterial course in front of the root.
- **`anomalous-lcx-right-sinus`:** the ostium is in the right sinus behind the RCA, with a retroaortic course. The whole circumflex system is `side: "right"`.

The two anomalous names are matched as strings until S1 adds them to `AnatomyVariant`.

### Performance

From `perf.ts`, Apple silicon laptop, Node:
- Per frame, motion of all about 5,200 coronary samples plus root displacement for 120 catheter nodes: median 0.52 ms (p90 0.58).
- Silhouette fit (`heartShapes`): 0.03 ms.
- Growing a new patient: about 65 ms (p90 72), once per genome, cached up to 24 genomes.
- Re-applying lesions and a stent: 0.15 ms.
- Trees have 4,600-7,000 samples in 180-300 vessels. The pre-wave tree had 2,220 samples in 119 vessels.

GPU cost. The silhouette is 10 ellipsoids, and with S4's merge 7 of them replace existing ones, so that part is unchanged. The coronary tube count is about 2.5 times the pre-wave tree, which matters if the tube pass (S2/S5) costs per segment. About 1,600 samples per tree (27%) belong to vessels whose largest radius is under 0.25 mm. They add little to the image; the integrator can skip them in the scene if the frame budget needs it (`Math.max(...v.radii) < 0.25`).

```ts
    const place = (sm: Sample) => moveWorld(samplePos(H, sm, m), rootW + (1 - rootW) * sm.w);
```

The return value can keep `contraction: e`. Keep exporting `contraction(phase)` for older callers and tests.

The integrator must check one thing. xray.test's "catheter tip and the left main ostium project to the same place" runs at `t = 0`. There `motionAt` gives zero displacement (R wave, before onset), so it passes. In systole the tip and the ostium move together only when the catheter is tethered (`legacyCatheter: true`) or carried by the rod solver.

### 4.2 thorax.ts (S4): consume the fitted silhouette by name

S4's `mergeHeartShapes` already takes `heartShapes`. It replaces HEART and HEART_FAT with entries 0 and 1 and appends the rest. To avoid duplicating S4's own RA, LA, LAA, RV, RV_FAT, RVOT and FAT_PAD, replace by name instead:

```ts
const BY_NAME: Record<string, number> = {
  "lv myocardium": ANAT.HEART,
  "lv epicardial fat": ANAT.HEART_FAT,
  "rv myocardium": ANAT.RV,
  "rv epicardial fat": ANAT.RV_FAT,
  "right atrium": ANAT.RA,
  "left atrium": ANAT.LA,
  "left atrial appendage": ANAT.LAA,
  "right ventricular outflow": ANAT.RVOT,
  "cardiophrenic fat pad": ANAT.FAT_PAD,
};
function mergeHeartShapes(shapes: AnatShape[], heart: AnatShape[]): AnatShape[] {
  const out = [...shapes];
  for (const h of heart) {
    const i = h.name ? BY_NAME[h.name] : undefined;
    if (i !== undefined) out[i] = { ...h, lung: false }; // keeps the shape count and the carve count
  }
  return out;
}
```

The "aortic root" entry is meant for a renderer without S4's ASC_AORTA; skip it here. The vessels sit 0-1.5 mm inside the fat shell at rest and in systole (test).

Note for S4: `thorax.test.ts` "bronchi run beside their arteries" builds its thorax on CASE_LIBRARY[0]'s heart, and its 12% threshold sits close to the measured value. With heart length factors 1.24 and 1.26 it read 12.6%; with the 1.22 used now, and with 1.28 and 1.30, it passes. The bronchus layout does not depend on the heart in principle, so a margin there (or a heart-independent fixture) would make the test robust to S3's per-genome hearts.

### 4.3 types/index.ts (S1)

### 4.4 engagement.ts and the rod physics (S7)

- **Ostial heights changed.** The LM ostium is now 13.5 mm and the RCA ostium 15 mm above the valve plane; they were 15 and 12 (Philip 2015). engagement.test, store.test, procedure.test and angio.test pass with the new heights. Catheter seating tuned to the old heights should be rechecked.
- **Engagement targets.** `engagementTargets(model)` lists the engageable ostia with their sinus: LM (or the separate LAD), LCx (separate, or anomalous in the right sinus), RCA (possibly in the left sinus) and conus.
- **Anomalous LCx.** `model.leftOstium2` is now also set for `anomalous-lcx-right-sinus`, inside the right sinus. engagement.ts uses it for left engagement whenever it is nearer than the LM. For this variant it should be treated as a right-sinus target: use `model.sinusOf?.lcx`. The circumflex system now has `side: "right"` throughout, so a right-sinus injection fills all of it.
- **Catheter tip motion.** Use `rootDisplacement(motion, tip.y, H)` for the tip's cardiac motion against the ostium, which is how engagement sees relative motion. Use it only on the legacy catheter path; the rod solver should move its root lumen with the same function.

## 5. Known limitations

- **Branch density.** Mid-tree transect crossings are 5/5/5. That is up from the 4/4/4 of the first wave-1 tree, and equal to the pre-wave baseline in AP and LAO cranial, but one below it in RAO cranial and short of the library's 8/7/4. Under the 7/3 law the visible branch count is bounded by the cited trunk calibres: proximal LAD from the left main (Dodge), distal LAD 1.9 ± 0.4 (Dodge), D1 and OM1 (Divia Paul), and a mid-LAD PCI reference of at least 2 mm (angioPci.test). Every setting that gave the small branches more territory (6/6/6 was reached) thinned the distal or mid LAD below those values. The synthetic raster's visibility threshold (about 0.6 mm diameter at its 0.162/mm iodine contrast) may also be higher than on real cines; that is a hypothesis for S2/S5 to test with the full render chain, not a measurement. The opacified-area proxy (7%) needs processing, background and blush before it can be compared with the 22.5% real value.
- **Extents.** RAO widths are 85 mm, inside the vessel-only band at the 5 F scale but below the 6 F medians (100-108). AP and LAO cranial widths (73, 63) are below the masks, whose catheter tips stay in. AP and LAO heights (101, 104) are above the masks, which lose the distal LAD over the diaphragm and spine. The axis fit itself prefers a lower, longer heart (elevation 25°, length 1.3-1.36). That fit widens the RAO views to about 100 mm, but on the test seeds it lets the circumflex climb up to 7 mm above the bifurcation, so the values used are a compromise.
- **LAD across-vessel motion.** On the xtrack scan lines the LAD moves about half as much as on the real lines (range 11.6 vs 29.5 px, step median 1.1 vs 2.5); the p90 step is 4.4 vs 6.7. The NCC P10-P90 along the principal axis matches (9.2 vs 8.25 mm). The real lines come from two patients. The missing motion is most likely the whole-heart rotation, which is not modelled.
- **Still fraction.** It falls from 60 to 80 bpm, then stays flat to 110 bpm (0.05 RCA, 0.19 LAD). Above 80 bpm only the isovolumic contraction remains still, and that shortens only with the square root of RR.
- **20-60 mm band on the LAD.** LAD tortuosity in this band is 4.9°, against 13.9°. Stronger long-wavelength undulation makes the LAD too long for xray.test's 200 mm limit.
- **Bifurcation ratios.** The major-bifurcation Finet median is now 0.677 (53% below 0.678). The 7/3 law alone cannot go below 0.673 at a single bifurcation; the lower values come from the Dodge-scaled left main and circumflex.
- **Not implemented**
  - Prior-CABG grafts (section 4.3 names the owners).
  - Left atrial circumflex branches.
  - Regional hypokinesis, i.e. per-territory contractility. `MotionAmplitudes.contractility` scales all motion. A per-segment factor would need the lesion and ischaemia model (S9).
  - Whole-heart rotation.
  - Respiration. It stays in sceneBuilder and thorax.
- **Bridge tissue.** The bridge compresses through `vesselRadiiAt` / `renderPath`; it has no muscle band of its own.
- **Stents.** Stents do not straighten the vessel. Straightening would move the drawn lumen away from the wire path that AngioPci computes on the unstented tree.
- **Heart axis and roll.** These are model parameters fitted to library extents and lengths. A 3D reconstruction from the multi-view library (audit proposal) would replace them.
- **Typecheck of the test.** The test imports its metrics from `scripts/` with `@ts-ignore`. Moving `metrics.ts` under `src/` would remove that, but it is outside S3's file list.
