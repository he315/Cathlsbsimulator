## What it does

Catheters, guides, the pigtail and wires are discrete Cosserat rods. Each segment carries a position and an orientation quaternion.

- **Solver.** A direct XPBD solve: one block-tridiagonal Cholesky sweep per iteration, 2 iterations per 120 Hz substep. It covers stretch, bending and torsion, with stiffness profiles along each device (shaft, transition and soft tip for catheters; core, transition and floppy tip for wires).
- **Trust region.** A single step scale for the rod keeps the multipliers consistent with the motion.
- **Lumens.** The rods move inside a union of lumens:
  - the access route (femoral/iliac or radial/brachial/subclavian, from outside the skin through the sheath);
  - the aorta, built from `thorax.aorticCourse`;
  - an analytic root with three sinus lobes and a closed-valve floor, which a pushed straight wire can cross;
  - the left ventricle;
  - the proximal coronaries, from the anatomy model or from `TreeVessel` samples.
- **Friction.** Regularised Coulomb friction with a Stribeck drop, inside the same linear solve.
- **Emergent behaviour.** Torque lag, wind-up and whip, prolapse onto the valve, deep seating, guide back-out and wire tracking all come out of the mechanics. Nothing is thresholded.
- **Classifier.** A read-only classifier reports the lumen holding the tip, depth, coaxiality, wall load, open fraction, damping risk and prolapse. It has no difficulty-dependent thresholds.
- **Nesting.** A wire or device inside the catheter is a composite: summed stiffness and stiffness-weighted rest curvature. The part beyond the tip is its own rod, clamped at the catheter tip. The device's axial load opens the catheter's curves while a hand holds it.

### Suite (`manoeuvreSuite.ts`), per configuration

- **JL4.** Engages the left main in at least 3 of 5 starts, never the RCA or the ventricle. Pushed on, it seats deep (more than 6.5 mm) and stays.
- **JR4.** Engages the RCA turning clockwise in at least 3 of 5 starts. It never engages without torque, and engages less often turning anticlockwise.
- **Over-torque.** Ends in whip, with peak torsion above 0.5 N·mm.
- **Pushing too far.** A JR4 in the left sinus prolapses in at least 2 of 3 starts.
- **Guide back-up.** The JL4 guide backs out (at least 3 mm, or out of the vessel) in every engaged start. The control stays within 1.5 mm.
- **Extra-backup.** EBU3.75 engages the left main in at least 2 of 5 starts, never the RCA or the ventricle.
- **Radial universal.** From the right radial, it reaches the root in at least 3 of 5 starts and engages in all of them.
- **Size fit.**
  - Small root: JL3.5 ≥ JL4, and JL3.5 > JL5.
  - Normal root: JL4 engages in at least 3 of 5, and JL4 > JL5.
- **Fixture only.** A coaxially engaged JR4 pushed on goes at least 0.5 mm deeper, stays in the RCA and never enters the ventricle.

## Performance

Measured on an Apple M4 with Node 26, using `PHYSICS_BENCH=1 npx vite-node src/physics/bench.ts`: median of 3 runs after warm-up, 2 iterations, trust region "rod". Load average about 5 (other agents running):

| Configuration | Catheter in root | Catheter + J wire through the arch |
|---|---|---|
| 120 Hz, scale 1.0 (default) | 89 bodies, 0.275 ms/substep, **2.20 ms/frame** | 103 bodies, 0.310 ms/substep, **2.48 ms/frame** |
| 120 Hz, scale 1.2 | 75 bodies, 1.70 ms/frame | 87 bodies, 1.98 ms/frame |
| 120 Hz, scale 1.4 | 65 bodies, 1.56 ms/frame | 75 bodies, 1.72 ms/frame |
| 240 Hz, scale 1.0 | 4.00 ms/frame | 4.64 ms/frame |

- **Budget.** 2 ms of solver time per 15 fps frame. The defaults exceed it by 10–25% on this machine. With `config: "auto"` the worker picks scale 1.4 here (predicted 1.77 ms). Under heavier load (load average 25) the same bench measured 4.3–5.2 ms at the defaults, and `autoConfig` then returns scale 1.4 flagged `overBudget`.
- **Node count.** The plan asks for about 300 nodes; this delivers 65–120 bodies per device. That deviation has **not** been agreed; it needs the integrator's or clinician's sign-off. The resolution sits where it matters: 3 mm through the preformed curve, 30 mm along the shaft.
- **GPU cost.** None.
- **Main thread.** One `postMessage` per frame carrying about 1.2 kB of node buffers plus about 0.4 kB of contact forces per device.
- **Per-frame allocation.** Only the read-out arrays (nodes, contact forces, the classifier's reading). The substep path no longer allocates.

## Known limitations

- **Shapes are model parameters, not calibrated.** The check against traced pre-contrast silhouettes (2 mm mean) has not been done. Amplatz catheters need their own technique (seated in the cusp, then withdrawn); with the Judkins procedure they never engage. The classifier also reads an Amplatz curve seated in the cusp as "prolapsed". That is a false positive of the prolapse rule for that shape.
- **Dilated root.** JL5 engages in only 0–2 of 5 starts against 3–5 for JL4, contrary to the Cureus size guidance (JL4 up to about 37.6 mm). The suite therefore does not assert the dilated-root fit.
- **The tests use closed-loop operators.** The procedures correct the tip towards the anatomy's nominal ostial height and azimuth, standing for an operator's knowledge of the usual ostial position (facts: operator procedures). Engagement itself is decided by the mechanics and read by the classifier. Open-loop scripts remain sensitive to sub-millimetre geometry changes, as real catheter work is to small differences in start position.
- **Radial universal catheter.** Engagement was reached with small clockwise corrections, not the anticlockwise rotation the Oxford notes describe. The tip entered the root near the left main azimuth.
- **0.014 in wire.** Led out of an engaged JL4 guide, the workhorse wire entered the LCx in this anatomy whatever the pre-rotation. Torque steering into the LAD was not achieved, and the wire often buckled back once it reached the closed end of the short coronary lumen. There is no test.
- **Nesting is a composite.** There is no wire–catheter friction, and a wire turns 1:1 with the catheter (no torque wind-up of the wire inside the catheter). Only one inner device at a time.
- **Lumens.** Coronary lumens are the proximal 70–80 mm, closed at their ends. The modelled RCA turns about 7 mm from the ostium, which limits deep RCA seating to about 4–6 mm. The LV is a static ellipsoid and the valve does not open in systole. There are no graft lumens (critique item 38).
- **Not modelled:** the deep-breath correction of a wire that has gone from the arm into the descending aorta. Respiration only changes subclavian tortuosity. The descent itself occurs.
- **Friction and stiffness are borrowed.** They come from neurovascular and tribology sources. Braided-shaft torsional stiffness is a model parameter. Clinician review is pending.
- **Budget.** At the defaults the solver is 10–25% over budget on this machine; `config: "auto"` handles that. Under heavy load no validated configuration fits, and the worker reports `overBudget`.
