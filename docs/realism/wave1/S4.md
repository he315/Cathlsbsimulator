## What changed

### thorax.ts
- **Thorax genome** (`ThoraxGenome`, `thoraxGenome(seed)`, `seedFrom`, `REFERENCE_GENOME`): habitus
  0.85-1.25 (right-skewed around 1.02), chest width and depth, dome height (higher in larger
  patients), lung height, rib slope and size, spine shift, scoliosis, kyphosis, epicardial fat,
  aortic unfolding, pulmonary tree seed, breast tissue. Seed 0 / no seed is the reference patient.
- **Shapes** (25-27 `AnatShape`s, body first, exactly 16 carve shapes): ventricles from heart.ts's
  LV and RV ellipsoids moved each frame by the affine fit of heart.ts's displacement field; thin
  epicardial fat envelopes the lungs are carved around; descending and ascending aorta, left atrial
  appendage, cardiophrenic fat pad, RV mass and envelope, optional breasts. ANAT indices 0-18 are
  unchanged, 19-26 are new.
- **Heart-shape merge by name (review fix).** `heartShapes?: AnatShape[]` now replaces S4's shape
  in the matching slot (`HEART_SHAPE_SLOTS`: lv myocardium -> HEART, lv epicardial fat -> HEART_FAT,
  rv myocardium -> RV, rv epicardial fat -> RV_FAT, right atrium -> RA, left atrium -> LA, left atrial
  appendage -> LAA, right ventricular outflow -> RVOT, cardiophrenic fat pad -> FAT_PAD). "aortic
  root" is skipped (`HEART_SHAPE_SKIPPED`) because ASC_AORTA covers the root; unknown names are
  ignored, never appended. Geometry and material come from the fitted primitive, the slot keeps its
  name and carve flag, so the shape count, order and carve set are unchanged. The earlier version
  duplicated seven chambers (32 shapes, doubled fat-pad attenuation, root dropped). Exported as
  `mergeHeartShapes` for tests.
- **Body frame (documented now).** `bodyFrame` changed the reference body from centre z -38 mm,
  AP half-axis 114 mm (wave 0) to centre z -46 mm, half-axis 126 mm, both scaled by habitus and
  the genome's depth. Reason (model parameter): with the rebuilt spine (vertebral bodies at z -104,
  posterior elements reaching about 50 mm behind them) the wave-0 back skin at z -152 cut through
  the spinous processes; the new back at z -172 puts the skin about 70 mm behind the vertebral
  bodies. Effect: the central AP chord is 24 mm longer, +0.48 in the soft-tissue line integral
  (MU.soft 0.020 /mm), so the AP transmission at the renderer's effective energy falls by a factor
  of 0.62 for the app as it runs today (exposure and grey levels shift; the image chain's auto
  window absorbs most of it). Downstream constants that hard-code the new body: `adrc.ts`
  `BODY_HALF_Z_MM = 126`, `BODY_CENTRE_Z_MM = -46` (and `BODY_HALF_X_MM = 176`, unchanged), and
  `dose.ts`, which imports them for its body cross-section. Change them together.
- **Vertebrae**: waisted cortical shells, oblate endplates, ring apophyses, pedicles as AP
  cortical tubes (rings end-on), per-level size, jitter, axial rotation, kyphosis and scoliosis,
  disc gaps 5-8 mm.
- **Ribs**: from the costovertebral joint through a centripetal Catmull-Rom ring; elliptic hollow
  cross-section, 1.0 mm cortex posteriorly thinning to 0.45 mm, NIST cortical excess 0.038 /mm,
  marrow 0.0032 /mm, density falling to 30 % anteriorly, tapered ends into costal cartilage,
  per-rib jitter, bucket- and pump-handle breathing.
- **Segment list (review fixes).** Hairpin joints (bend over 139 degrees, for example the TAVI
  frame's crowns) are rounded into two joints of half the bend, so every joint keeps one shared
  bisector plane at least 45 degrees from both segments (the old square-end fallback broke the
  join). Zero-length segments are dropped and closed loops are mitred across their seam.
- **Bronchi (review fix)**: offset perpendicular to the local artery tangent (posterior where
  defined, otherwise carried from the previous point) by both radii plus 0.5 mm. Bronchus points
  inside a large artery fell from 23 % to 10 % (the rest are at branch points next to a sibling
  or parent artery).
- **Hardware buffer key (review fix)**: the per-frame hardware buffer's key is now a hash of the
  packed data, so a new patient, spec, heart frame, breath or contraction always re-uploads.
- **Two drawing paths**: `segments: true` returns `Thorax.segments` for `ThoraxSegmentPass`;
  otherwise ribs, clavicles, cartilage, the larger pulmonary vessels and hardware wires are ribbon
  tubes (legacy path, below the wave-0 CPU budget). Legacy hardware tubes now carry the area-weighted
  shell and core materials (so the copper in an ECG lead is not lost).
- **CPU tracer** `traceAnat(anat, ro, rd)` and `shapeInterval` for tests and exposure control.
- Exports kept: `MU`, `Shape`, `AnatShape`, `MAX_ANAT`, `BonePrim`, `TubeSpec`, `ANAT` (keys
  added), `ANAT_COUNT`, `CARVE`, `DIAPHRAGM_DESCENT`, `ThoraxOptions` and `Thorax` (optional fields
  only), `buildThorax`, `aorticCourse`. New: `HEART_SHAPE_SLOTS`, `HEART_SHAPE_SKIPPED`,
  `mergeHeartShapes`, `hashFloats`, `MAX_CARVE_SHAPES`, genome and segment exports.

### pulmonaryTree.ts
Space colonisation per lung and tree type (2,000 attraction points per lung and type outside the
carve shapes), pipe-model radii from `LEAF_RADIUS` 0.45 mm (exponent 2.05), two tapering terminal
twigs per leaf (0.405 -> 0.25 -> 0.07 mm, two steps). Review fixes:
- The `LEAF_RADIUS` comment no longer claims the plan's 0.35-0.4 mm limit: 0.45 mm is a model
  parameter above it; the twigs cover that limit.
- **Budget**: growth lengths (step, attraction and kill radii, twig length, simplification run)
  scale with the cube root of the lung volume against the reference patient (`scale` option), so
  the tree has the same topology in every habitus, and `VESSEL_SEGMENT_BUDGET` (18,000, model
  parameter) caps the vessel segments by thinning twigs evenly. Measured (probe_budget.ts, seeds
  1-4): vessel segments 14.9k-18.0k at habitus 0.85, 18.0k at 1.0 and 1.25 (before: 18.1k-18.9k,
  21.7k-22.5k and 25.9k-26.9k); total static segments 17.2k-21.3k (before 20.4k-30.3k); first
  build 42-102 ms per patient, then cached.

### clutter.ts
- `CLUTTER_GLSL` keeps `float clutter(vec3 ro, vec3 rd, vec2 bodyIv, float lungPath)`: six
  octaves of 3D gradient noise marched in lung-local coordinates (breathes with the lungs),
  weighted by the aerated chord fraction, plus a weak soft-tissue term. `CLUTTER_AMPLITUDE` (4.5) is
  the refit constant, `CLUTTER_TILT` (0.75) the octave balance; optional uniforms set by
  `applyClutterUniforms` (fallback: patient-fixed field).
- **`TRABECULAR_GLSL` (new)**: `float trabecular(vec3 ro, vec3 rd, vec2 iv, float mu, float muI)`
  for BONE_FS. It acts on core primitives only (mu < 0 and muI == 0: every cancellous interior of
  the thorax bones is a negative core inside a cortical shell; a test checks that the gate selects
  exactly those). Anisotropic gradient noise (stretched 2.5x along the body axis, octaves 1.5, 3
  and 6 mm), 6 stratified taps, scaled like the projection of a stationary field.
  `TRABECULAR_AMPLITUDE` 3.0 (model parameter: local cancellous density SD about 54 %, so a 30 mm
  vertebral chord's cancellous signal varies about 14 %); optional uniform `uTrabAmp`.
  Self-contained (own hash and noise).
- `ThoraxSegmentPass` (`AccumulationPass`, key `THORAX_SEGMENTS_KEY`): instanced exact elliptic
  cone segments between shared mitre planes, hollow shells with an inner material (2 samples per
  pixel), sub-pixel vessels widened to 1.3 px with their integral kept.

### hardware.ts
`buildHardware(spec, ctx)`: sternal wire loops with twisted tails (0.9 mm steel, fitted, see
below), clips along the internal mammary bed, pacemaker / ICD / CRT generator, battery and
header, RA / RV / LV leads with Pt tips, rings and shock coils on heart.ts surface points, aortic
mechanical and bioprosthetic valves, annuloplasty ring, a generic self-expanding frame. New in
this revision:
- **ECG electrodes**: four limb-lead sites on the chest skin, each a hollow steel snap on a faint
  10 mm sensor disc, oriented along the skin normal.
- **ECG lead wires**: a catenary (`catenary(a, b, slack, down, n)`, exported) from each snap to a
  fixed cable anchor beyond the head end of the field, 8-14 % slack, sagging posteriorly (gravity
  for a supine patient) and laid on the skin where it would sag into the chest. Copper conductor
  (NIST, 1.427 /mm) inside PVC insulation (NIST, 0.0468 /mm in the soft channel). The snap moves
  with breathing and the anchor does not, so the wire shifts and changes its sag every breath.
- **Table edge and mattress**: 40 mm foam mattress with rounded sides (a flattened cylinder, so
  its edge fades in), then a 460 mm carbon sandwich panel (two 1.5 mm skins 40 mm apart, foam core,
  1.5 mm end walls). Foam: NIST polystyrene with an assumed 0.05 g/cm3 density (model parameter).
- `HARDWARE_PREVALENCE` comment now labels valve 1/36 a model parameter and the other two as the
  audit's measured lower bounds. `sampleHardware(seed)` uses them; `enabled: false` or
  `hardware: null` is the teaching toggle. `HardwareContext.bodyHalfX` (optional) lets the leads
  follow the patient's skin.

### Thin lines on the current chain (review item)
The thin-line count fell on the current chain in the first delivery (3,527 -> 2,410) and does so
again here (3,855 -> 3,365), while it rises by 45 % on the wave-0 chain. Investigation (separate
runs on the current chain, each pair interleaved):
- ridge.py counts pixels at least **12 grey levels** below their 15 px box mean. The S4 structures
  are physical (vessels 0.0135 /mm over lung, NIST cortex) and come out 4-8 levels deep on the
  current chain, so whether they cross the 12-level threshold is decided by the chain's fine-band
  gain; small rendering differences swing the count. Three runs of S4 against the app on the
  current chain gave -1 %, -18 % and -13 %.
- Sub-pixel vessel widening is not the cause: switching it off changed the count by +4.6 %
  (2,244 against 2,146). The lung-anchored clutter is not the cause either: without the lung
  uniforms the count was 2,113, without any clutter 2,067 (both lower). Ribs drawn as legacy ribbon
  tubes gave 1,913.
- The app's legacy ribbon ribs have aliased, slightly sharper margins than the exact
  anti-aliased shells, which is the likely source of its few hundred extra 12-level pixels.
- With more fine-band gain the order reverses: at edge enhancement 2.0 (`tune: { edge: 2 }`) S4
  gives 5,557 against 4,934 for the app (+13 %), lung band RMS 6.15 against 4.80 and power 69.9
  against 52.0. Without quantum noise (`counts: 1e6`) the S4 high-band slope is -2.21 (real -2.46),
  so the shallow slope is the chain's noise floor.
Conclusion: on the current chain's processing S4 does not increase the 12-level thin-line count,
and the target is not met on any chain; the count follows the chain's fine-band gain (S2).

   The trabecular gate relies on every negative-mu, iodine-free bone primitive being a cancellous
   core (true for all of thorax.ts's bones and for sceneBuilder's own primitives, which use mu 0 or
   positive mu). If S2 prefers an explicit flag, an instance attribute `iTrab` multiplying the call
   is equivalent. `uTrabAmp` is optional (0 = 1).
3. **caseGenerator.ts (S9)**: optional `hardware?: HardwareSpec` per case, filled with
   `sampleHardware(seedFrom(case.id))` unless a scenario specifies it (prior CABG, pacemaker,
   valve), and `case.id` passed as `patientSeed`.
4. **UI (S10/S12)**: a "hide hardware" teaching toggle passes `hardware: null`.
5. **Refits after the image chain settles** (harness `/scripts/thorax/harness.html`, own tab):
   - Clutter: `await __s4.measure({ thorax: "s4", chain: "cur" })`, compare `lungBandRms` and
     `lungPower0125` with 9.3 and 325; scale `CLUTTER_AMPLITUDE` (try `clutterGain` first). Past
     about twice the current amplitude the lung turns to mottle: the remaining gap then belongs to
     the chain's fine-band gain. Measure in one interleaved run (see above).
   - Trabecular: harness `trab: true` on the wave-0 copy, or the integrated renderer; compare spine
     tiles with library spine tiles; scale `TRABECULAR_AMPLITUDE`.
   - Sternal wires: `python3 scripts/thorax/library_scan.py wires <capture> ...` on an RAO cranial
     hardware capture against the library boxes in facts/S4.md; refit the wire radius on the
     integrated contrast (depth x FWHM), not on depth alone.

## Known limitations
- Lung texture, thin lines, local contrast, lung profile, rib margins and endplates stay 2-3
  times below the library on both chains (acceptance table); the evidence points at the chain's
  fine-band gain and noise floor for most of it, but the thorax's own rib and endplate contrast is
  also low.
- The segment pass costs about 2.6 ms of GPU, above the 1 ms budget; dropping the second shell
  sample or the twigs are the obvious savings.
- Habitus does not reach exposure until sceneBuilder passes the genome's body to the ADRC path.
- Simulated wires are sharper and deeper than real; device geometry is generic.
- About 10 % of bronchus points still lie inside a neighbouring artery at branch points.
- The clutter without the lung uniforms (today's app) is static in the patient frame.
- heart.ts's displacement field makes the fitted epicardial volume fall about 40 % at full
  contraction; the silhouette follows it (motion stream's amplitude).
