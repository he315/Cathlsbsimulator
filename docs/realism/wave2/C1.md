## 1. What the sheets showed at the start (judged against the library runs)

Read side by side with the nearest-angle real runs (`c1w0` column of the sheets):

- The lungs were two ellipsoids lateral to the heart, with 100 mm of plain soft tissue between them at heart
  level (axial slice, `probe_c1_slice.ts`). No ray met the heart against lung, so there was no heart
  silhouette; the mediastinum, spine and aorta formed one uniform dark band with straight edges.
- The hemidiaphragms sat at or below the bottom edge of the field in every view (line-integral maps): no dome
  in AP, LAO or RAO cranial, where the library shows a large crisp dark dome over the lower heart.
- The processing compressed the whole field (bands 5, 6 and residual all 0.42), so the display window spent its
  grey levels on large-scale range and the lung texture, ribs, bone and vessels were faint (lung band-pass RMS
  4.0 against 7.0-10.8).
- Spine: uniform band; vertebral bodies without texture.

## 2. What changed

### Anatomy (`thorax.ts`, `XrayRenderer.ts` ANAT_FS, `clutter.ts`)

- **Lungs reach the midline** (`ANATOMY_TUNE.lungR/lungL`): the carve shapes (heart and pericardial envelopes,
  atria, great vessels, mediastinum, domes, spine carve) now form the medial lung surfaces, so the heart
  borders, the right atrial border, the paravertebral lines and the retrocardiac lung are drawn by the organs.
- **Lung union per ray**: ANAT_FS and the CPU tracer `traceAnat` merge the lung intervals before subtracting the
  carve union, so where both lungs reach the midline the overlap is counted once (test added). The clutter
  weight uses the union chord as well.
- **Domes raised and taller** (`ANATOMY_TUNE.dome`): right apex 38 mm and left 39 mm higher than before at the
  same breathing state, vertical half size 92 -> 115 mm. At cine breath-hold the right apex is 47 mm and the
  left 32 mm above the lowest point of the ventricles of the reference patient. Cranial views now show the dark
  dome across the lower heart (AP cranial about the lower 40 % of the field, RAO cranial the lower third).
- **Spine carve** (`ANATOMY_TUNE.postMedia`): the posterior mediastinum ellipsoid now covers the vertebral
  column and paraspinal soft tissue only (x -4 +-22 mm), so lung fills the paravertebral gutters.
- **Venae cavae** (`ANAT.IVC`, new carve slot; `venaeCavae()`): the IVC joins the lower right atrium to the
  liver and the SVC ends in the upper atrium; both follow the fitted atrium after the heart-shape merge. This
  removed the gap where the right lung closed above and below the atrium. Slot count 27 (+2 optional), carve
  count 18 of 20.
- **Gastric air** smaller, deeper and anterior (it entered cranial views as a bright ellipse once the dome rose).
- **Lung clutter**: amplitude 4.5 -> 7.0, tilt 0.75 -> 0.825, and a new weight on the coarse octaves
  (`CLUTTER_COARSE` 0.3, uniform `uClutCoarse`). A texture strong enough for the library's texture rows drew
  cloud-like blotches (variant sets c1k, c1u, c1cl); real lung fields are smooth with fine markings, so the
  40 and 20 mm octaves were cut instead of raising the gain further.
- **Trabecular bone** amplitude 3.0 -> 7.5 (the uniform `uTrabAmp` is now set each frame).
- `ANATOMY_TUNE.pulmonary` makes the tree density tunable; denser trees (4000, 6000 attractors) changed the
  texture rows by less than 5 % and the default stays 2000.

## 3. Calibration method and the variants tried

All variants were captured with `sweep()` (the committed constants are restored after each sweep), judged on
side-by-side sheets against the library, then measured with `quick.py` and, for candidates, `report.py`.

| step | variants | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| processing alone | band 5-6 at 0.7 / 1.0; residual 0.25 / 0.12 | Texture rows reach the band only at residual 0.12, but anatomy stays wrong (no dome, no heart). Anatomy first. |
| domes | apex +23 / +24 mm, hy 92 / 115 | Domes appear in cranial views; taller domes project higher under cranial angulation. |
| lungs | medial reach, union, spine carve | Heart and paravertebral lines appear; AP cranial pre-contrast local contrast 18.9 -> 25.6. |
| harmonisation x clutter | residual 0.15-0.3, clutter x1-3.2 | 192/235 with clutter x2.6 (c1w2) but cloud-like lungs on the sheets; rejected for appearance. |
| coarse octaves | coarse 0.3-0.5 at gain x1-1.2 | Fine mottle instead of clouds; committed 0.3. |
| band smoothing | mid bands 1.3/1.1/0.9 | Overshoot and contrast plateau back in band (187 on c1x-b1). |
| domes +15 mm | apex -30 / -45 | +2 rows on the Z pair (AP cranial grey and motion, overshoot); committed. |
| final clutter | x1.15, x1.3 | 182 and 181 against 183: not kept. |

Scores of variants differing by 1-4 rows are within the run-to-run spread of borderline rows (for example the
same constants scored 187 and 183 with only the dome height changed, with 9 borderline flips each way).

## 5. Remaining issues (with owners where outside C1)

1. **RCA vessels at peak are too contrasty** (local contrast p99 55.4 / 55.6 against 35.5-45.4 / 32.7-46.7):
   the RCA scenario is calcified and its proximal RCA is about 4 mm (Dodge diameters), and the stronger
   harmonisation raised all fine-scale contrast by about a third. LCA views are in band. Levers outside this
   step: RCA mixing in `contrastTransport.ts` (I1), calcium shell density (S3), or the field size of RCA runs.
2. **Opacified area** in LCA RAO views stays low (0.12 against 0.25-0.34): the LCA trees are narrower than the
   library's (tree extent width 55-68 against 76-131 px, geometry rows; S3).
3. **Lung texture power and slope** (power at 0.125 cycles/px 136-148 against 143-373; slope -1.56 against
   <= -1.82) and **thin dark lines** (8,300 against 10,070-16,400): the library's texture comes from fine
   linear markings (vessels, rib cortex); ours still partly from 3D noise. Denser pulmonary trees did not move
   these rows. Next step: rib cortical margins (the harness has no rib annotations with thorax segments on:
   `rib margins` rows have no value) and bronchovascular bundles.
4. **Block-matching motion in RCA views** is too large (median 6.2 against 1.3-3.5 px in RCA LAO): with the
   heart silhouette now visible against lung, the S3 heart motion shows in the frame differences.
5. **Right atrium** still reads as a round disc in RCA RAO (fitted ellipsoid of the heart-shape stream; S3/S4).
6. **Spine**: vertebral bodies are textured now but still read as a straight tube in LAO cranial (capped
   cylinders with a 1.4 mm step waist); a smooth concave waist needs a new bone primitive (S4).
7. Lost against i1s3final (13 rows, all borderline or explained above): RCA lc_peak (2), RCA LAO motion (2),
   opacified area/darkening in LCA RAO and AP views (4), thin dark lines, frames at >= 90 % in RAO caudal,
   RAO caudal arrival / time above 50 % / rise (3). Gained 25 (see the scorecard).
8. Earlier stalled-attempt scratch remains in `datasets/tmp/captures/` (`c1an1__*`, `c1pg*__*`, `c1spr__*`,
   `c1pp-v4__*` flat frames and the `c1base`, `c1cat-*`, `c1cw*`, `c1pp-v*` folders, about 1.5 GB) and their
   caches in `datasets/tmp/realism/cap/`; not created in this run and left for the user to remove. The disk was
