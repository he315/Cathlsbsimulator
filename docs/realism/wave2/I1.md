# Wave 2, track I1: integration of the wave-1 imaging work (step 1)

Step 1 puts every wave-1 imaging piece into the live scene and into both labs: the image chain's run
bookkeeping and device-pixel resolution (S2), the clock-driven heart motion with the aortic root and
catheter moving together (S3), the per-patient thorax with the beating heart silhouette, the thorax
segment pass and lung-anchored clutter (S4), the analytic segment integrator behind its flag (S2b),
automatic dose-rate control with a dose tracker per lab (S6), and the capture harness (S0).

Result: **154 of 228 scorecard metrics inside the real band on the matched pipeline (baseline 133 of
229), 157 of 228 lossless (baseline 137 of 229).** Scorecard and per-family comparison:
[I1-scorecard-step1.md](I1-scorecard-step1.md). Constants and their basis: [../facts/I1.md](../facts/I1.md).

Files changed (all owned by I1): `src/render/xray/sceneBuilder.ts`, `src/render/xray/xray.test.ts`,
`src/ui/sim/FluoroView.tsx`, `src/ui/sections/AngioPci.tsx`, `src/main.tsx`, `package.json` (one
script), `docs/realism/facts/I1.md`, this file and the scorecard. `contrastTransport.ts`,
`injection.ts` and their tests belong to the contrast-transport steps and were not started here.

## 1. What changed

### sceneBuilder.ts

`buildXrayScene(inp)` now returns `{ scene, params, resp, contraction, motion, exposure, iso }`
(`SceneResult`). New optional `SceneInput` fields, all backward compatible:

| field | meaning | default |
|---|---|---|
| `clock` | `ClockState` of the lab's `CardiacClock`; heart motion (`heartMotion.motionAt`), beat phase and breathing come from it | `clockStateAt(t, heartRate ?? 72)` |
| `breathHold` | explicit held inspiration; overrides the clock's breathing | clock's `resp`, else the old quiet-breathing formula |
| `framesSinceStart`, `profile` | passed to `ImageParams`; `params.mode` is now always set | renderer inference |
| `catheterNodes` | rod-solver centre line (S7), drawn as given, never tethered | `aorticCourse` + `catheterRoot` |
| `legacyCatheter` | tether the catheter (and the 0.035 wire and the second catheter) to `rootDisplacement`, decaying from the tip with `catheterTether` | `true` unless `catheterNodes` is given |
| `patientSeed` | thorax genome (habitus, ribs, spine, lung trees) and clutter seed | the coronary genome's seed (the case id) |
| `hardware` | `HardwareSpec` or `null` | ECG electrodes and leads, table and mattress (`DEFAULT_HARDWARE`) |
| `thoraxSegments` | exact thorax segments for `ThoraxSegmentPass` | `true` |
| `segments` | analytic segment integrator for vessels, catheters, wires and devices | `segmentsEnabled(SEGMENTS_DEFAULT)`, `SEGMENTS_DEFAULT = false` |
| `exposure`, `adrc`, `meter`, `meteredWaterCm`, `calibration` | dose-rate control (see below) | steady state on the analytic body path, "legacy-ap" gains |

Inside the builder:

- **Motion (S3 4.1).** `motionAt(clock, H)` replaces `contraction(phase)`. Coronaries are drawn on
  `renderPath(v, m)` (dense path around lesions and stent edges, myocardial-bridge squeeze); occlusion,
  thrombus and the terminal-tip `fade` are indexed through `baseIndex`; calcium shells use the base
  samples. Samples fixed in the aortic frame (ostia, LM, proximal RCA) move with the root, and so does
  the legacy catheter: `p + rootDisplacement(m, p.y, H) x catheterTether(s from tip)`. The root plume
  also moves with the root. The `extras` `place` callback uses the same motion. Vessels with nothing
  visible (not opacified, no calcium) are skipped.
- **Thorax (S4 step 1).** `heartShapes(H, m)` moved by the breathing shift is passed to `buildThorax`
  with `motion`, `contraction`, `seed`, `segments: true` and `hardware`; S4's by-name merge keeps the
  shape and carve counts. `scene.extra` carries `thoraxSegments` and `clutter`
  (`clutterStateFromAnat`).
- **Segments (S2b step 1).** When on, every non-thorax tube goes through `tubesToSegments` with the
  tree's parent links (converted to dense-path indices), previous-frame tubes for motion blur and
  velocity, `[1, iodineGain, metalGain]` gains and topology reuse per tree. The moved tubes stay
  available as `scene.extra.segmentTubes` for CPU readers.
- **Dose-rate control (S6 step 1).** `patientPath(cArm, { body: BODY half-axes, isoAboveTableMm })`
  from the patient's own thorax, then `adrc.step(...)` (lab) or `steadyState(...)` (captures, tests),
  then `imageParamsFromAdrc(exposure, { calibration: "legacy-ap" })`. `params.counts`,
  `iodineGain`, `metalGain`, `spr`, `gridIn` and `pulseWidthMs` come from it. Counts are rescaled by
  230/200 (`COUNTS_CALIBRATION_FIELD_MM`) because S2 fitted its noise model with counts 1500 at the
  200 mm field while the controller references 230 mm; without it noise rose 9 % (facts/I1.md). The old
  `kv = 62 + (path - 220) x 0.12` formula is gone.
- `SOD` and `SID` are exported (S0 can import `SOD` instead of copying 760).
- Development only: `window.__sceneOverrides = { ... }` is merged into every `SceneInput` (A/B runs).

New exports for the labs and the renderer:

- `LabImaging`: one per lab instance. `clock` (`CardiacClock`), `adrc` (`Adrc`), `dose`
  (`DoseTracker`), `exposure` settings, `operator`, `lastExposure`, `onExpose`. `tick(nowMs)` advances
  the clock by wall time (at most 2 s per call); `frame(mode, xr)` returns the clock state, the run
  frame (restarted on a mode change or beam-off), the breath-hold (0.7 in cine, the clock is told too),
  and each new renderer meter reading once, as a water-equivalent path; `expose(result, cArm, field,
  nowMs)` books dose; `beamOff(nowMs)` ends the run and idles the tracker.
- `labImaging = { diagnostic, pci }`: the mounted labs' `LabImaging`, for a dose panel (S10:
  `labImaging.pci?.dose.totals`, `.live`, `.skinMap`, `lastExposure`).
- `installScenePasses(xr, { thorax?, segments? })` and `prepareRender(xr, scene)`.
- **Renderer bridge.** At module load sceneBuilder wraps `XrayRenderer.prototype.render` once so every
  render of a scene first runs `prepareRender`: it registers the thorax segment pass (and the segment
  and thin passes when the scene carries segments) on first use, and sets the lung clutter uniforms on
  the anatomy program. This is what makes the labs, the capture harness and development pages draw the
  same image without editing `XrayRenderer.ts` or `xrayCapture.ts`. It reads the private `pAnat`
  program and becomes redundant (still harmless) once S2 applies S4.md step 2 in the renderer itself.

### Labs

- **FluoroView** (diagnostic lab): a `LabImaging` at 60 / `HEART_PERIOD` = 71.4 bpm, the rate of the
  lab's pressure traces, so the old fixed 72 bpm image beat is gone. Each exposed frame passes the clock,
  breath-hold, run frame, controller, meter reading and metered path, `legacyCatheter: true`, and books
  dose; beam-off ends the run; a last-image-hold repaint after a resize is drawn as a settled frame with
  no controller step and no dose. The GL canvas is sized in device pixels and the renderer uses
  `fitCanvas(px)` (render at most 1024, Lanczos present, automatic 768 fallback). `window.__fluoroLab`
  in development.
- **AngioPci** (PCI lab): a `LabImaging` per scenario (new patient, new clock and dose record), passed to
  `PciFluoro`; the same per-frame wiring; device-pixel canvas with `fitCanvas`, re-fitted when the
  layout changes. `window.__pciLab()` in development. `pciDevices` is unchanged and stays in this file.
- Development hooks (`__pciDraw`, `__fluoroDraw`) draw with the pedal up; the labs skip the pedal-up
  beam-off for 500 ms after a hook draw, so a hook-driven sequence is one run (run frames count up, the
  meter loop runs) instead of a run start on every frame.
- **main.tsx** installs the capture harness under `import.meta.env.DEV`; **package.json** has
  `"realism": "bash scripts/realism/run_all.sh"`.

# Step 2: physical contrast (hand injection, ostial mixing, transport)

The canned bolus is gone. A cine run is now: the operator's press starts the cine; after a reaction delay the
hand pushes the syringe plunger; force over time becomes flow through the manifold, line and catheter lumen
(Poiseuille, wire and device shafts as an eccentric annulus, jet loss, hand limited to 50 psi); the jet meets
the phasic native coronary flow at the ostium (capture, reflux, overdrive by a sealing tip, dilution when the
push is weak); the strength is carried along the tree by a 1D advection-dispersion response in flow time
(branch splitting by S9's flow distribution, slowing only beyond a haemodynamically significant stenosis,
laminar streaming and patchy columns at low force, washout from proximal to distal with distal persistence);
the myocardial blush and the sinus reflux cloud are compartments fed by it.

Result as first submitted: **Timing 38 -> 54 of 64 scored rows; total 168 of 233 matched (step 1: 154 of 228), 171 of 233 lossless.**

## 1. What changed

### `src/simulation/injection.ts` (pure, no rendering imports)

- Constants with sources: 350 mg I/mL agent, viscosity 20.4 / 10.4 mPa s at 20 / 37 deg C and density 1406 kg/m^3
  (label), hand limit 50 psi (Zhang 2015), lumens `LUMENS` 5 Fr diagnostic 1.19 mm, 6 Fr diagnostic 1.42 mm,
  6 Fr guide 1.80 mm (manufacturer specifications), 7 Fr guide 1.98 mm (model parameter), 100 cm.
- Hydraulics: `laminarResistance(id, length, mu, obstruction)`, `jetLossCoeff`, `circuitFor(lumen)` (catheter
  plus manifold and line, tip pressure 93 mmHg), `flowForForce`, `forceForFlow`, `pressureForFlow`,
  `maxHandForce`, `deadVolumeMl`, `lumenObstruction(wire, device)`, `lumenForFrench(french, guide?)`.
- Plans: `InjectionPlan { kind: "standard" | "puff" | "forceful" | "held", side, catheter, delayS?, pushS?,
  targetFlow?, forceN?, volumeMl?, primed? }`. The operator aims for a flow (`TARGET_FLOW_ML_S` left 4.0, right
  2.5 mL/s; puff 2 mL at 3 mL/s; forceful x1.5) and the commanded force is capped at the hand's 50 psi. `forceN`
  is there for the future syringe UI.
- `InjectionTrace { dt = 5 ms, q[], c[] (strength leaving the tip, 0..1), pushStart, pushEnd, volumeMl,
  contrastMl, peakPsi, side, kind, empty }`, time 0 = the press. `planTrace(plan)` tabulates a whole planned
  injection (cached); `traceAt(trace, t)`.
- Hand dynamics: first-order force rise `HAND_RISE_S` 0.12 s and release `HAND_FALL_S` 0.9 s (library
  calibrated; **withdrawn in the revision below: now 0.1 s**); the syringe empties (10 mL); the lumen's dead volume leaves first (saline after a flush).
- `Injector` (one per lab and catheter): `start(nowS, plan)` at the press, `release(nowS)`, `advance(nowS)` in
  real time, `done()`, `finish()` (books the volume, updates what the lumen holds), `flush()`,
  `setCatheter(lumen)`, `primed`, `trace`, `elapsed(nowS)`, `stats { totalMl, injections, puffs, last }`. A press
  is a standard push (1.8 s); holding longer pushes until release or until the syringe is empty.
- `OPERATOR_DELAY_S` 0.3 s (press to push; library calibrated), `STANDARD_PUSH_S` 1.8 s, `CINE_RUN_S` 3.4 s (the
  library's median run), `CINE_TAIL_S`.
- Development only: `window.__injectionTune = { delay, rise, fall }` for calibration captures.

### `src/render/xray/contrastTransport.ts`

- `transportNet(tree, lesions)` (cached per tree and lesion set, 5-6 ms once): S9 `coronaryFlowFor` +
  `supplyFor` at rest, with each region's antegrade share (lesions' antegrade maximal capacity x CFR, capped at
  1: collateral supply does not carry the injected contrast), `vesselFlowProfile` per vessel, and the nominal
  transit time `theta` per base sample (integral of area / (gain x flow); `TRANSIT_GAIN` 1.2 calibrated on the
  Gibson TIMI frame counts); collaterals spread at 30 mm/s from their donor; resting ostial flow per side;
  terminal-theta centiles for the blush.
- `thetaOnPath(net, vessel, n, baseIndex?)` for dense render paths.
- `contrastField({ net, side, trace, t, phase, rr, flowScale?, seat? })` -> `ContrastField { S, at(theta),
  streak(theta, hash), blush, sinus, arrival, pushEnd, meanInlet, now { qInj, qCoronary, qReflux, inlet },
  injecting }`: the ostial history on a 1/60 s grid (past beat phases counted back at the current RR, S9
  `phasicFlow`), the Duhamel sum on a 96-point theta grid, the step response `stepResponse(tau, theta, m)`
  (dispersed plug + laminar streaming), blush and sinus compartments.
- `Seat { capture, seal }`, `DEFAULT_SEAT` {1, 0.3}. Development only: `window.__contrastTune`.

## 1. What changed

### High: contrast slowed far too much beyond chronic 80-95 % stenoses (`contrastTransport.ts`)

The cause was that S9's antegrade maximal capacity over resting demand (`hypFactor x (1 - collateral) x CFR`) falls to 0.47 at
80 %, 0.17 at 85 %, 0.04 at 90 % and 0.002 at 95 % (case 0 LAD, 0.38, 12 mm). S9 credits the shortfall to its
rest-reserve term (`MP_CHRONIC_REST_RESERVE`: collaterals or reduced demand), and the transport had treated all of that as
non-antegrade. Distal samples then sat on the 20 mm/s numerical floor, and the PCI lab's first case (LAD 85 %) showed a
TIMI 1 vessel.

- New `antegradeShare(region, cfr, lesions)`. For chronic non-occlusive lesions it is `a + (1 - a) x CHRONIC_RESERVE_ANTEGRADE`
  (0.57), with `a` = S9's raw share (exported as `rawAntegradeShare`). Acute (thrombotic) lesions and occlusions (>= 99 %, or a
  CTO >= 95 %) keep `a`. K was calibrated so the model's TIMI frame count beyond an 80-90 % stenosis is x1.31 on average over
  three library cases. The target is Li et al. 2021's corrected TIMI frame count, 28.56 in flow-limiting against 21.64 in
  non-flow-limiting vessels (x1.32; abstract opened through PubMed, facts/I1.md).
- Model TIMI frame count ratios (LAD 0.38, 12 mm; origin to 85 % of the LAD) at K = 0.57: 70-75 % 0.96-1.00, 80 % 1.12-1.16,
  85 % 1.24-1.40, 90 % 1.32-1.55, 95 % 1.35-1.58. The distal LAD reaches 0.83-0.86 strength inside the 3.4 s run (0.87-0.89
  without a lesion). Before the fix the reviewer measured 0.16 at 85 %.
- **Live flow (missing scope, now done).** `transportNet(tree, lesions, mods?: TransportMods)`:
  - `occlusions`: balloon occlusion with S9 `supplyFor` semantics. A region cut below `BLOCKED_SHARE` (2 %) of its resting flow
    gets no antegrade contrast, and its samples are set to `THETA_MAX_S`, outside the grid and the blush input.
  - `hyperaemia`: S9 semantics; the whole side runs faster.
  - `noReflow { vessel, param, scale }`: regional slow flow beyond a point.
  - `net.impaired` gives the share of each side's demand that a live modifier cuts or slows, and the scene scales the blush by it.
- `SceneInput.flowMods` passes these into both transport calls of the scene builder. The PCI lab sets them through the new
  `pciFlowMods(s, st, g, tree)` in `AngioPci.tsx`. An inflated balloon occludes from its proximal edge. Slow flow is no longer
  the whole side x 0.3: it is regional (`NO_REFLOW_SCALE` 0.3 beyond the target lesion).
