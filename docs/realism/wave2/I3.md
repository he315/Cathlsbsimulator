## 1. Shape calibration against real silhouettes

### Tracing method (`src/physics/calibration/`)

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `image.ts` | JPEG to PNG through macOS `sips`, cached in the system temp directory; PNG decode and encode with zlib. Node only. |
| `ridge.ts` | Dark-line detector and ridge follower. Gaussian scale σ = 2 px; dark-line strength from the Hessian eigenvalues; entry points on the edge of the exposed field; 1.5 px steps with at most 24° of turn per step and a 6 px look-ahead; re-centring across the line. The tip is where the line strength drops below 30% of the shaft's strength. Also measures outer width and valley depth. |
| `trace.ts` | Traces the first frame of every run that has C-arm angles (artefact-tagged runs are excluded). Applies an automatic pre-filter: tip inside the field, at least 80 mm traced, width 4-10 px, and the distal 70 mm a continuous dark valley. Writes QC sheets of 16 full-resolution 256 px crops (trace drawn as offset red dots, tip blue, 60 mm mark green). `TRACE=1 npx vite-node src/physics/calibration/trace.ts -- out.json qcDir [filter]` |
| `qc-decisions.json` | Every visual decision and the acceptance rule, recorded once before any fitting. A trace was accepted when it follows one catheter to its visible end over at least its distal 70 mm. It was rejected when it follows a pacing lead, sternal wire or bone/lung edge, jumps between structures, or stops short of or past the tip. |
| `select.ts` | Builds `traces.json`. Each trace is converted to mm at the library's 6F scale (0.3077 mm/px, S0 facts), flipped to y-up, tip first, at 1 mm spacing, keeping the distal 100 mm. `TRACE_SELECT=1 npx vite-node src/physics/calibration/select.ts -- raw-lca.json raw-rca.json` |
| `shapeError.ts` | Simulated side: the catheter is engaged with the scripted technique on the live anatomy with arch branches, from each standard start; each engaged end state is a candidate. | `calibrate.ts` | `CALIBRATE=eval` scores the catalogue. `CALIBRATE=fit` alternates a grid search on the root orientation with a coordinate search on `SHAPE_PARAMS.jl` / `.jr`. `CALIBRATE=holdout` fits the root orientation on half of the patients and scores the other half (`heldOutOrientation`, `splitByPatient`). |

**Funnel.** 1,303 runs had angles: 999 left and 304 right.
- Traced: 956 left and 300 right.
- Passed the automatic pre-filter: 143 left and 50 right.
- Accepted visually: **95 left and 36 right**.

**Scale check.** The per-run outer width along the accepted traces has a median of 7.0 px (left) and 6.5 px (right), consistent with the 6.5 px median behind the 6F scale.

**Caveats of the data.**
- **Unknown catheter.** The catheter used in each run is not recorded, and the study centre may use radial access. Left runs are therefore scored against the Judkins left family, each size in its own root: JL3.5 small, JL4 normal, JL5 dilated. Right runs are scored against the JR4.
- **Engagement assumed.** The first frame is assumed to show the catheter seated before injection.

### Findings

1. **The model's aortic root is oriented wrongly for the silhouettes, so the Judkins left rest shapes are not within 2 mm in the app until S3/S4 tilt the root.** In the model the root and the ascending aorta run straight up the aortic frame's y axis (`thorax.aorticCourse`: x = z = 0 from the root to y = 80 mm), and `anatomy.toWorld` applies only the case's tilt about z. In real cranial views the Judkins left arm runs horizontally with the tip turned up; in the model it runs diagonally. A joint fit over both arteries (`FITTED_ORIENTATION`) puts the root's top about 40° anterior and 10° to the patient's left. This roughly halves the left error and leaves the right error unchanged.

   **For S3/S4 (anatomy, thorax) and S5 (scene builder):** once physics nodes are drawn (S7 integration step 1), catheters in cranial views will look wrong until the root and ascending aorta are tilted like this in the anatomy. The tilt is library-measured from catheter shapes only; it confounds root orientation with any remaining shape error. It should be cross-checked against the heart axis fit (`scripts/anatomy_validation/fit_axis.ts`, azimuth 50°, elevation 30°) before it is adopted.

2. **The Judkins shape parameters are not identifiable from these traces beyond noise, so they were not changed.**
   - **In-sample gains vanished on held-out starts.** The coordinate search cut the left median from 2.27 to 1.70 mm in-sample with a 4.07 mm tip, and the right median from 0.90 to 0.78 mm. On held-out starts (55, 65, ..., 95 mm) neither held: left 1.84 mm at the wave-1 values versus 1.89 mm with the 4 mm tip; right 0.72 versus 0.88 mm.
   - **Single-parameter changes stay within noise.** With 10 starts, ±15% on the secondary radius, ±15° on the primary curve, ±4 mm on the arm or a 1.5-5 mm tip moved the left median by 0.1-0.6 mm. The direction was not consistent: a 15% smaller secondary radius gave 1.51 mm once, and 1.95 mm at a 4.7 mm radius factor. Start-to-start noise is about ±0.3 mm.
   - **The fits do not identify the catheter size.** The best-matching size per run was JL5 53, JL3.5 37, JL4 5.

3. **The metric sees curve shape but not size.** With free rotation and translation, a 20% larger copy of a real trace costs only 0.5-0.9 mm over 60 mm. A tip straightened over 25 mm costs more than 1 mm. A 2 mm mean therefore constrains the hook and arm shape, not the JL size number. This is tested.

## 2. Dilated root

**Cause.**
- **The wave-1 JL5 engaged poorly everywhere.** It engaged 0-2 of 5 starts in the small, normal and dilated roots alike, so its "loss" to the JL4 in a normal root was not a size effect.
- **Torque did not reach the tip.** In the dilated root the tip settled about 15° short of the left main azimuth and pointed tangentially. The hub turned 2.4 rad while the shaft stored 2.5 rad of wind-up and the tip barely moved (`windup` 2.5 rad, torsion 5 N·mm).
- **Operator changes did not help.** A tighter azimuth tolerance (8° and 10°), larger turns (0.2 rad), more steps (40), to-and-fro movements while turning (`protocol.jlJiggle`), and presenting the curve at the left main azimuth (`protocol.jlFacing`) all left the dilated JL5 at 0-4 of 5.

**Change.** Only the JL5 curve (`SHAPE_PARAMS.jlBySize["5"]`) changed: secondary curve 22 mm and 185° (was 27.5 mm and 180°), primary curve 95° (was 90°).
- **How it was chosen.** A scan of 27 combinations of secondary radius 22-23 mm, secondary turn 180-190° and primary turn 95-105°, then a wider 54-point pre-scan. Every candidate was run on all six cells: fixture and live with arch branches, at scales 1.0, 1.2 and 1.4. Each cell got the 5-start size suite and 12-start counts.
- **Why this candidate.** It keeps every wave-1 size assertion at every configuration. It also meets the new requirement on the live geometry at every scale.
- **Trade-off.** No candidate met everything on the frozen fixture as well: the JL5 shapes that engage the dilated root also engage the normal root more readily.

| live + arch, 1.0 | **12** | 10 | 7 | 9 | 5/4/1, 3/4/3, 3/4/5 |

**Tests.** On the three live files the suite asserts JL5 dilated ≥ JL4 normal and, as a guard against size discrimination getting worse, JL4 dilated ≤ JL5 dilated (12 starts each). The frozen-fixture file carries a test named as a known gap: JL5 dilated within 3 of 12 starts of JL4 normal (measured 10 vs 11 at its scale 1.0). The fixture should be regenerated when the root is re-oriented.

**Remaining realism gap.** The JL4 still engages the dilated root in 3-5 of 5 starts and 9-12 of 12 (live). Clinically it should struggle above about 37.6 mm (Cureus 2024, S7 facts #7). The model does not yet make an undersized Judkins left fail in a wide root.

## 3. Amplatz

**New curve** (`SHAPE_PARAMS.al`): hook radius 12 + 6·size mm (AL1 18 mm, AL2 24 mm, the sinus radius of the normal and the dilated model root), hook turn 130°, reverse tip bend 6 mm / −50°, tip 4 mm.
- **Why the wave-1 hook failed.** It had radius 7 + 3·size mm and 200° of turn, and stood 25-30 mm tall when seated in the sinus (curve bottom at y = 5-11 mm, tip at y = 32-36 mm, left main at 13.5 mm). It could never reach the ostium.
- **How the new curve was chosen.** A scan of hook radius (13-19 mm), turn (130-200°) and tip bend.

**Technique** (`manoeuvres.amplatzLeftTechnique`, `amplatzInRoot`, `amplatzEngagement`):
- The catheter is led into the root over the J wire and the wire is withdrawn.
- If the tip has dropped into the ventricle, the operator pulls back.
- If the curve has lifted out of the sinus, the operator re-seats it.
- Otherwise the operator advances while the tip is below the expected ostial level, so the tip rides up the sinus wall, and withdraws once it is at or above it, which turns the tip into the ostium.
- Azimuth corrections are as in the Judkins left technique.
- Source: Johnson Francis, "pulling the catheter will cause deeper engagement" (facts I3 #1).

**Prolapse rule** (`engagementClassifier.ts`):
- **Old rule.** Wave 1 called the catheter prolapsed whenever the distal curve reached the valve region with the tip at least 12 mm above it. A seated Amplatz hook satisfies exactly that.
- **New rule.** The fold must also exceed the device's free shape: the total turning from the tip to the shaft 10 mm above the fold, minus the free shape's total turning over the same arclength, must exceed `PROLAPSE_EXCESS_DEG` = 90°.
- **Data path.** The engine passes the free-shape tangents (`restTangentsOf`, cached per spec). `EngagementReading.foldExcessDeg` reports the excess. `classifierOptions.preformedFold = false` restores the wave-1 rule for comparisons.
- **Separation.** Measured on all six configurations: a JR4 pushed in the left sinus folds 127-185° beyond its free shape; Amplatz hooks seated in a cusp stay within −9 to 66°.

**Amplatz results** (engaged / reached the root, 5 starts):

| Cell | AL1, normal root | AL2, dilated root |
|---|---|---|
| fixture 1.0 | 5/5 | 3/5 |
| fixture 1.2 | 5/5 | 4/5 |
| fixture 1.4 | 3/3 | 0/1 |
| live + arch 1.0 | 4/5 | 3/5 |
| live + arch 1.2 | 5/5 | 4/5 |
| live + arch 1.4 | 2/3 | 1/2 |

**Suite threshold** (tightened in the second round): at scales 1.0 and 1.2 the AL1 must engage in at least 4 of 5 starts; at 1.4, in every start that reaches the root but one, and at least 2.

**Overpush** (`amplatz.test.ts`, frozen fixture, 5 starts). Pushed 4 mm per 0.5 s for 12 steps (48 mm) from the root, the current AL1 (normal root) and AL2 (dilated root) folded in some starts with excesses of 32-68° and were never called prolapsed: the largest is 22° below the 90° threshold, and the test requires at least 15° of margin. Pushed 8 mm per 0.5 s for 15 steps (120 mm), they looped on the valve in 4 of 5 starts each with excesses of 107-212° and were called prolapsed every time; the test requires at least 3 of 5 and every call above the threshold. So the preformed-shape rule does not hide a genuine Amplatz prolapse.

Behaviour across the technique runs:
- **No RCA entry and no prolapse call.** The Amplatz never entered the RCA and was never called prolapsed, seated or engaged.
- **Withdrawal holds the engagement.** A 3-4 mm withdrawal never took an engaged AL1 (at least 1 mm in) out of the vessel or more than 1 mm shallower.
- **Engagement is deep.** Engaged AL1 tips sit 4-8.7 mm in; the modelled left main is 9 mm long and closed at its end.

## 4. Per-frame budget

**Verdict: not met.** On the app's geometry, on this loaded machine, no validated configuration holds 2 ms per frame in the worst case, with or without extra load.

**Measurement.** `PHYSICS_BENCH=frames HOGS=<n> [GEOMETRY=fixture] npx vite-node src/physics/bench.ts` runs `frameBench`. It measures what the worker spends per 15 frames/s frame: 8 substeps, both read-outs and the classifier. Median, p95 and process CPU time are reported over 3 s per load, with `n` extra busy-looping processes as background load. Since the second round the bench runs on the live anatomy with the arch branches (the app's geometry) by default. Machine: Apple M4, 10 cores (4 performance, 6 efficiency), with other agents running throughout (system load average 7-14 during these runs).

Same moment as the third row, frozen fixture (`GEOMETRY=fixture`): scale 1.4 at 1.99 / 2.28 ms, against 2.15 / 2.83 ms on the live geometry. The fixture under-estimates the app by 8-25%, which is why the bench moved to the live geometry.

First round, frozen fixture, for comparison: 0 extra processes, scale 1.4 at 1.41 / 1.63 ms; 6, 1.72 / 2.12 ms; 10, 3.32 / 3.50 ms; 14, 3.46 / 3.81 ms (p95 12-14 ms). The first-round notes called 6 busy processes "met"; that was wrong, since the arch case at 2.12 ms was over the budget.

**What "auto" does now** (`bench.autoConfig`, `chooseMeasured`):
- **Measured, not extrapolated.** The wave-1 choice took one short measurement at scale 1.0 and assumed cost scales with 1/segmentScale. That over-predicted the saving of a coarser configuration (review: scale 1.2 predicted 1.94 ms, measured 2.04 ms; first round: predicted 1.99 ms, measured 2.12 ms). `autoConfig` now measures each validated configuration in turn, finest first, and stops at the first whose worst-case median is at most `AUTO_MARGIN` x budget.
- **Margin.** `AUTO_MARGIN` = 0.85 (model parameter): 15% of headroom for the difference between a short init measurement and a sustained run, and for load that changes after init. The cases above that missed (2-7% over) fall inside it.
- **The scene's own geometry.** The worker (`host.makeEngineWithConfig`) passes a factory that builds the scene it was given (model, route, course, arch branches), so the bench has the same contact work as the app. If the bench loads cannot be placed in that scene, it falls back to the live anatomy with the arch branches.
- **Honest flag.** If nothing fits the margin, the cheapest measured configuration is returned, flagged `overBudget` when its measured cost exceeds the budget itself. `ConfigInfo.predictedMs` is now that measured cost and `ConfigInfo.initMs` reports how long the bench took.
- **Init cost.** 1.0-2.0 s in the CLI runs above when all three configurations are measured (it stops earlier on a quiet machine); about 1 s in the test. It runs once, in the worker, before the first frame.
- **Per-frame flags.** `PhysicsHost` adds `costMs` (median over the last 15 frames, scaled to one 15 frames/s frame) and `overBudget` to every frame (first round, unchanged).

**Why the budget is not met.**
- **CPU time rises with load.** CPU time per frame tracks wall time as load rises (for example 2.31 versus 2.15 ms, and 2.84 versus 2.83 ms, at scale 1.4 in the third row). The worker loses no time waiting; the same work takes longer on the cores it gets, which include the efficiency cores. Under heavy load, scheduling noise also shows in p95 values of 5-17 ms.
- **The cost is the direct solve itself.** Per frame: 8 substeps x 2 iterations x one block-tridiagonal Cholesky over about 65-100 bodies, plus contacts. Profiling (`node --cpu-prof`) puts about 80% of solver time in the block solve (`solveRodIteration`, `jwj`, `fwd6`, `bwd6`, `chol6`) and about 10% in contact queries. The coarsest validated configuration removes only about 27% of the bodies.
- **Inlining gives nothing.** Writing out the hottest substitution loop of `xpbd.ts` (bit-identical results) gave no gain in an A/B microbenchmark: 0.0896 versus 0.0883 ms per iteration. V8 already inlines it. The experiment was reverted: `src/physics/xpbd.ts` is byte-identical (`cmp`) to the copy taken before I3 started; only its modification time changed.
- **One iteration per substep was tried and fails validation.** At scale 1.4 with 1 iteration it cost 1.92-1.96 ms (root) and 1.70-2.82 ms (arch), against 2.47-2.72 and 3.60-4.43 ms with 2 iterations in the same alternating runs: a saving of 25-50%. The robustness matrix (`ITER=1 ROBUST=1 ... robustness.ts -- live 1.4 120 arch`, and `fixture 1.4`) kept most outcomes, but on the live geometry two checks failed: the unloaded control guide moved 2.0 mm in one of three back-up starts (the check requires it to stay), and the AL1 engaged in 3 of 5 starts that reached the root (the suite requires all but one). It is therefore not in `VALIDATED_CONFIGS`.
- **Other real savings would change the dynamics.** 60 Hz or coarser curve segments would need the matrix re-run. Coarsening only the shaft at scale 1.4 while keeping 3 mm curve segments (first round) dropped JL4 normal-root engagement to 8/12 (fixture) and 4/12 (live). Not kept.

**GPU and main thread.** The physics uses no GPU. The main thread receives one `postMessage` per frame.

## Remaining issues and next steps

1. **Root orientation (anatomy owners).** Tilt the root and ascending aorta (about 40° anterior, 10° left; held out 40-45° anterior, 5-15° left) or reconcile them with the heart axis fit, then re-run `CALIBRATE=eval` and the robustness matrix. The fixture must be regenerated (`FIXTURES_GEN=write`). Until then the Judkins left rest shapes are 3.74 mm (median) from the real silhouettes in the app, and the "app's current state" calibration test will need updating when this changes.
2. **Rest shapes not changed.** The Judkins left and right parameters keep their wave-1 values: the search found no change that held on held-out starts. The metric is weakly sensitive to size (20% larger costs 0.5-0.9 mm) and has no scale-aware term; a tip-to-secondary-curve distance should be added before it is used to choose sizes.
3. **Size discrimination.** The JL4 engages the dilated root in 3-5 of 5 starts and 9-12 of 12 (live; 12 of 12 at scale 1.4, equal to the JL5). The JL5 discriminates normal from dilated less sharply than in wave 1 (7-9 of 12 in the normal root against 12 of 12 in the dilated root). The frozen fixture misses the dilated requirement at scales 1.0 and 1.4 by 1-3 starts in 12. A mechanism that makes an undersized curve fail in a wide root (contact of the secondary curve, torque transmission) is still owed.
4. **Amplatz push-out not reproduced.** Pushing an engaged Amplatz does not disengage it: it is jammed at the end of the 9 mm closed left main. Withdrawal resistance is also not specific to the Amplatz, since a slowly withdrawn JL4 also stays in. AL2 in the dilated root engages only 0-4 of 5, and at scale 1.4 the AL1 reaches the root in only 3 of 5 starts. Longer coronary lumens with a bifurcation would allow both behaviours.
5. **Calibration data.** The catheter and access route of each real run are unknown. A dataset with recorded catheters, or a reconstruction from paired views, would allow the size to be fitted.
6. **Budget.** Not met on this machine under load. Options, each needing the matrix re-run:
   - one iteration per substep with a compensating change (for example a stiffer guide-hold or a retuned Amplatz technique) so the two failing checks pass;
   - a reduced-order shaft (the part outside the root as a single stiff rod with friction);
   - WebAssembly SIMD for the 6x6 block solve.
   The store (S10) should react to `overBudget` (frames and `ConfigInfo`) at a quiet moment, for example by lowering the frame rate of the physics or warning.
