# Wave 2, track I2: coronary tree density, generator artefacts, LAD meander, whole-heart rotation, rest windows

This track follows up stream S3: the open issues in `docs/realism/wave1/S3.md` and the tree artefacts that S2b reported. This version includes the fixes for the adversarial review of the first I2 delivery (section 2).

Every public API is unchanged, and no consumer needs to change anything.

- **Additions.** All are optional or new exports:
  - `MotionAmplitudes.wholeRot`, with a default value;
  - `TortuosityOptions.courseDeg` and `courseHeight`;
  - `Topology.cuts` (named vessels the clearance check cut short or left out, for validation);
  - the exported constants `CLEAR_MARGIN`, `COURSE_BAND`, `REST_EXP`, `ONSET_EXP` and `T_ES_HOLD`;
  - `PRIORS.lvWidthMean`.
- **Changed defaults.** `PRIORS.heartLengthMean` 1.22 → 1.19; `DEFAULT_AMPLITUDES.trans` (2.4, -2.4, 1.92) → (1.92, -1.92, 1.536) mm, `wholeRot` (0, -4.5, 2.5) → (0, -5, 3)°, `rvSwing` 13 → 15°.
- **Index stability.** It holds as before: the geometry depends only on the genome and the ostia.
- **Order of the named left vessels.** The marginals are built before the diagonals. The LM is still the first left vessel.
- **Names.** A crowded third posterolateral branch (`PL3`, 10 of 37 trees) or a late septal that cannot run clear is now left out instead of drawn as a stub. The septals are numbered consecutively over the ones drawn.
- **Sources.** Constants and their sources are in `docs/realism/facts/I2.md`.

### 3.4 Still fraction above 80 beats/min

Still fraction (speed < 10 mm/s, the anatomy.test method, "anat-0"):

| Beats/min | RCA wave 1 | RCA review | RCA now | LAD wave 1 | LAD review | LAD now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 0.258 | 0.248 | 0.278 | 0.368 | 0.358 | 0.368 |
| 70 | 0.130 | 0.120 | 0.163 | 0.271 | 0.246 | 0.256 |
| 80 | 0.053 | 0.025 | 0.065 | 0.185 | 0.158 | 0.173 |
| 95 | 0.050 | 0.018 | 0.063 | 0.175 | 0.130 | 0.158 |
| 110 | 0.053 | 0.008 | 0.048 | 0.188 | 0.133 | 0.153 |
| 130 | 0.030 | 0.000 | 0.018 | 0.150 | 0.085 | 0.120 |

**Mechanism now:**
- The R-to-onset delay scales only weakly with RR (`ONSET_EXP` 0.5, a model parameter): 45 ms at 60, 34 ms at 110 and 31 ms at 130 beats/min. The review version had 11 ms at 130.
- The end-systolic hold (`T_ES_HOLD`, 60 ms at RR 1 s, at most 80% of isovolumic relaxation) and the end-diastolic pause scale with RR^1.8. The exponent is from the Jahnke 2006 rest periods; applying it to these two sub-windows is an extrapolation, labelled as such in the facts file.
- With the weak onset alone, the still fraction stopped falling above 80 beats/min. The hold is what keeps it falling.

The test requires s110 < s80 - 0.01 for both vessels (margins 0.007 for the RCA and 0.010 for the LAD), and that the envelope is still zero 25 ms after R at 130 beats/min.

## 5. Remaining issues

- **Crossings.**
  - The means are 6.35 / 6.04 / 6.37 against 8 / 7 / 4.
  - RAO and AP cranial are short, and the gain is mostly septal.
  - LAO cranial is about 2.4 above the library. Part of that may be measurement: the library LAO masks lose the distal LAD over the spine.
  - The binding constraint is now the distal LAD calibre (1.6 mm against Dodge 1.9 ± 0.4), not the lad-focal reference diameter.
- **LAD 20-60 mm band.** It is 12.7° against 13.85°, limited by the bounded depth range in the groove fat (0.6-8 mm, a model parameter with no source) and the low RAO visibility of a lateral meander.
- **LM+LAD projected length.** The median is 136.7 mm, above the library median (134.9) and p90 (135.7). It is coupled to the RAO width: a shorter heart that fixes the length narrows RAO cranial below the band. The real trees are wider relative to their LAD length than ours.
- **3-8 mm band.** It is 6.8° (real 12.95°), down from 8.1°, with 0.3° above the test floor.
- **RCA 20-60 mm band.** It is 16.5° against 11.6°. It comes from the RCA's course in the groove, not from its tortuosity.
- **Tortuous-RCA ratio.** It is 1.26, against a test floor of 1.25.
- **LAD across-vessel motion.** It is 57% (range) and 76% (step median) of the real lines. The RCA step median now overshoots (3.2 against 2.7 px). The rotation size is a model parameter: Negishi 2015 shows the swing exists but gives no size in the catheter-lab frame.
- **Named branches.** D1 and D2 are still 7 and 5 mm shorter than in wave 1, and PL3 is left out in 10 of 37 trees. Both follow from keeping clear of the vessels already placed.
- **Left-right lumen contacts.** 0-1 remain per tree near the apex and crux.
- **GPU per-frame cost.** Not measured (section 3.8).
