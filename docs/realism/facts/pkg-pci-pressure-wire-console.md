# pci-pressure-wire-console: model parameters

The PCI lab's pressure wire (`src/simulation/pci/pressureWire.ts`, `src/simulation/pci/actions/pressure.ts`) now checks the set-up before it equalises, as the physiology lab's pressure-wire session does (`src/simulation/pci/pwSession.ts`, lesson pm-physiology-setup, Brilakis 12.2, Oxford ch 8): the haemostatic valve closed, the introducer out, saline (not contrast) in the guide, the aortic transducer zeroed and levelled, and no air in the aortic line. The causes are the module's: air in the aortic line stops equalisation and a loose or malfunctioning transducer causes drift (pm-monitoring-transducers, src/content/manual/pci-manual/02-monitoring.ts:541; tip pci-mon-aortic-line-physiology, Brilakis 12.2). The equalisation window, the sensor position, the drift window (0.98-1.02) and the cut-offs are the existing sourced values of pressureWire.ts and physiology.ts `CUTOFF`. Hyperaemia uses pwSession's adenosine model and constants unchanged (`MP_GUIDE_HOLDUP`, `MP_DISENGAGED_DELIVERY`, `IC_ADENOSINE_MAXIMAL_UG`, `IC_ADENOSINE_LEGACY_UG`, `IV_ADENOSINE_UG_KG_MIN`, `IV_READ_AFTER_S`, the patient engine's pharmacology); the aortic line's damping uses pwSession's `MP_AIR_ML` and `MP_INTRODUCER_LEAK_MMHG` through the Transducer of patient/transducer.ts. This package adds the values below; none comes from a source.

| Parameter | File | Value | Unit | Rests on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MP_LOOSE_TRANSDUCER_DRIFT_MMHG_S` | src/simulation/pci/pressureWire.ts | 0.05 | mmHg/s on the aortic channel | Choice (3 mmHg a minute): enough for Pd/Pa at the guide tip to leave 0.98-1.02 within a two-minute study at a mean aortic pressure near 90 mmHg. The module names the cause (a loose or malfunctioning transducer, 02-monitoring.ts:541), not a rate |
| `MP_UNZEROED_TRANSDUCER_MMHG` | src/simulation/pci/pressureWire.ts | 15 | mmHg added to the aortic channel | Choice: the size of the error of an aortic transducer not zeroed and levelled. The module says the line must be zeroed before equalising (02-monitoring.ts:541); the console refuses to equalise until it is |
| `MP_JAILED_FUNCTIONAL_SHARE` | src/simulation/pci/pressureWire.ts | 0.5 | fraction of the angiographic narrowing of a pinched side-branch ostium | Choice, resting on the module text "the eye overestimates a jailed ostium" (tip pci-phys-jailed-side-branch, Brilakis 12.1, 16.2, 17.12): the pressure wire meets half the narrowing the angiogram shows (lumenFor's ostial narrowing of a side-branch compromise) |
| hyperaemic reading threshold | src/simulation/pci/actions/pressure.ts (recordPressure, samplePullback) | 0.6 | hyperaemia fraction | Earlier value (the engine's inline threshold, kept): a reading counts as hyperaemic above it |
| team IC syringe window | src/simulation/pci/actions/pressure.ts (teamIcHyperaemia) | 120 | s | Choice: an intracoronary adenosine syringe the team gave (its 'drug' telemetry) is looked back for this long; its effect is gone well before (pwSession's IC model) |
| contrast in the guide | src/simulation/pci/actions/pressure.ts (pwGuideHoldsContrast) | primed fraction > 0.5 | fraction of the lumen | Choice: the guide syringe's `primed` (1 contrast, 0 saline) above a half counts as contrast in the guide; without a syringe, contrast since the wire went in or since the last injection through the guide |
| co-registration frame step | src/simulation/pci/actions/pressure.ts (pwRouteFrame, pwStoredFrame) | 1 | mm along the route | Choice: the route of the pullback is placed on the stored run every millimetre, rest geometry (diastole, no cardiac motion) |
| stored frame shown for co-registration | src/ui/pci/inputBindings/pressureWire.ts (storedFrameIndex) | the reference frame marked on the reference monitor, else the frame on which the route is most opacified against its local background; without a route, the darkest frame on average (mean grey over every 16th pixel) | frame of the run | Choice: a run stores no cardiac phase per frame, so the trainee's marked frame (the end-diastolic frame chosen as for QCA) is used when there is one. Otherwise each frame is scored by the mean, over up to 48 route points, of the median of a 16-point ring around the point less the darkest pixel near it; the image's mean is not used because the renderer's exposure control makes a pre-contrast frame as dark on average as one at peak opacification. The route is placed on the image with the camera the live monitor rendered the run with (sceneBuilder makeCamera, treeCentre, CINE_BREATH_HOLD, the patient model's toWorld; the breathing weight at the root mirrors sceneBuilder's rootW 0.45), and the table as it is now, since a stored run does not record the table position |
| co-registration dot colours | src/ui/pci/consoles/PressureWireConsole.tsx (CoregView) | warn #E9B95A at or below the cut-off, accent #4F8EF7 above | design tokens | Each sample is compared with the cut-off of its own kind: FFR ≤ 0.80 at hyperaemia, the wave-free ratio ≤ 0.89 at rest (physiology.ts CUTOFF) |
| aortic line kept across a wire exchange | src/simulation/pci/actions/pressure.ts (armPressureWire, pwKeepLine) | the last 'pw-line' record | telemetry | Not a value: the aortic line, its transducer and the hyperaemia given belong to the patient and the team, so a wire loaded later takes them as last recorded; a loose transducer goes on drifting at `MP_LOOSE_TRANSDUCER_DRIFT_MMHG_S` meanwhile. The case's faults are armed once per case |
| `MP_COREG_WINDOW_MM` | src/ui/pci/inputBindings/pressureWire.ts | 2 | mm | Choice: the search window around each route point for the darkest pixel when scoring a frame's opacification, wide enough for the route (drawn at rest geometry) to find the vessel it runs in |
| `MP_COREG_RING_MM` | src/ui/pci/inputBindings/pressureWire.ts | 6 | mm (radius) | Choice: the ring the local background is read from, outside a coronary lumen and the search window |
| aortic channel on the patient's arterial line | src/ui/pci/inputBindings/pressureWire.ts (aorticLineOf, applyPressureAction), src/simulation/pci/actions/pressure.ts (pwWithAorticLine) | the patient engine's Transducer | not a value | When the lab has a patient engine the wire's aortic set-up is mirrored from the engine's arterial line before and after every pressure-wire action: zeroed and levelled (patient/transducer.ts `MP_LEVEL_TOL_CM`), its air, and its own offset (hydrostatic plus drift less zero), which then replaces `MP_UNZEROED_TRANSDUCER_MMHG`. The console's team steps call the line's level(), zero() and reprime(); the case's 'pwFault' faults go into the line once per patient with the engine's own fault values (`setFault('air-bubble')`, `MP_FAULT_AIR_ML`; `setFault('unzeroed')`, `MP_FAULT_DRIFT_MMHG`, only when the line has not been zeroed); a loose transducer's drift, run by the wire's set-up at `MP_LOOSE_TRANSDUCER_DRIFT_MMHG_S`, is added to the line's drift after every lab step and counted once |
