# S3 facts: heart motion and coronary morphology

Every constant in the S3 code is one of three kinds:

- **Literature.** The source was opened in this session. Most abstracts came from NCBI E-utilities (`efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=…&rettype=abstract`), because the PubMed web pages returned only a cookie wall. The raw text is saved in `scripts/anatomy_validation/refs/`.
- **Library measurement.** The value was measured from the CoronaryDominance cine library with the scripts in `scripts/anatomy_validation/`.
- **Model parameter.** The code comment names it as a model parameter. The last section lists them.

## Library measurements

All of these come from `scripts/anatomy_validation/reference.json`, which `build_reference.mjs` builds from the audit's library tool outputs in `scripts/anatomy_validation/data/`. The tools are in `library/`. They assume a 6 F catheter, 2.0 mm per catheter width in px, measured per run.

- **Motion P10-P90 along the principal axis.** NCC landmark tracks (`library/track.mjs`, 0.31 mm/px):
  - RCA in LAO: median 17.95 mm (IQR 12.43-24.13, n = 19).
  - LAD in RAO cranial: median 8.25 mm (IQR 5.89-11.81, n = 25).
  - RCA/LAD ratio 2.18.
- **Projected LM+LAD length in RAO cranial.** From `library/shape.mjs` on minimal-path traces: median 134.9 mm (p10 109.1, p90 135.7, n = 5).
- **Tangent-angle band RMS of real traces** (`library/spectrum.mjs`):

  | Trace | 3-8 mm | 8-20 mm | 20-60 mm |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | LAD, RAO cranial | 12.95° | 11.24° | 13.85° |
  | RCA, LAO | 14.4° | 11.0° | 11.6° |
  | Catheter shaft (noise floor) | 7.03° | n/a | n/a |

- **Projected LCA mask extents** (`library/realskel.mjs`, p2-p98 of the contrast-only mask):
  - RAO caudal 97.7 x 104.5 mm.
  - RAO cranial 100.2 x 94.5 mm.
  - AP cranial 106.1 x 87.3 mm.
  - LAO cranial 91.7 x 68.8 mm.

  The masks include the contrast-filled catheter and miss faint vessels. They are compared loosely; see S3.md.
- **Vessel-only LCA extents** (`library/realskel_nocath.mjs`, this fix): the same runs and frames as realskel.mjs, with the catheter chain traced from the image border along the mask skeleton and removed. Two masks: `peak` (peak frame minus pre-contrast frame, which misses faint and late-filling vessels: a lower bound) and `fill` (temporal maximum over the filling phase, widened by motion: an upper bound). Medians, width x height, mm at the 6 F scale (reference.json `extentsVesselOnly`):

  | View | peak | fill |
  |---|---|---|
  | RAO caudal | 91.1 x 82.5 | 104.4 x 106.7 |
  | RAO cranial | 100.5 x 87.7 | 108.3 x 96.9 |
  | AP cranial | 106.1 x 84.9 | 108.6 x 98.1 |
  | LAO cranial | 62.2 x 68.9 | 87.3 x 84.1 |

  The debug overlays (`out/nocath/`) show that in RAO views the catheter enters from the top and is either removed or absent from the subtraction mask, while in AP and LAO cranial its curved tip, which enters sideways, often stays in the mask, and in LAO cranial the distal LAD over the spine and diaphragm is often missing. The catheter emulation of `metrics.extent({catheter: true})` adds 7-17 mm of width in the simulator, so the catheter does not explain the earlier 60-70% widths; the simulated heart was too small and too vertical (fixed by the fit below).
- **xtrack scan-line tracks** (`docs/realism/research/xtrack.py` outputs `xtrack_cd-*.json`, 15 frames/s, 512 px; view angles from the library index; reference.json `xtrackLines`). LAD lines (AP cranial cd-01031-LCA-4, RAO caudal cd-01031-LCA-6 and cd-01048-LCA-5): range median 29.5 px, step median 2.5, step p90 6.7. RCA lines (LAO cd-01032-RCA-5): range 27.8, step 2.7, p90 8.3. `metrics.scanlineMotion` repeats the measurement on the simulator at the same view angles.
- **Heart axis and length fit** (`fit_axis.ts`): the mean axis (azimuth 50°, elevation 30°, roll -8°) and base-apex length factor were fitted to the vessel-only extent bands, the traced RAO cranial LM+LAD length (weight 2) and an anatomical constraint (LAD and LCx must not climb more than 2 mm above the LM bifurcation in their first 25 mm). The fit itself prefers a lower elevation (25°) and longer heart (1.3-1.36); the values used are the nearest that keep the climb test (on the test seeds the fitted axis lets the circumflex climb up to 7 mm), xray.test's length limits and S4's thorax test passing (length 1.22, between the fit and the original 1).
- **Motion amplitude fit** (`fit_motion.ts`): `radial` 0.12 and the translation scale were grid-searched against both the NCC P10-P90 values and the xtrack lines.
- **Values quoted from the plan's measurementTargets.** The per-run outputs of these tools were not kept:
  - Transect crossings 8/7/4.
  - LM-region excursion about 8 mm.
  - Proximal landmarks 6-25 mm.

## Model parameters (named in the code)

**heart.ts**
- `BASE`, base-plane offset from the valve.
- `LEN = 88`, heart length at length factor 1.
- LV and RV ellipsoid sizes and offsets: `LV_*`, `RV_*`.
- `BLEND = 3`.
- The default axis of `makeHeart` (azimuth 45°, elevation 42°); the genome passes its own.
- Groove scan ranges (anterior -90…100°, posterior 80…290°) and hole filling of the groove tables.
- The heart is shifted down per genome when the anterior groove would sit above the LM bifurcation.

**heartMotion.ts**
- `radial` 0.12 (fitted, `fit_motion.ts`; about twice a physiological epicardial inward motion, it also stands in for the unmodelled whole-heart rotation).
- `trans` (2.0, -2.0, 1.6) mm. The direction comes from Shechter; the size is a model value (the fit kept scale 1).
- `rvSwing` 13 mm.
- Timing: `T_ONSET` 0.045 s at RR 1 s, scaled by sqrt(RR); `T_IVR` 0.07 s, `T_RAPID` 0.16 s, the atrial window 0.15-0.01 s before R, `T_RV_LEAD` 0.03 s, `ATRIAL_SHARE` 0.25, `IVR_RETURN` 0.1.
- `rootAttenuation` (0.1 + 0.9 exp(-y/22)).
- `catheterTether` length constant 45 mm.
- The ejection shape (raised cosine of x^0.8).

**coronaryAtlas.ts**
- `female` 0.38 (equals the Manpoong cohort mix; not a population prior).
- The "multiple" split, 30% four branches.
- Bridge length SD 4 mm and depth 0.35-0.75.
- RV branches 1-3, posterolateral branches 1-3.
- Tortuosity lognormal sigma 0.35 and corkscrew threshold 1.8; tortuous-RCA index factor 1.8.
- Heart scale SD 0.06, female scale 0.95.
- Mean axis azimuth 50°, elevation 30°, roll -8° (fitted, see above); axis SDs 6°.
- `heartLengthMean` 1.22 (fitted, see above), `rvWidthMean` 0.9 (keeps the RCA ostium-to-crux course at about 125-175 mm).
- LM truncation 5.5-17 mm and diameter truncation 3.9-5.3 mm.
- LVH heart width 1.08.

**tortuosity.ts**
- `BANDS`: degrees 24/25/22 at index 1.
- `MIN_RADIUS_MM` 2.2.
- `HEIGHT_RATIO` 0.8 and `HEIGHT_RATIO_LOW` 0.3.

**coronaryGrowth.ts**
- `STEP_MAIN` 1.0 mm and `STEP_TWIG` 1.4 mm.
- `AVE` -0.94.
- `H_GROOVE` 3.2 mm and `H_EPI` 2.4 mm.
- `RV_WEIGHT` 0.4, `SEPTAL_W` 7, `S1_WEIGHT` 2, septal weights by order (1.1, 1.0, 0.9, then 0.7 falling to 0.3), `ATRIAL_W` 6.
- `TIP_TERRITORY` 50 mm² per terminal tip.
- Territory ownership offsets: `OWN_PENALTY` 0 for all orders; `LAD_OWN_PENALTY` -4.5 mm (insert radius 30 mm) and `LAD_APEX_PENALTY` -10 mm over the apical rows from `APEX_ROW` (72% of the length); no first-generation small branches from the LAD beyond ell 0.3.
- `SYSTEM_KEEP` 0.3: share of the territory-driven log deviation of the RCA and circumflex trunks from the Dodge mean that is kept.
- Series rule: D1 and OM1 at least ρ x the largest later branch of their series, ρ ~ U(0.8, 1.1) per patient.
- `LM_TURN`.
- The `GEN` growth parameters (three generations).
- Every node table for named vessels (reach, spread, origin fractions), including the diagonal reach (0.8 - 0.1k), the marginal fan (16-26°), septal lengths (S1 about 50 mm, later 38 falling to 20 mm), the RCA entry search (-100…-20°, at least 2 mm below the ostium; -48° and a 1.0 lead for the anomalous RCA), and the sinoatrial nodal courses (about 45-55 mm from the RCA, 70-85 mm from the circumflex; no opened source gives an angiographic length).
- RCA, LCx and LAD end angles.
- Tip taper over 4 mm to 0.12 mm.
- The tip iodine fade over 3 mm.

**heartShape.ts**
- `FAT_SHELL_MM` 3. The plan's range is 2-4 mm.
- Placement and size of the atria, appendage, outflow tract, cardiophrenic pad and root.

**anatomy.ts**
- `LM_OSTIUM_HEIGHT` 13.5 mm and `RCA_OSTIUM_HEIGHT` 15 mm. The order comes from Philip 2015; the values are model values.
- Anomalous ostium placements.

**scripts/anatomy_validation/metrics.ts**
- Synthetic raster: iodine 0.162 per mm of chord on a background of 150, 3x3 blur, noise SD 3.2 (as the audit's simraster.ts).
- `xtrackSpec`: landmark positions for the xtrack lines from their names (prox 0.2, mid 0.45 or 0.5 on the RCA, distal 0.75).
