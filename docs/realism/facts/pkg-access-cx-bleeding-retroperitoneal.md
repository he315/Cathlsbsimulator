# access-cx-bleeding-retroperitoneal: model parameters

The retroperitoneal bleed after femoral access: recognition, general measures, the diagnostic route, reversal, transfusion, treatment of the source and the antiplatelet plan. The sourced values (presentation within 3 hours; protamine at most 50 mg, no faster than 5 mg/min; idarucizumab 5 g in two 2.5 g doses; andexanet alfa or prothrombin complex concentrate for factor Xa inhibitors; platelets for irreversible agents and not expected to work for ticagrelor; clopidogrel 75 mg without a loading dose 24 hours after the last ticagrelor or prasugrel; cangrelor 30 µg/kg then 4 µg/kg/min; aspirin 300 mg and ticagrelor 180 mg loads) are cited in `src/simulation/access/bleeding.ts` (`RP_SOURCED`) and `src/simulation/session/scenarios/bleeding.ts` from `src/content/manual/complication-management/06-access-complications.ts` (lines 145-200), `05-patient-complications.ts` (975-1085) and `pci-manual/03-antithrombotics.ts` (709, 730, 1017). 'General measures within the first minute' is the success criterion of lab request cm-access-complications#0, used as a scoring rule. Everything below is a MODEL PARAMETER: a simulator setting that gives the right direction, not a clinical value.

Known limits, stated in the code: the iliofemoral angiogram and CT are result cards, not drawn images; a retroperitoneal bleed drawn by the closure's risk model is followed (volume, haemoglobin, grading) but cannot be stopped by the module, because the session's own bleed has no public stop: it holds no time-lapse, a definitive treatment is graded without a false seal, and the episode closes when the bleed has drained. The retroperitoneal risk terms of `femoralRisk` have no live caller yet (caseSession.ts femoralFactors, outside this package).
