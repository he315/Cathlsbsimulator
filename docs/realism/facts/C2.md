# C2 facts: diagnostic lab shell (step 1)

Every literature value used by the C2 files is listed here with the page that was opened in this step (October 2026). Abstracts were read through the NCBI E-utilities efetch service for the PMID given. Everything else in the C2 files is a model parameter, listed at the end and marked "model" (or "model parameter") in the code.

## Sources

**Q1. French catheter scale.** Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_catheter_scale
- "1 French (Fr) defined as 1/3 millimeter"; a 6 Fr catheter has an outer diameter of 2.00 mm.
- Used in `src/simulation/imaging/qca.ts` (`MM_PER_FRENCH`): the trainee's catheter calibration takes the chosen French size times 1/3 mm as the catheter's diameter.

**Q2. Accuracy and precision of QCA systems.** Keane D, Haase J, Slager CJ, et al. Comparative validation of quantitative coronary angiography systems. Circulation 1995;91(8):2174-83. doi:10.1161/01.cir.91.8.2174, PMID 7697846 (abstract opened via https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=7697846&rettype=abstract&retmode=text).
- Ten QCA systems, phantom stenoses 0.5 to 1.9 mm, four conditions including calibration "by use of the catheter as a scaling device".
- "accuracy, +0.07 to +0.31 mm; precision, +/- 0.14 to +/- 0.24 mm".
- Used as the realistic bounds in `src/simulation/imaging/qca.test.ts`: on synthetic frames with known sizes, calibrated on a 6 Fr catheter, the tool's mean signed MLD error must be within 0.31 mm and its SD within 0.24 mm.

**Q3. Edge detection overestimates small vessels.** Hausleiter J, Jost S, Nolte CW, et al. Comparative in-vitro validation of eight first- and second-generation quantitative coronary angiography systems. Coron Artery Dis 1997;8(2):83-90. doi:10.1097/00019501-199702000-00003, PMID 9211047 (abstract opened via efetch, id 9211047).
- "first-generation quantitative coronary angiography (QCA) systems overestimate small vessel sizes owing to the point-spread function of the respective X-ray imaging chain"; second-generation systems reached "an accuracy within +/-0.028 mm" below 1 mm.
- Cited in `qca.ts` for the behaviour of a plain steepest-slope edge detector (no point-spread correction), which this tool is.

**Q4. Catheter calibration is the weakest step.** Liénard J, Sureda F, Finet G. The 6sigma quality approach for quantitative arteriography performance improvement. Int J Cardiovasc Imaging 2002;18(2):77-92. doi:10.1023/a:1014658913126, PMID 12108912 (abstract opened via efetch).
- "the catheter calibration procedure was identified as the weakest function in term of sensitivity to procedure parameters, like point-spread-function, field-of-view, catheter dimensions."
- Cited in `qca.ts` and the QCA panel text ("calibration on a catheter a few pixels wide").

**Q5. QCA on flat-panel images.** Van Herck PL, Gavit L, Gorissen P, et al. Quantitative coronary arteriography on digital flat-panel system. Catheter Cardiovasc Interv 2004;63(2):192-200. doi:10.1002/ccd.20117, PMID 15390252 (abstract opened via efetch).
- Overall accuracy and precision 0.07 +/- 0.04 mm (flat-panel algorithm) against 0.19 +/- 0.06 mm. Context for the bounds in Q2; not used as a constant.

**Q6. Single-vessel QCA at bifurcations.** Ishibashi Y, Grundeken MJ, Nakatani S, et al. In vitro validation and comparison of different software packages or algorithms for coronary bifurcation analysis using calibrated phantoms. Catheter Cardiovasc Interv 2015;85(4):554-63. doi:10.1002/ccd.25618, PMID 25059819 (abstract opened via efetch).
- "Conventional single-vessel analysis underestimated the reference vessel diameter and percent diameter stenosis in the proximal main vessel while it overestimated these parameters in the distal main vessel and side branch."
- Cited in the notes to explain the browser measurement near the left main bifurcation; the tool is single-vessel.

**Q7. Calibration on the guide catheter.** Davies AG, Conway D, Reid S, et al. Assessment of coronary stent deployment using computer enhanced x-ray images. Catheter Cardiovasc Interv 2013;81(3):419-27. doi:10.1002/ccd.23366, PMID 22262472 (abstract opened via efetch).
- "For absolute measurements, the guide catheter should be used for calibration purposes." Context for the calibration step; not a constant.
