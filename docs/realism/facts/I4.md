# Facts and parameters: track I4 (stream S12, progression, entrustment and logbook)

Every literature item below was opened in this track (abstract or article page; the URL or DOI that was actually fetched is given). Everything else in the progression layer is a model parameter, listed in the second half with where it lives in the code. Nothing here is a clinical standard.

## Literature opened

### EAPCI 2020 levels of competence

- Van Belle E, et al. EAPCI Core Curriculum for Percutaneous Cardiovascular Interventions (2020). EuroIntervention 2021;17(1). doi 10.4244/EIJ-D-18-00448.
- Opened: https://eurointervention.pcronline.com/article/eapci-core-curriculum-for-percutaneous-cardiovascular-interventions-2020committee-for-education-and-training-european-association-of-percutaneous-cardiovascular-interventions-eapcia-branch-of-the-european-society-of-cardiology
- Used for: the five entrustment levels. Level I needs no procedural skill; level II is some procedural skill, usually as second operator; level III is first operator under direct supervision, managing complications; level IV is first operator without direct supervision; level V adds teaching and supervising. The UI shows short paraphrases (`LEVEL_LABEL` in `src/progress/competency.ts`).
- Also stated there and respected here: the curriculum does not set minimum procedure numbers, so every count in this track is a labelled model parameter, not an EAPCI requirement. It notes that simulators help novices and are being added to training, with their role in advanced skills still unclear; the UI says a simulator level is not a clinical entrustment decision.

### EPA description in eight sections

- ten Cate O, Taylor DR. The recommended description of an entrustable professional activity: AMEE Guide No. 140. Med Teach 2021;43(10):1106-1114. doi 10.1080/0142159X.2020.1838465. PMID 33167763.
- Opened: the PubMed abstract through NCBI E-utilities (efetch, id 33167763). The publisher page returned HTTP 403.
- Used for: the eight sections of every EPA in `EPAS` (title; specification and limitations; risks in case of failure; most relevant competency domains; knowledge, skills, attitudes and experience; information sources to assess progress and support summative entrustment; entrustment or supervision level expected at which stage; time to expiration if not practised). The section names come from the abstract; the content of each section is written for this simulator. The competency-domain labels ("Procedural skill", "Patient safety", ...) are descriptive and are not taken from a named framework.

### Distributed practice (the daily cap on blocked practice)

- Moulton CA, Dubrowski A, MacRae H, Graham B, Grober E, Reznick R. Teaching surgical skills: what kind of practice makes perfect? A randomized, controlled trial. Ann Surg 2006;244(3):400-409. doi 10.1097/01.sla.0000234808.85789.6a. PMID 16926566.
- Opened: the PubMed abstract (efetch, id 16926566).
- Used for: the decision to spread practice across days. 38 junior residents learning microvascular anastomosis, same total practice time, massed (one day) or distributed (weekly); the distributed group did better at one-month retention and on the live-animal transfer test. The cap value itself (4 counted passes per activity per day) is a model parameter; the paper gives no such number.

### Progressive difficulty (the ladders)

- Grover SC, Scaffidi MA, Khan R, et al. Progressive learning in endoscopy simulation training improves clinical performance: a blinded randomized trial. Gastrointest Endosc 2017;86(5):881-889. doi 10.1016/j.gie.2017.03.1529. PMID 28366440.
- Opened: the PubMed abstract (efetch, id 28366440).
- Used for: ordering practice by rising complexity. 37 novices; 6 hours of simulation either progressing in complexity and difficulty or in random order of difficulty; the progressive group performed better in their first two clinical colonoscopies. Six rungs and the difficulty keys are model parameters.

## Model parameters

Entrustment rules, `ENTRUST_RULES` and `MASTER_ASSESSMENT` in `src/progress/competency.ts`:

| Parameter | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Level I knowledge | 60% of linked quiz items retained (2+ correct in a row), or any logged session | Level I needs no procedural skill |
| Level II | 3 passed practice sessions on distinct cases | some procedural skill shown more than once |
| Level III | 2 exam passes, held-out cases, rung 3 or above, on at least 2 days, no critical error since the last critical-error exam (reading: no failed set since the last failed set) | entrustment needs more than one observation on more than one occasion |
| Level IV | 3 further exam passes at rung 5 or above, on at least 2 days, after the pass that completed level III | harder unseen cases |
| Exam rung bands | level III exams draw rungs 3-4; level IV exams and the final assessment draw rungs 5-6 (`EXAM_BANDS`, `schedule.ts`) | level III never uses the cases level IV needs |
| Reset on failure | critical error (diagnostic), any failed set (reading, `resetsOnFail`) | reading exams have no critical errors; without this, retrying would be free |
| Abandoned exam | a held-out case counts as attempted when it loads; leaving before the end, navigating away or closing the app logs a failed first attempt (`beginExam`, `abandonExam`, hydrate) | otherwise a seen case could be credited later as unseen |
| Level V | level IV and the final assessment | teaching cannot be observed in a simulator |
| Exam credit | first attempt on each held-out case only | a seen case is no longer unseen |
| Final assessment | one exam pass at rung 5+ in each of radial access, left engagement, right engagement, full study and reading, each taken after the exam that completed that activity's level IV (compared by time, so later the same day counts; next day when only the award date survives), all within 14 days, with no critical-error exam (or failed reading set) on a component inside the window | defines the plan's undefined "Level 10, pass Master Assessment" |
| Practice mode and role | from the level in force (`labPolicy`): guided as assistant up to level II, supervised (first operator, direct supervision) at III, independent from IV; applied to every practice session the lab did not label itself (tutorials keep guided, exams keep exam) | EAPCI wording of the levels |
| Expiry per EPA | 60 days (PCI EPAs and bail-out), 90 days (access, engagement, study, MCS, structural, bedside), 120 days (reading, lesion assessment, haemodynamics, physiology) | AMEE Guide 140 section 8 asks for a value; none is published for these simulated tasks |
| Expiry effect | level in force drops by one until the next passed session; awards are kept | lapsed skills need a refresher, not a restart |

Ladders and practice, `LADDER_PARAMS` in `src/content/mentor/catalogue.ts`:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Rungs per ladder | 6 |
| Practice cases per rung (diagnostic) | 5 |
| Exam cases (diagnostic) | generated on demand from the X-seed sequence `X:<epa>:<preset>:<i>` (presets in turn), rung from the practice rungs' key ranges (a key on a shared boundary goes to one rung by FNV-1a of the id); `exam` lists the first 3 per rung for display |
| Exam scan bound | 6000 X-seed indices per EPA per request (`examScanMax`); measured with `vite-node` on a scratch script: at least 381 held-out cases per rung within that bound for every diagnostic EPA (fewest: access-femoral rung 2); 200 rung 5-6 cases in 1.4-3.6 ms cold, a pick after 150 used in 0.02-0.14 ms warm |
| Exam choice | seeded pick among the first 5 unseen cases in the band (`EXAM_CHOICES`) |
| Candidates drawn per preset | 60 |
| Passes to clear a rung | 2 distinct passes at that rung or above |
| Counted rung passes per activity per day | 4 |
| Difficulty keys | weighted sums of `caseDifficultyAxes` and `difficulty`, one per EPA (`DIAG_SPECS`) |
| Reading hold-out | patients with FNV-1a(`exam-run:<dataset>:<patientId>`) mod 5 = 0 (about 20%; measured: 154 of 731 patients, 956 runs, in the installed library) |
| Reading exam sets (`READING_EXAM`) | items never shown in an exam of that drill (runs; patients for dominance and planning); a set may be smaller than a full practice set but not below half of it or 4 items; items last shown more than 90 days ago return only when unseen ones cannot fill a set; one set per drill per day; exam answers are not written to the practice drill results |
| Reading exam capacity (measured) | full-size sets from never-seen held-out items, in the installed library (154 held-out patients), measured in the browser with `makeSet` on a fixed random stream: artery 45+, plan 7, dominance 6, view 2, and 1 each for occlusion, collaterals, grade, estimate and locate. With the half-size minimum: view 6, plan 8, collaterals 2, locate 2; occlusion, grade and estimate stay at 1 |
| PCI variants | length 0.7-2.0 x base (8-38 mm), position base ±0.2 (0.15-0.75), severity base ±10 (70-95) unless occlusive, calcium 0/1 for the focal LAD; key = length/6 + 2 x calcium + 5 x |position - 0.4| + 1 if occlusive |

Pass thresholds, `PASS_SCORE` in `src/progress/logbook.ts`:

| Lab | Pass at | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Labs reporting only a score | 70 | model parameter |
| PCI lab | 65 | the lab's own debrief boundary for "Good result with points to improve" (`debrief()` in `src/simulation/angioPci.ts`) |
| Real-angiogram drill sets and exams | balanced accuracy 70% | model parameter; constant answering scores 50% or 1/K by construction (S13) |
| Diagnostic lab | `casePassed()` and zero critical errors | critical = dangerous injections + dissections + air emboli + VF |

Module mastery, `MASTERY_PARAMS` in `src/progress/mastery.ts`: apply credit needs rung 2 cleared on each linked EPA (capped at the rungs playable today: 1 for PCI and lab ladders); reading credit rises linearly from chance to 80% pooled balanced accuracy, after at least 10 answers per drill. Overall = mean of the parts that apply, never 100 while any part is short.

Spaced practice of skills, `reviewSkill` in `src/simulation/srs.ts`: the SM-2 rule of the quiz memory, except that a pass before the due date is counted without extending the interval. Daily-plan minutes (`MIN` in `DailyPlan.tsx`): 15 per due skill, 20 for the daily challenge, 1 per due question or read (5 to 30).

Scoring (`src/simulation/scoring.ts`): the composite weights are wave-1 model parameters, unchanged except that the hint penalty (2 points of efficiency per hint) was removed. The composite is internal analytics only.
