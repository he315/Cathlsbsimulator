# pci-equipment-tray: model parameters

The table workflow of the PCI lab: the cart (`src/simulation/pci/cart.ts`), the device actions (`src/simulation/pci/actions/devices.ts`: requestDevice from the cart, checkLabel, returnBox, the prep station, backloadDevice, the introduction, aspirateGuide, setUpWire, assistStep, checkCart, restockCart), the wire's tip damage (`src/simulation/pci/coronaryWire.ts`), the balloon's filling on fluoroscopy (`src/ui/pci/render/balloonFill.ts`) and the rule group `src/mentor/rules/pciTray.ts`. Clinical statements are taken from the module text: pm-prep-and-stent, lesson "Balloon angioplasty I" (`pm-prep-and-stent-balloon-choose`, Step 4: "Usually half contrast and half saline. A weaker mixture inflates and deflates faster but the balloon is harder to see"; negative pressure with the plunger pointing up; connect the indeflator; a balloon that cannot be seen was filled with saline or still holds air), `pm-prep-and-stent-prep-why` (the wire tip and the guide, back-bled and aspirated, before the first device and after each removal), pm-wiring lesson "Shaping the tip and loading the wire through the Y-connector" (`pm-wiring-shape-load`: rotating and automated haemostatic valves; hide the tip in the introducer; pass the introducer all the way through the valve; a tip that catches shows only "when the wire refuses to steer in the artery"; the torque device about 90 cm from the tip), and eq-balloons-stents-cart, lesson `eq-balloons-stents-cart-cart` (the cart check list before complex and CTO cases, restock at the end). The undiluted contrast is the engine's 350 mg I/mL (`IODINE_MG_PER_ML`, src/simulation/injection.ts, product label, facts/I1.md); render/core.ts already filled every balloon at 175 mg/mL ("about 1:1"). Everything else is a model parameter.

## Realism re-capture

The baseline capture set was re-captured after the change as `wp-pet` (10 October 2026, at a 1600 x 1000 viewport: `__xrayCapture.baseline("wp-pet")`, the twelve offscreen sequences in one call; then `live("pci", "wp-pet")` on lad-focal at the lab's default start and `live("diagnostic", "wp-pet")`). `python3 scripts/realism/report.py wp-pet`: 189 of 236 rows inside the real band, as the baseline. `python3 scripts/realism/compare_sets.py baseline wp-pet` lists no lost and no gained rows, so no row was re-scored. Byte for byte, all twelve offscreen sequences are identical to wp-pge (the previous package's set); dev_balloon and dev_stent differ from the baseline's frames but not from wp-pge, wp-piv and the other earlier sets, so they changed before this package. The live sequences run on the lab clock and are never byte-identical. render/balloonFill.ts draws nothing for a device the assisted path loaded (`loadDevice`, which the device scenes use: src/dev/xrayCapture.ts) and nothing for a 1:1 balloon; src/ui/pci/render/balloonFill.test.ts checks both. In the live lab (guided lad-focal, the balloon taken through the tray and inflated at 10 atm), averaging six fluoroscopy frames each, the brightest 12 x 12 px block of the difference between the 1:1 and the saline balloon was 17.9 grey levels brighter for saline, against 3.4 for two runs of the same 1:1 balloon (noise).
