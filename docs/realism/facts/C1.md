# Facts used by track C1 (wave 2, image calibration step 1)

Track C1 introduces one literature constant (the French scale). Every other constant it added or
changed is a model parameter fitted with the realism harness, listed below with its method. Physical
attenuation coefficients it relies on were sourced earlier and are only referenced here.

## Literature

| Constant | Value | Source opened | Quote |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CATHETER_OD_MM` (sceneBuilder.ts) | 5 Fr 1.67 mm, 6 Fr 2.00 mm, 7 Fr 2.33 mm | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_catheter_scale (opened 2026-10-05) | "1 French (Fr) defined as 1⁄3 millimeter"; "the outer diameter of a catheter in millimeters can be calculated by dividing the French size by 3" |

Referenced, not new:

- Steel 0.949 /mm and cortical bone excess 0.0387 /mm at 60 keV (NIST XCOM): docs/realism/facts/S4.md.
- Catheter lumen diameters (6 Fr guide 1.80 mm, 6 Fr diagnostic 1.42 mm): docs/realism/facts/I1.md.
- Coronary diameters (Dodge et al. 1992): src/render/xray/coronaryAtlas.ts and its facts.

## Model parameters fitted with the realism harness

Method for every row: capture sets from the development server with `window.__xrayCapture`
(`standard(set)` for scorecard sets, `sweep(set, variants, views)` for calibration variants, which
applies the overrides to the live tuning objects and restores them afterwards); contact sheets
`python3 scripts/realism/contact_sheet.py <sets> --side-by-side` against the nearest-angle library
runs; image rows with `python3 scripts/realism/quick.py <set>`; final numbers with
`python3 scripts/realism/report.py <set>` (matched pipeline). Anatomy geometry was also checked on axial
and coronal slices (`scripts/thorax/probe_c1_slice.ts`) and line-integral maps
(`scripts/thorax/probe_c1.ts`). Set names refer to docs/realism/wave2/C1.md.
