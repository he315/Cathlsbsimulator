# S4 facts: thorax background

Every constant in the S4 files that comes from outside the code is listed here with the source I
opened and the value quoted from it. Anything else in the code is a model parameter and says so in
a comment.

Derived values used in code (linear attenuation = mu/rho x density, per mm):

- `MU.corticalShell = 0.038` (thorax.ts): cortical bone minus soft tissue at 60 keV,
  0.3148 x 1.92 / 10 - 0.2048 x 1.06 / 10 = 0.0604 - 0.0217 = 0.0387 /mm (0.0235 /mm at 80 keV).
  60 keV is used because the renderer's existing constants sit there: `MU.soft` 0.020 /mm against
  NIST soft tissue 0.0217 /mm at 60 keV, and sceneBuilder's steel guidewire 0.95 /mm against NIST
  iron 0.949 /mm at 60 keV.
- `METAL.steel = 0.949` (hardware.ts): iron 1.205 x 7.874 / 10.
- `METAL.titanium = 0.348`: 0.7661 x 4.54 / 10.
- `METAL.platinum = 9.31`: 4.339 x 21.45 / 10.
- `METAL.copper = 1.427` (hardware.ts, ECG lead conductors): 1.593 x 8.96 / 10.
- `PLASTIC.pvc = 0.04675` (ECG lead insulation): 0.3325 x 1.406 / 10.
- `PLASTIC.foam` (mattress, table core): 0.187 x 0.05 / 10 = 0.000935 /mm. The polystyrene mass
  attenuation is NIST's; the foam density 0.05 g/cm3 is ASSUMED (model parameter, labelled in code).
- `METAL.nitinol = 0.759`: mass-weighted Ni (1.512) and Ti (0.7661) at 55/45 % by mass = 1.176
  cm2/g, times an ASSUMED density of 6.45 g/cm3. The density and the mass fractions were not taken
  from an opened source; this value is a model parameter and is labelled so in the code.

### Pulmonary vasculature

- Huang W, Yen RT, McLaurine M, Bledsoe G. Morphometry of the human pulmonary vasculature.
  J Appl Physiol 1996;81:2123-33. doi:10.1152/jappl.1996.81.5.2123 (PubMed 8941537, abstract
  opened). Quoted: "A total of 15 orders of arteries were found between the main pulmonary artery
  and the capillaries in the left lung and a total of 15 orders of veins". Used only to justify
  growing a truncated tree (the visible orders) with terminal twigs standing in for the rest; no
  number from it enters the code.

## Measured from the real library (scripts in this repository)

- Lung texture, thin dark lines, local contrast, spectral slope, lung profile and spectral
  anisotropy of 16 Philips Azurion pre-contrast frames (CoronaryDominance lossless npz, frame 2;
  the first 16 runs of docs/realism/research/ridge_npz.json):
  `python3 scripts/thorax/thorax_metrics.py real 16`, results in
  scripts/thorax/out/real_metrics.json. Definitions are those of texture.py and ridge.py plus the
  ones documented at the top of thorax_metrics.py. Medians (IQR): lung 4-32 px band RMS 9.33
  (8.64-10.87); power at 0.125 cycles/px 325 (262-371); high-band slope -2.46 (-2.70 to -2.41);
  beta 2.53 (2.38-2.99); local-contrast p99 29.7 (28.1-42.8); thin dark-line pixels 14,114
  (12,468-15,753); lung vertical profile 13.0 (11.5-15.6); anisotropy concentration 0.38
  (0.35-0.45), second orientation peak 0.19 (0.17-0.20).
- Hardware prevalence (`HARDWARE_PREVALENCE`, hardware.ts): sternotomy wires in at least 5 of 36
  and pacing hardware in at least 1 of 36 randomly sampled CoronaryDominance frames. This is the
  image-forensics audit's visual count (docs/realism/phase1-research-and-plan.json, audit
  "image-forensics", measurement "Prevalence of surgical and device hardware"), used as default
  case probabilities. The valve probability (1/36) is a model parameter.

- Sternal-wire contrast on lossless library frames (`python3 scripts/thorax/library_scan.py wires
  <run> x0 y0 x1 y1`; frames found by eye on contact sheets of all 81 readable domain-shift studies,
  `library_scan.py sheets`): local minima at least 12 levels below a 31 px profile background, the
  deepest quarter, depth and FWHM along the narrowest of four directions, 512 px.
  cd-01162-LCA-1 box (5,25)-(95,55): depth 39 (IQR 32-51), FWHM 4.26 px (3.16-5.19);
  cd-01162-LCA-1 box (15,115)-(80,170): depth 59 (53-62), FWHM 4.69 px (4.48-4.92);
  cd-01172-LCA-1 box (35,175)-(80,195): depth 67, FWHM 5.16 px (3 minima);
  cd-01168-LCA-1 box (90,118)-(120,138): depth 63, FWHM 4.17 px (3 minima).
  cd-01164-LCA-1 contains a mechanical valve and cd-01034-LCA-1 an ICD lead with shock coils (seen
  on the sheets; not measured).
- Per-patient pre-contrast median grey (`library_scan.py grey`, frame 2 of the first readable run
  of each of 81 studies, central 80 %): median 136, IQR 130-147, p10-p90 125-161, SD 14.2; within
  the largest angle group (59 runs with |primary| and |secondary| under 10 degrees in the npz
  metadata) SD 13.5. Results in scripts/thorax/out/library_grey.json.
- Per-tissue texture (grey terciles of texture.py as tissue proxies: bright = lung, mid =
  mediastinum and heart, dark = spine, diaphragm and liver), 16 Azurion frames
  (real_metrics.json rows): 4-32 px band RMS medians lung 9.33, mid 7.78, dark 6.66.

## Model parameters (no external source)

Geometry of the body, lungs, mediastinal shapes, rib ring and cross-sections, vertebral sizes,
kyphosis and scoliosis amplitudes, the genome's ranges, the space-colonisation settings,
`LEAF_RADIUS`, `PIPE_EXPONENT`, `VESSEL_SEGMENT_BUDGET`, twig count and shape, the bronchus gap,
`MU.ribMarrow`, `CLUTTER_AMPLITUDE`, `CLUTTER_TILT` and the clutter octave weights,
`TRABECULAR_AMPLITUDE` and the trabecular field's scales and stretch, the valve prevalence (1/36),
all device and hardware dimensions (the sternal wire radius 0.45 mm was raised from 0.35 mm by
comparison with the library wire measurement above, so it is a fitted model parameter), ECG
electrode sites, lead slack and anchors, conductor and insulation radii, coil filling factors, the
table-top material and dimensions, the mattress thickness, the foam density, the body frame
(centre z -46 mm, half axes 176 x 126 mm at habitus 1) and breathing displacements. Each is marked
"model parameter" in the code.
