# pci-tip-context: model parameters

The PCI lab's tip context (`src/ui/lab/pciTips.ts`) and its base event sources (`src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts`) decide when a lab tip becomes relevant. They add no dose, threshold of clinical practice or device dimension: every value below is a MODEL PARAMETER that sets when the context reports an event, chosen to match the engine's own rules or the plan's design. Clinical thresholds the context reuses keep their own sources where they are defined (the patient-event thresholds and ACT goals in `src/ui/lab/patientEvents.ts`, the radiation dose levels in `src/simulation/dose.ts`).

| Parameter | File | Value | Unit | Rests on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `POOR_SUPPORT_RATIO` | `src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts` | 0.8 | ratio of required force to the guide's back-up force | The plan's design value for poor-guide-support (docs/curriculum/lab-build-plan.json, pci-tip-context: requiredN/backupN > 0.8). Not from a source |
| `STALL_ZONE_MM` | `src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts` | 2 | mm proximal to the lesion start | A balloon or stent whose distal end stopped within this distance of the lesion, or inside it, counts as stalled at the lesion. The criterion of the device catalogue's tip test (`src/simulation/pci/equipment.tips.test.ts`) |
| `STALL_ADVANCE_MM` | `src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts` | 0.05 | mm | A push that moved the device less than this did not advance it. The same test's criterion |
| `DEVICE_WIRE_MARGIN_MM` | `src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts` | 6 | mm proximal to the wire tip | The engine's own distal wire limit for a balloon or stent (`src/simulation/pci/actions/devices.ts` advanceDevice: maxDistal = st.tipMm - 6, and its 'move' record's beyondWire flag), mirrored because the engine does not export it. A device held there stopped at the wire, not at the lesion, so it raises neither balloon-uncrossable nor stent-uncrossable. Keep the two values equal |
| `STENT_UNDEREXPANDED_RATIO` | `src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts` | 0.85 | diameter at the lesion / expanded stent diameter | The engine's own waist criterion (`src/simulation/pci/actions/inflation.ts`: achieved diameter below 0.85 of the chart diameter) applied to a placed stent |
| `OCCLUSION_SEVERITY_PCT` | `src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts` | 95 | % diameter stenosis | The engine's own occlusion rule (`src/simulation/pci/state.ts` lesionNow). total-occlusion is raised at or above it, and never in a CTO case |
| `COMPROMISE_EVENTS` | `src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts` | hypotension, vf, vt, tamponade | events | The patient events taken as haemodynamic compromise, which put the tips at phase 'complication' before the learner names a complication. A choice of events, not from a source |
| `RECOGNISED_NOT_COMPLICATION` | `src/ui/lab/pciEvents/base.ts` | wire-subintimal | events | Events behind the recognition gate that, once named, do not put the tips at phase 'complication' (a recognised subintimal wire is still wiring). A choice of events, not from a source |
| Transient hold | `src/ui/lab/eventLog.ts` (`TRANSIENT_HOLD_S`, reused) | 30 | s of procedure time | Already a MODEL PARAMETER of found-lab-session-plumbing; the context holds a transient event (guide back-out, poor guide support) for it after its last occurrence |

Rules of the context that are design choices rather than numbers:

- Cue events before recognition: slow-flow, st-elevation, chest-pain and hypotension (`CUE_EVENTS`). A complication's own name (dissection, perforation, no-reflow, thrombus, air, side-branch-compromise) is raised only once `snap.recognised` names it (LAB_BUILD_PLAN section 2: complications are named only after the learner's assessment).
- The waist on a balloon is read from the engine's outcome message of the last inflation ("A waist persists ...", "under-expanded at the lesion"), which is what the operator is told; the engine records no structured waist flag.
- wire-subintimal is behind the recognition gate as well: the engine tells the operator only that the tip moves unusually freely (`src/simulation/pci/actions/wire.ts`), so the context names it only once `snap.recognised` does. The deprecated headless form never names it.
- A wire counts as across the lesion when its tip is beyond the lesion in the target vessel, or in a branch beyond it once the engine has recorded the crossing (`st.wire.crossed`): at the end of a vessel the engine carries the wire on into a terminal branch (`src/simulation/pci/coronaryWire.ts`). Such a wire is not in a side branch.
- A guide back-out makes a device at the lesion uncrossable only until a later push advances the device by `STALL_ADVANCE_MM` or more.
- A device stopped at the distal wire limit (too little wire beyond the lesion) is not stalled at the lesion, and neither is one whose last push stopped there and has not been pushed since the wire was advanced. Only a push judged by the delivery physics (advanceDevice) can show a stall; the compatibility moveDevice stops a device only at the wire limit.
