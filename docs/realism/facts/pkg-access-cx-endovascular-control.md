# access-cx-endovascular-control: model parameters

Endovascular control of an iliofemoral bleed from the opposite groin, and the cold leg treated by its cause. The sourced rules and numbers are cited in `src/simulation/access/endovascular.ts` (`ENDO_SOURCED` and the header) from `src/content/manual/complication-management/06-access-complications.ts`: a low-pressure balloon before anything definitive (line 187, and 972 for the crossover balloon); a covered stent for a large-vessel hole that prolonged inflation does not seal (192); coils after excluding retrograde filling for a small branch, microspheres, glue or thrombin for very distal holes, a prolonged balloon or a covered stent over the branch's mouth as alternatives (197); surgery for failures or the femoral bifurcation (202); the 7 Fr long crossover sheath of the TAVI series (930); balloon-expandable covered stents for precise placement in the iliac artery, self-expanding ones preferred in the common femoral artery with a landing zone above the bifurcation, never across it, sheaths of 6 or 7 Fr (one balloon-expandable type) or 7 Fr (the others), and a smallest diameter of 5 mm (987); the leg checked afterwards (997); the six Ps against the cholesterol triad (442-444); dissection from forced wires and injections with the sheath tip against the wall, embolisation from a clotted sheath (461, 463); treatment by cause (489-505). The common femoral diameters 9.8 mm (men) and 8.2 mm (women) are `SOURCED_SITE` in `src/simulation/access/site.ts`. Everything below is a MODEL PARAMETER: a simulator setting that gives the right direction, not a clinical value. Devices are abstract classes with generic names, not products.
