# S7 facts: catheters and wires as elastic rods

1. **Sourced.** Listed here with the URL or DOI that was opened and the quoted value.
2. **Definition.** For example, 1 inch = 25.4 mm.
3. **Model parameter.** Named as such in a code comment, with no claim to a source.

Nothing below comes from memory. Where a source was opened only at abstract level, the entry says so.

## Definitions

- 0.035 in = 0.889 mm and 0.014 in = 0.356 mm (1 in = 25.4 mm).
- Area moment of a solid round wire: I = πd⁴/64.

## Model parameters (all named in code)

Each item below names the file it lives in.

**Numerics** (`rod.ts`, `engine.ts`)
- Lateral drag 2e-6 N·s/mm² and spin drag 2e-7 N·mm·s/mm. These are drag "masses" that make each substep an implicit quasi-static step. The physical blood drag is smaller, and motion is quasi-static either way.
- Trust region of 1.5 mm and 0.3 rad per iteration, mode "rod": one step scale for the whole rod, applied to the displacement and to the multiplier increments alike (`xpbd.ts`). Mode "joint" (per-body scale, per-joint multiplier scale) is kept as an option.
- 120 Hz substeps with 2 direct iterations.
- Validated solver configurations (`bench.VALIDATED_CONFIGS`): 120 Hz at segment scale 1.0, 1.2 and 1.4. **Measured**, not chosen: the robustness matrix (`robustness.ts`) gave the same pass/fail result for every required manoeuvre at these settings on the frozen and the live geometry (table in `docs/realism/wave1/S7.md`).
- Segment lengths: 3 mm through the preformed curve and 30 mm along the shaft. Arm routes use 12 mm shaft segments, with end-point contacts.

**Hands** (`engine.ts`)
- Hand speed 60 mm/s and 2π rad/s.
- Grip limit 4 N and 8 N·mm.
- A hand pushing an inner device keeps it "advancing" for 0.3 s after its last push (hand state only; it no longer gates the compression load).
- A released hub turns back at 1.5 rad/s per N·mm and slides back at 20 mm/s per N.
- Snap threshold: tip speed 150 mm/s.
- Femoral length allowance of 100 mm outside the patient. The femoral route built on `thorax.aorticCourse` measures 0.81 m from skin to root.

**Devices** (`catheterShapes.ts`)
- Preformed curve region at 25% of shaft EI for diagnostic catheters and 35% for guides.
- Soft tip at 8% of shaft EI over 2.5 mm.
- Poisson ratio 0.4 for polymer and 0.3 for metal.
- Braided-shaft torsion GJ = 4 × shaft EI.
- EA = 450 N for a 6 F catheter.
- J tip radius 3 mm, floppy length 30 mm, transition 40 mm.
- J wire Ecore 13 GPa, which gives 4 N·cm², inside source #3's range.
- Exchange-length wire 2.6 m.
- Device-shaft Ecore 6 GPa.
- All arc lengths and angles for JR, AL, AR, MPA, extra-backup, radial universal, radial backup and pigtail.
- JL secondary-curve radius 5.5·n mm.
- JR primary curve 60° and secondary 30°.

**Friction** (`contact.ts`)
- Creep speed vε = 0.4 mm/s.
- Stribeck speed 3 mm/s.
- Valve-crossing force 0.08 N within 1.2 × the orifice radius.
- Sheath friction scale 0.5; external line 0.

**Lumens** (`lumen.ts`)
- Root wall: 0.88 R at the annulus and the sinotubular junction, bulging to R at the cusp centres.
- Lobe half-width 1.1 rad.
- Floor: coaptation 8 mm above the cusp nadir, rising by 10 mm towards the commissures.
- Orifice radius 0.32 R.
- LV cavity ellipsoid with semi-axes 40 mm and 22 mm along the heart long axis from `heart.ts`.
- Sheath lumen 1.15 mm; external line 1.2 mm.
- Abdominal aorta radius 8.5 mm; arch radius 14.5 mm.
- Brachial 1.6–2.2 mm, axillary 2.8 mm, subclavian 3.6 mm, innominate 5.5 mm.
- Femoral and radial control points.
- Subclavian tortuosity 4 mm, or 9 mm with the variant, scaled by (1 − 0.7·resp).
- Ostial funnel at 1.9 × the vessel radius.
- Fallback coronary radii (LM 2.2, LAD 1.75, LCx 1.6, RCA 1.9, conus 0.75 mm), consistent with the ranges in the `coronaryTree.ts` header.

**Coupling** (`engine.ts`)
- Inner-device compression applied to the catheter curves whenever a hand holds the device, equal to the device's axial load at the catheter tip, capped at 3 N (below the 4 N grip limit). A device with nothing beyond the exit carries no load (no wire–catheter friction).
- Wire lateral load on the catheter tip, capped at 0.4 N and 4 N·mm, ramped in over 4–14 mm of free wire, with relaxation 0.3.

**Feedback channels** (`contact.ts`, `engine.ts`)
- Stick-slip: a contact is "stuck" once its slip speed is at most vε (0.4 mm/s) and "sliding" once it reaches the Stribeck speed (3 mm/s); a stuck contact under more than 0.01 N that starts sliding counts as one stick-slip event.
- Wedged: the classifier's damping risk above 0.6 (`WEDGED_RISK`).

**Classifier** (`engagementClassifier.ts`)
- "Deep" means more than 6.5 mm beyond the ostium: the 4 mm funnel plus one catheter diameter.
- "Coaxial" means the tip is within 45° of the vessel axis.
- The manoeuvre tests count a coronary as engaged when the tip is inside its lumen beyond the ostium (depth > 0), with the same rule on both sides.

**Legacy mappings** (`legacyBridge.ts`)
- cram = wall load × 20.
- forwardPressure = axial load × 3.

**Operator procedures in the manoeuvre tests** (`manoeuvres.ts`). These are test-protocol parameters, not device or anatomy constants. They stand for what an operator does while watching the tip on fluoroscopy without contrast:
- Judkins left and radial universal: every 0.5 s a correction of at most 2 mm towards the expected ostial level (the anatomy's left main height minus 1 mm), a 1 mm advance when at the level, and a 0.1 rad turn towards the expected azimuth of the left main when the tip is more than 15° away; at most 24 corrections (30 for the radial catheter).
- Judkins right: hub turned 0.15 rad per 0.5 s for at most 22 steps (3.3 rad), tip held at the expected ostial level (the anatomy's RCA height minus 2 mm) with corrections of 1 mm per mm of height error, at most 1.5 mm per step.
- Over-torque: the catheter is loaded with an 8 mm push before turning at 1.2 rad/s; a tip jump of more than four times the hub step (13.6°) within 50 ms with peak torsion above 0.5 N·mm counts as whip.
- Guide back-up: device pushed 8 mm per 0.5 s for 12 s; "backed out" is a retreat of at least 3 mm along the vessel or leaving it; the control must stay within 1.5 mm.
- Deep seating: the first start that engages at least 2 mm into the vessel; pushes of 2 mm (left) or 1 mm (right) per 0.5 s for 4 s.
- Exchange: the operator pulls the wire back when its tip advances more than 4 mm while the catheter is withdrawn over it.
