# pci-guide-engagement: model parameters

The guide (`src/simulation/pci/guide.ts`) gained engagement by hand from the aortic root on the analytic engagement model of the diagnostic lab (`computeEngagement` in `src/simulation/engagement.ts`, its fixed thresholds), a seeded settle on engagement, a wedge of the ostium that the patient engine's transducer reads as the trace (`src/simulation/pci/patientBridge.ts` `tipFromGuide`), turning off the ostium, stored twist and kinking, the guide's aim for the wire (`src/simulation/pci/coronaryWire.ts`), the guide exchange over the wire (`src/simulation/pci/actions/guide.ts`), the rod physics through the PhysicsDevices interface with its start-up bench (`src/ui/pci/useGuidePhysics.ts`), the engagement event sources (`src/ui/lab/pciEvents/guide.ts`) and the intravascular part of a deep-seated guide on the image (`src/ui/pci/render/guide.ts`). Device dimensions come from the catalogue (`src/simulation/pci/equipment.ts`: the guides, their back-up ordering, deep-seat ease, French sizes, outer and inner diameters, lengths), which is already sourced. Every value below that does not cite the module text is a MODEL PARAMETER, chosen so the behaviour matches what the module text describes; none is a clinical measurement.

## MODEL PARAMETERS

| Parameter | File | Value | Unit | Rests on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MP_GUIDE_TORQUE_FIDELITY` | `guide.ts` | 0.75 | share of hub rotation | Lab request pm-wiring#1 asks for less torque fidelity than a diagnostic catheter (thinner wall, softer tip). Applied once, in `twistGuide`: this share of the hub rotation reaches the tip and the rest is stored as twist. Out of the ostium the analytic model then turns that share with the stand-in diagnostic catheter's own torque response (`guideCatheterType` leaves the response unchanged), so the guide's effective response is 0.75 of the diagnostic catheter's; engaged, the share turns the tip off its coaxial position. In rod mode the physics turns the tip and the share only sets the stored twist |
| Stand-in shapes for the analytic model | `guide.ts` `guideCatheterType` | EBU3.75 = EBU3.5 with reach 19.5 and seat height 14.5; EBU4.5 = EBU4.0 with reach 22.5 and seat height 15.5; AR1 = AR; AR2 and hockey stick = AR with reach 19; Williams right = JR4 | model units | The catalogue of diagnostic shapes (src/data/catheters.ts) lacks these curves; the reach lies between or beyond the neighbouring sizes |
| Physics shapes | `guide.ts` `guideShapeId` | JL4 = JL4G, JR4 = JR4G, EBU4.5 = EBU4.0, AR1 and AR2 and hockey stick = AR, Williams right = JR4G | ids | The solver's shapes (src/physics/catheterShapes.ts); nearest where it has none of its own |
| `MP_ROOT_START_ABOVE_MM` | `guide.ts` | 8 | mm | A guide brought up to the root starts this far above the top of the sinuses (ascending aorta), so the hands bring it down to engage |
| `MP_OSTIUM_DEFAULT_MM` | `guide.ts` | 4 | mm | Ostial lumen when the geometry has not given one (the assisted engagement without the geometry); otherwise the reference lumen at the start of the left main or right coronary in the case's tree |
| `MP_SEAL_BASE`, `MP_SEAL_PER_MM`, `MP_SEAL_NONCOAX` | `guide.ts` | 0.55, 0.25, 0.3 | share; share per mm; share | The wedge: (outer diameter / ostial lumen) x seal. Chosen so a coaxial 6 Fr guide at a normal left main ostium leaves most of it open, one pushed about 5-6 mm in fills it and damps, and an aorto-ostial stenosis makes damping likely at a modest depth |
| `MP_DAMPING_GAP` | `guide.ts` | 0.16 | open fraction | Calibration against the patient engine's transducer (src/simulation/patient/transducer.ts): on the engine an ostium or coronary tip input gave a damped or ventricularised trace at a gap of 0.15 or less and an aortic one from 0.2, left and right, and none with side holes |
| `MP_DIVE_MM` | `guide.ts` | 3 | mm | The seeded extra depth on engaging is up to this times the curve's deep-seat ease (equipment.ts `deepSeatEase`) times French / 6: an Amplatz dives further than a Judkins, a larger guide further than a smaller one |
| `MP_SETTLE_COAX_SPREAD` | `guide.ts` | 0.3 | coaxiality | The seeded loss of coaxiality on engaging |
| `MP_TURN_OFF_RAD` | `guide.ts` | 0.6 | rad at the tip | Rotation of an engaged guide that turns it off the ostium |
| `MP_COAX_PER_RAD` | `guide.ts` | 1 | coaxiality per rad | Coaxiality lost per radian turned off the coaxial position (non-coaxial from about 0.35 rad, off the ostium at 0.6 rad) |
| `MP_TWIST_NOT_TRANSMITTED_RAD`, `MP_KINK_TWIST_RAD` | `guide.ts` | pi/2, 3 pi | rad of stored twist | Stored twist past which the hub feels wound up and the tip lags (torque-not-transmitted), and past which the shaft kinks (about six turns in one direction without moving the guide) |
| `MP_TWIST_RELEASE_PER_MM` | `guide.ts` | 0.15 | rad per mm | Axial movement lets stored twist reach the tip |
| `MP_AIM_DEPTH_MM`, `MP_WIDE_LM_DEG` | `guide.ts` | 0.5 mm, 80 degrees | mm; degrees | A large left curve seated at least this deep in a left main whose LAD and LCx take-offs are at least this far apart points the wire at the circumflex (lad-focal's are 91 degrees apart) |
| `LARGE_LEFT_CURVES` | `guide.ts` | EBU3.75, EBU4.0, EBU4.5, JL5, AL1, AL2 | ids | The curves counted as large for the circumflex aim |
| `MP_SHALLOW_JR_MM` | `guide.ts` | 0.5 | mm | A Judkins right shallower than this at a separate conus ostium points the wire at the conus |
| `MP_WIRE_DRAG_ON_PULL` | `guide.ts` | 0.6 | share of the pull | An unpinned wire comes back with a guide pulled out of the ostium |
| `MP_AIM_FOLLOW` | `coronaryWire.ts` | 0.8 | probability | Chance at each first exit from the guide that the wire follows the guide's aim instead of its tip's own direction (EBU3.75 in lad-focal: 38 of 50 seeds into the LCx with the tip turned to the LAD) |
| `MP_BLOCKED_EXIT_MM` | `coronaryWire.ts` | 1 | mm | How far the wire gets beyond the tip of a guide sitting against the wall |
| `PCI_ROD_GUIDE_WAIT_S` | `useGuidePhysics.ts` | 30 | s | Lab time to wait for the physics' first reading (the solver's own start-up bench takes about 9 s in a worker: physicsDevices.ts `MEASURED_COST`) |
| `DRAW_DEEP_MM` | `render/guide.ts` | 0.3 | mm | Depth from which the part of the guide inside the coronary is drawn. The drawn part takes the guide's outer diameter for its French size from the catalogue (`GUIDE_LUMEN`, Brilakis Table 30.4, 5 to 8 Fr, no clamp), a 0.2 mm wall, and the depth rounded to 0.1 mm (the samples are cached per geometry, side, depth and French size) |
| `AORTO_OSTIAL_MM` | `pciEvents/guide.ts` | 5 | mm | The target counts as aorto-ostial within this distance of the left main or right coronary origin (the rule of actions/imaging.ts `aortoOstial`) |

## Realism re-capture

The baseline capture set was re-captured after the change as `wp-pge` (9 October 2026, at a 1600 x 1000 viewport: the twelve offscreen sequences of `baselineSpecs("wp-pge")` captured a few at a time through `__xrayCapture.capture`, because the development server reloaded the page during one call and the interrupted sequence was captured again; then `live("pci", "wp-pge")` on lad-focal and `live("diagnostic", "wp-pge")`). `python3 scripts/realism/report.py wp-pge`: 189 of 236 rows inside the real band, as the baseline. `python3 scripts/realism/compare_sets.py baseline wp-pge` lists no lost and no gained rows, so no row was re-scored. Byte for byte, the eight scorecard sequences and dev_guide and dev_wire are identical to the baseline's frames; dev_balloon and dev_stent differ from the baseline but are identical to wp-piv, wp-pwm and wp-dav2, captured by earlier packages, so they changed before this package. render/guide.ts draws only for a guide seated deeper than 0.3 mm, which no capture scene has.
