# S0 facts: where every number in the realism harness comes from

S0 uses one literature value, the Finet ratio, as an unasserted reference (see Literature). Every threshold the harness applies is either measured from the project's own library by a committed script, re-aggregated from the phase-1 anatomy audit's result files of the same library, copied from existing project code or from the plan's measurement targets, or a method or model parameter named as such below.

## Measured from the real library

All pass bands live in `datasets/library/realism_targets.json`, written by `scripts/realism/targets.py`. Each target records:

- the metric, pipeline (`lossless` or `matched`), profile, artery and view family;
- the median, IQR, p10 to p90, minimum to maximum and n;
- the band and its rule;
- the method, the script and the run ids it came from.

To reproduce the file, run `bash scripts/realism/run_all.sh --library-only`. It takes about 15 minutes cold on 8 workers and a few seconds from cache.

The run selections replay the phase-1 audit (`scripts/realism/selections.py`):

- Fixed lists are copied from `docs/realism/research/run_all.sh`, `timing_runs.txt` and `timing2_npz.json`.
- Seeded samples replay the audit's own `random` calls in the same index order:
  - seed 11: `m5_contrast.py` and `m2_dynamics.py`;
  - seed 3: `field.py`;
  - first 25 runs per family in index order: `m7_pct.py`.

### Re-aggregated from the anatomy audit's result files (`scripts/realism/geometry.py`)

The anatomy-motion audit measured real CoronaryDominance runs with node scripts and left its per-run results in `docs/realism/research/anatomy/`. S0 does not re-run those scripts (they need hand-placed waypoints); it reads their outputs and applies Python ports of the audit's own measures:

- `traces/*.json`: Dijkstra centre lines of 9 runs (5 LAD in RAO cranial, 4 RCA in LAO) on peak-opacification frames, from `trace.mjs`. `tangent_bands()` ports `spectrum.mjs` (tortuosity band RMS) and `shape_metrics()` ports the length part of `shape.mjs`. The 3 catheter-shaft traces give the noise floor: 3-8 mm band 6.6-7.5 deg, 8-20 mm 1.7-3.9 deg (`geometryNotes` in the targets file). The 3-8 mm real band (9.0-18.6 deg) therefore sits close to the tracing floor.
- `motion_trace2.json`: NCC landmark P10-P90 displacement (px) of the same 9 runs, from `track.mjs`.
- `tracks/*.json`: the per-frame landmark tracks. Speeds use frame-to-frame steps between consecutive kept frames only; this rule reproduces the audit's 48 / 17 mm/s and 104 / 38 mm/s within 3%. (Pooling all steps gives 39 / 15 mm/s, and median of landmark medians including steps over dropped frames gives 52 / 18; neither matches.)
- `realskel.json`: p2-p98 vessel-mask extents of 6-14 runs per view, from `realskel.mjs`.
- Scale: the audit's 6F calibration, median catheter shaft width 6.5 px over 1,072 of 1,303 runs (`cathwidth.mjs`), 2.0 mm / 6.5 px = 0.3077 mm/px. Using each run's own catheter width instead does not reproduce the audit's extents (for example 94 x 107 mm instead of 101 x 98 in RAO caudal), so the fixed scale is used, as the audit did.

### Contrast arrival (`metrics.arrival_features`)

The definition is the audit's `summarise.py` timing(): baseline = median of frames 0-1, arrival = first frame at or above baseline + 30% of the rise, time above 50%, last three frames still at or above 50%, arrival at frame 0. The audit applied it to `run_analysis.py`'s vessel fraction (Pillow, 1,314 runs); the harness applies it to its own opacified-pixel signal, already cached in every sequence result, so library and captures share one signal. On the 49-run timing selection the harness gives LCA median 0.733 s and RCA 0.667 s, against the audit's 0.73-0.8 s and 0.67 s; no run has arrival at frame 0 (audit: 0 of 1,314).

### mm per px of the captures

At the lab's 20 cm field and 768 px canvas, the capture metadata gives 0.1867 mm per canvas px at the iso-centre, which is 0.280 mm per 512-px analysis px, 9% finer than the library's 6F scale of 0.3077 mm/px. The 6F guide catheter measures 7.0 px outer FWHM in the baseline scorecard, inside the real 7.0-9.75 px. A vitest check keeps the scale within 15% of the library's. Geometry rows use mm at the iso-centre for the simulator and the 6F scale for the library, as the audit did.

### Measured by S0 and not in the audit

Four catheter-shaft scan lines are listed in `scripts/realism/jobs/library_jobs.json` with `added_by: "S0"`. They were picked on gridded frame-24 views of these runs:

- cd-01031-LCA-6
- cd-01031-LCA-8 (two lines)
- cd-01048-LCA-5

They are tracked with the audit's `xtrack.py` method, with no frames lost and a periodic course of about 13 frames. Their ranges are 24.9, 10.4, 19.6 and 30.8 px. They raise the catheter-motion target from n = 1 to n = 5.

### JPEG effect

The domain-shift runs were measured both as lossless npz frames and as the library's own JPEG q72 frames (`jpegEffect` in the targets file). The table gives the median ratio of matched to lossless:

| Quantity | Matched / lossless | Runs |
|---|---|---|
| Noise SD | 1.004 | 13 |
| Noise lag-1 autocorrelation | 1.206 | 13 |
| Dark/bright ratio | 1.004 | 13 |
| Lung band-pass RMS | 1.000 | 49 |
| Thin-line count | 1.021 | 49 |
| Pre-contrast local contrast | 1.001 | 49 |

JPEG q72 therefore leaves the noise amplitude unchanged but raises its lag-1 correlation by about a fifth. For this reason, captures are compared with targets from the same pipeline.

## Method and model parameters (named, not measured)

These parameters define S0's own methods. They are stated in code comments and here.

- **Automatic border halo** (`metrics.edge_auto`), parameters chosen by S0:
  - 9 x 9 box smoothing;
  - +-8 px large-scale gradient on an 8-px grid;
  - top 3% of gradient magnitude, at least 20 grey levels;
  - direction coherence: cosine above 0.8 and magnitude above half at +-10 px along the border;
  - +-40 px profiles.
  The same code runs on the library and on captures, so the parameters cancel in the comparison.
- **Band rule:**
  - n of at least 10: p10 to p90 of the per-run values;
  - n from 3 to 9: the observed range;
  - n below 3: indicative only, kept out of pass and fail counts.
- **Still frame for captures** (texture, ridges, halo): frame 2 when the bolus has not started by then, otherwise frame 0. The lab's bolus starts with the first cine frame.
- **Pre-contrast frame for local contrast:** the largest k <= 2 whose coronary iodine is at most 1% of the run's peak (model parameter); none, and the row is n/a, when every candidate holds more. In the current lab frame 0 already holds about 2%.
- **Capture annotations:** at most 32 points per vessel. Twig and collateral vessels are left out. Rib margins are recorded on frames 0-4. Full-resolution centre lines of LM, LAD, LCx and RCA are recorded on frames 0 and n/2 for the geometry rows.
- **Simulator tree extent:** projected centre lines of vessels whose radius is at least 0.4 mm (the audit's `simext.ts` cut-off), sampled every canvas px, p2-p98, at the frame of peak iodine.
- **Simulator landmarks:** 10/30/50/70/90% of the ostium-to-distal arc length at frame 0 (LM + LAD, or RCA), followed on the projected geometry (not image tracking), as the audit's `simmotion.ts`.
- **Per-view targets:** used when the view family has at least 3 real runs, otherwise the artery-level target (`report.target_for`).
- **Drill clips:**
  - capped at 90 frames (`MAX_FRAMES` in `make_clip.py`, a length cap for the drill; the library itself truncates at 120);
  - 3 runs per view family, seed 5;
  - real runs restricted to those not tagged artefact, occlusion or uncertain.
- **Drill injection time:** 0.40 s. This is the measured library median first-appearance time (`timing.f10_s`, cd-ds lossless).
- **Drill heart rate:** each pair's simulated heart rate is the real run's own rate from `m2_dynamics.py`. When that run has no accepted period, the library median of 64.6 beats/min (`heart_rate.bpm`, cd-ds matched, 52 runs) is used. This applied to 15 of the 30 pairs.
- **Wilson score interval:** z = 1.959964, the 97.5th centile of the standard normal distribution. This is a mathematical constant, not a sourced figure.

## Literature

- Finet G, Gilard M, Perrenot B, et al. Fractal geometry of arterial coronary bifurcations: a quantitative coronary angiography and intravascular ultrasound analysis. EuroIntervention 2008. doi:10.4244/eijv3i4a87. Opened via the Europe PMC REST record of PMID 19736093 (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:19736093%20AND%20SRC:MED&resultType=core&format=json) on 2026-10-05; the PubMed page itself returned only a cookie notice. Quoted from the abstract: "Dm=0.678 (Dd1+Dd2)", from 173 bifurcations in 47 patients with normal coronary arteries. The abstract gives no spread for the ratio, so `realism.test.ts` prints the simulator's LM Finet ratio beside 0.678 and does not assert a band.
