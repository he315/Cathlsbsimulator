# I2 facts: coronary tree density, artefacts, LAD meander, whole-heart rotation, rest windows

Every constant that track I2 added or changed is listed here as literature (source opened in this session), a library measurement or fit (script and method named), or a model parameter. The S3 facts file (`docs/realism/facts/S3.md`) still covers everything I2 did not touch.

## Literature

Abstracts were fetched through NCBI E-utilities (`https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=…&rettype=abstract&retmode=text`). The raw text is saved in `scripts/anatomy_validation/refs/i2_rest_period_abstracts.txt` and `refs/i2_motion_abstracts.txt`.

## Library fits and measurements

- **Whole-heart rotation, translation and RV swing (refit after the review).** `DEFAULT_AMPLITUDES.wholeRot` = (0, -5, 3)° (aortic frame, about the base-plane centre; 5.8° at end-systole), `trans` = (1.92, -1.92, 1.536) mm (0.8 of the first I2 value) and `rvSwing` = 15° (was 13). Method: `scripts/anatomy_validation/fit_motion.ts 8` on 8 right-dominant genomes at 65 beats/min and 15 frames/s.
  - Grid: translation scale 0.8 / 0.9 / 1.0, rotation y -3.5 / -4.5 / -5.5°, rotation z 2 / 3°, RV swing 15 / 17 / 19 / 21° (72 settings). Objective: summed squared log ratios to the real medians of the NCC P10-P90 (LAD RAO cranial 8.25 mm, RCA LAO 17.95 mm) and the RCA/LAD ratio (2.18), each counted twice, plus the LAD and the RCA xtrack line medians (range, step median, step p90) with equal weight. Penalties: LAD landmark step median above 4.3 px or p90 above 9.7 px (anatomy.test bounds), ratio below 1.85.
  - The first I2 fit used the LAD terms only, which let the RCA/LAD ratio fall to 1.80. The best grid settings used an RV swing of 17°, but that moved the acute marginal more than 3 mm inside the fitted silhouette in systole (anatomy.test, heartShape), so 15° was kept and the rotation adjusted.
  - Result (`fit_motion.ts 8 "1,0.12,0,-5,3;1,0.12,0,0,0"`, rotation on / off): NCC P10-P90 LAD 8.8 / 9.6 mm, RCA 17.8 / 18.4 mm, ratio 2.02 / 1.93; LAD xtrack 18.6 / 2.1 / 6.5 px against 12.4 / 1.1 / 5.4 (real 29.5 / 2.5 / 6.7); RCA xtrack 16.5 / 3.1 / 10.4 px (real 27.8 / 2.7 / 8.3).
- **LAD course meander (redone after the review).** The first I2 version let the LAD's depth meander run up to 15.8 mm into the myocardium: the height clamp had no working floor (`clampH(h, lo, hi)` clamped against the new height). The floor is now the smaller of `hMin` (0.6 mm) and the height before the undulation, for every vessel.
  - With the floor alone, the LAD 20-60 mm band fell from 14.2° to 10.6° (`sim_metrics.ts 24 tortuosity`).
  - `LAD_COURSE_DEG` 7, `LAD_COURSE_HEIGHT` 4, `LAD_FAT_LIFT` 2 mm, `LAD_SOFT` 0.5 mm and `LIFT_RAMP` 8-30 mm were chosen from this scan (lateral degrees, height ratio, lift, knee: 20-60 mm band median, LM+LAD projected median), all with the floor:
    - 5 / 4 / 2 / 0.8: 10.9°, 137.1 mm; 5 / 5 / 2 / 0.8: 11.8°; 8 / 3 / 2.8 / 0.8: 12.0°, 138.2 mm;
    - 8 / 3 / 2.8 / 0.4: 12.3°; 10 / 3 / 2.8 / 0.5: 12.7°, 139.5 mm; 10 / 4 / 3 / 0.5: 12.9°, 140.7 mm;
    - 7 / 4 / 2.8 / 0.5: 12.5°, 139.1 mm; 7 / 4 / 2 / 0.5: 12.8°, 138.8 mm (chosen); 7 / 4 / 2.8 / 0.5 with an 9 mm ceiling: 13.4°, 140.6 mm (not adopted: a higher ceiling has no source).
  - The band saturates near 13° because the depth range is bounded (0.6-8 mm above the myocardium) and the lateral meander is only 0.35-0.56 visible in RAO cranial.
- **Heart length.** `PRIORS.heartLengthMean` 1.22 → 1.19 (coronaryAtlas.ts). `sim_metrics.ts 24 length,extent`: LM+LAD projected median 138.8 mm at 1.22, 136.7 at 1.19, 134.7 at 1.17 (library median 134.9). 1.17 put the RAO cranial width below the extents band in anatomy.test (82.9 against 83.4 mm), so 1.19 was kept. A larger heart scale with a shorter axis (scale 1.03, length 1.14) gave 134.9 mm but narrower RAO widths (83.0 mm), and a heart roll of -18° / 0° / +8° changed the crossings by at most 0.2 per view, so neither was adopted.
- **Transect crossings.** These are library values from the plan (8 / 7 / 4) compared with `metrics.rasterMetrics`. The new `peaks` output names the vessel behind each counted crossing (`crossing_report.ts`).
- **Artefact counts.** `metrics.treeArtefacts` (definitions in its doc comment, after the S2b review) reproduces S2b's wave-1 examples on the old tree, for example RCA-twig0 inside the RCA at sample 123 (origin 105) and D1[60] ~ OM1[44].

## Model parameters (named in the code)

**heartMotion.ts**
- `wholeRot`, `trans` scale and `rvSwing` (fitted, above). The translation direction is still from Shechter 2006.
- `T_ONSET` 0.045 s at RR 1 s, scaled by RR^`ONSET_EXP` with `ONSET_EXP` = 0.5: 45 ms at 60 beats/min, 34 ms at 110, 31 ms at 130. Model parameter; the weak dependence follows Van der Hoeven 1977 (qualitative). Before the review it was scaled by RR^1.8, which gave 11 ms at 130 beats/min.
- `T_ES_HOLD` 0.06 s at RR 1 s (capped at 80% of isovolumic relaxation), scaled by RR^`REST_EXP`: the annulus holds its end-systolic position at the start of isovolumic relaxation. Model parameter; an end-systolic rest period exists (Shechter 2005, 76-80 ms below 1 mm). Scan with anatomy.test's still-fraction method (RCA / LAD at 80 and 110 beats/min): onset exponent 0.5 and hold 0.04 s gave 0.065 / 0.043 and 0.173 / 0.158; 0.06 s gave 0.065 / 0.048 and 0.173 / 0.153 (chosen, both fall by at least 0.01); 0.08 s gave 0.065 / 0.058 and 0.173 / 0.155. With the onset exponent alone (no hold) the still fraction stopped falling above 80 beats/min (RCA 0.040 / 0.050 / 0.050 at 80 / 95 / 110 with exponent 0.3).
- `T_ATRIAL_END` 0.01 s at RR 1 s, scaled by RR^`REST_EXP` (unchanged).

**coronaryGrowth.ts**
- `CLEAR_MARGIN` 0.3 mm: extra clearance between two lumens.
- `TWIG_TORT_ROOM` 0.4 mm: room for the twig tortuosity added after growth.
- Provisional radii before the flow law (`provisionalRadius`), in mm, tapering linearly along the vessel:
  - main trunks 1.95 to 0.8 (x LM/4.5);
  - named branches 1.25 to 0.45;
  - septals 0.8 to 0.3;
  - nodal 0.5;
  - small branches 0.6 / 0.45 / 0.35 to 0.18 by generation.
- Course variants tried when a named branch would touch a placed vessel:
  - diagonals: spread x 1 / 0.8 / 0.65 / 0.5 / 0.38, then spread 0.5 with reach 0.75;
  - marginals: fan x 1 / 0.5 / 1.5, then reach 0.8;
  - RV branches: fan x 1 / 0.4 / 1.6;
  - acute marginal: x 1 / -0.5 / 2;
  - posterolateral branches: x 1 / 0.4 / 1.6 / 0;
  - left PLV: x 1 / 0.3 / 1.8.
- Minimum kept lengths if cut: 25-30 samples for named branches, 10 for the others.
- Build order: the marginals, the left posterior branches and the circumflex SA-nodal artery are now placed before the diagonals and septals, so the diagonals adapt to them.
- `LAD_COURSE_DEG` 7, `LAD_COURSE_HEIGHT` 4, `LAD_H_MAX` 8 mm, `LAD_FAT_LIFT` 2 mm, `LAD_SOFT` 0.5 mm, `LIFT_RAMP` 8-30 mm (scan above). The LAD's mid course sits 2 mm higher in the groove fat and its height saturates smoothly between 0.6 and 8 mm above the myocardium, so it never dips into the myocardium outside a flagged bridge. `RCA_LOW_OCTAVE` 0.5 is unchanged from wave 1. Lowering it to 0.3 did not change the RCA's 20-60 mm band, which comes from its course.
- `RCA_LOOP_LIFT` 1.5 mm and `RCA_LOOP_H_MAX` 8 mm: a looping (tortuous) RCA gets the same soft range, so its loops no longer dip into the myocardium. Lifts of 2.5 and 3 mm and a 10 mm ceiling gave the same tortuous/plain ratio (1.26; anatomy.test needs 1.25; wave 1 1.29).
- A protected deep run (myocardial bridge) gets no lift and may rise at most 0.5 mm above its pre-undulation depth. Before this, the course undulation lifted one genome bridge (CASE_LIBRARY large-root-0) above -1.5 mm, so it was no longer flagged and lost its systolic compression. Now 7 of 7 genome bridges are flagged (`named_report.ts`).
- Course variants added after the review (model parameters):
  - PDA: a first node straight down from the origin (drop 0.025-0.04 in u), a phi offset of -4 or +4° within the groove, or a 1.5 mm lift in the groove fat. Before, the PDA had one course and was cut to 29 mm in 8 of 124 trees.
  - Near-origin rule in `firstConflict`: within 2 x the host radius + 3 mm of its origin, a new vessel may run beside its host for as many samples as it has run (an acute or recurrent take-off), and the first 2 x radius + 3 mm of a sibling leaving the same host within the host window are no obstacle.
  - Origin alternatives 4-10 mm along the parent for the diagonals (0, +5, -4, +10 mm), marginals (0, +5, -4) and posterolateral branches (0, -4, +4, -8).
  - `DIAG_REACH_KEEP` 0.4: a narrower diagonal reaches further towards the apex, so it keeps its length; a wider (x 1.25) spread was added.
  - Ramus: take-off turned towards the LAD (share 0.5-0.75 of the start tangent) and the groove entry moved round by -6 to -16° or +6°.
  - Posterolateral: a backward fan (x -0.6). `PL_MIN_MM` 15: a posterolateral side branch that cannot run 15 mm clear of the crowd beyond the crux is left out rather than drawn as a stub (10 of 37 PL3s); the small-branch growth fills its territory.
  - Septals: fan variants x 1.5 / 0.5 / 2 on the phi turn; a septal that cannot run 12 mm clear of the previous one is left out (the first septal is always drawn).
- `COURSE_BAND` 35-90 mm (tortuosity.ts): where the library's "20-60 mm" filter (differences of 21 and 61 mm moving averages) responds. Its gain is 0.89 at 45 mm, 0.72 at 70 mm and 0.45 at 100 mm, but only 0.03 at 20 mm.
- Territory ownership offsets:
  - `OWN_PENALTY` { main 3, branch 2.5, twig -2.5 } mm (was 0/0/0): the small branches win ties, so less flow is drained invisibly by the named vessels.
  - `LAD_OWN_PENALTY` -4.5 mm, now only beyond `LAD_OWN_FROM` = 0.35 of the LAD's length, so the proximal LAD wall is drained by its branches while the mid-LAD keeps its calibre.
  - `LAD_APEX_PENALTY` -10 mm, unchanged.
- First-generation small branches are allowed on the LAD up to ell 0.6 (was 0.3), and the trunk spacing factor is 1.2 (was 1.7).
- `FLOOR_TO_BRANCHES` 0.6: the share of a cited-floor flow deficit (D1, OM1, ramus near-ostial diameters, Divia Paul 2018) given to the floored branch's own small branches, in proportion to the territory they already drain.
- Septal territory:
  - `SEPTAL_W` 7.5 mm² per mm (was 7);
  - order weights S1 x2 (unchanged), S2-S5 x `SEPTAL_ORDER_W` = 1.5 / 1.35 / 1.2 / 1.0, later ones 0.6 falling to 0.2 (was 1.1 / 1.0 / 0.9, then 0.7 falling to 0.3);
  - septal lengths S1 about 55 mm and later ones 46 falling to 22 mm (was 50, then 38 falling to 20).
  - The first septal is "more pronounced" (Kesieme 2024, S3 facts #23). The rest is model.

**coronaryAtlas.ts**
- `lvWidthMean` 1. This new named prior keeps the old value: a 1.1 setting widened the projected tree by only 1-2 mm and lengthened the LAD.
