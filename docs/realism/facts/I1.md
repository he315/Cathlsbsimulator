# Facts used by track I1 (wave-2 integration, step 1)

Step 1 integrates wave-1 modules; it introduces no literature constant. Every constant added in
`src/render/xray/sceneBuilder.ts`, `src/ui/sim/FluoroView.tsx` and `src/ui/sections/AngioPci.tsx`
is listed here with its basis. Constants carried over unchanged from the wave-0 scene builder
(contrast front velocity 115 mm/s, washout, blush, root plume, catheter wall 0.32 /mm, root weight
0.45, 72 bpm stand-in heart rate, quiet-breathing formula) are not repeated; they are model
parameters of the earlier code and will be replaced by the contrast-transport steps of this track.

## Calibrations measured with the realism harness (scripts/realism)

All measured on the S0 standard capture set (six reference views, 63-frame cine at 15 frames/s,
20 cm field, 768 px, plus the 40-frame fluoroscopy companion), scored with

(matched pipeline). Sets live in `datasets/tmp/captures/`, scorecards in

Cross-check (in `injection.test.ts`): the 6 Fr diagnostic specification's 28 mL/s at 600 psi through 0.056 inch x
100 cm implies a Poiseuille viscosity of 14.8 mPa s, between the label's 37 and 20 deg C values; at that flow
the Reynolds number is about 2300 (transitional), so the vendor figure is not used as a hand-injection value.
At hand-injection flows every lumen stays laminar (Reynolds 50-2000, tested).

From S9 (facts/S9.md): the per-vessel resting flows (`coronaryFlowFor`, Huo-Kassab r^(7/3) distribution,
1.0 mL/s at 3 mm, a model parameter of S9) and the phasic flow shape `phasicFlow()`; the 93 mmHg resting MAP
(`OSTIAL_PRESSURE_MMHG`, the back pressure at the catheter tip).

## Physics (no literature value needed)

Poiseuille tube resistance 128 mu L / (pi d^4); concentric annulus 8 mu L / (pi [R^4 - a^4 - (R^2 - a^2)^2 / ln(R/a)]);
jet exit loss rho u^2 / 2; syringe pressure = (force - friction) / barrel area; Duhamel superposition of the
ostial strength history over a step response; the laminar (Poiseuille, no diffusion) cross-section-averaged
step response 1 - theta / (2 tau) for tau > theta / 2.

## Literature opened in the revision

| Constant | Value | Source and quote |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration target of `CHRONIC_RESERVE_ANTEGRADE` | corrected TIMI frame count 28.56 in vessels with a flow-limiting stenosis (FFR <= 0.80) against 21.64 without, a ratio of 1.32 | Li X et al. ## Calibrated against the library or a sourced target

| Constant | Value | Method |

| `CHRONIC_RESERVE_ANTEGRADE` (`contrastTransport.ts`) | 0.57 | S9's antegrade maximal capacity over resting demand, `a = hypFactor x (1 - collateral) x CFR`, is 1.43 at 70 %, 0.92 at 75 %, 0.47 at 80 %, 0.17 at 85 %, 0.04 at 90 % and 0.002 at 95 % (case 0 LAD, lesion at 0.38, 12 mm; `vite-node` script, numbers in I1.md).
