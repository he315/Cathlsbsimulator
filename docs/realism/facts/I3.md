# I3 facts: catheter shape calibration, dilated root, Amplatz technique, budget

Every constant added or changed by track I3 under `src/physics/` is listed here as one of three kinds:

1. **Sourced.** The URL or DOI that was opened in this session and the quoted value.
2. **Library-measured.** Measured from the local real angiogram library, with the script and method.
3. **Model or tracing parameter.** Named as such in a code comment, with no claim to a source.

The facts of stream S7 (`docs/realism/facts/S7.md`) still hold for everything I3 did not change.

## Model and tracing parameters

**Tracing** (`calibration/ridge.ts`, `trace.ts`): Gaussian scale 2 px; dark-line strength lambda1 - |lambda2|; exposed-field border SD 4 and 6 px inset; entry band 6 px, maxima above twice the band's 90th centile and 1.5; step 1.5 px, turn at most 24° per step, 15% straight-ahead preference, 6 px look-ahead, re-centring within 2.5 px; tip where the look-ahead strength stays below 30% of the shaft median for 5 steps; pre-filter: tip 15 px inside the field, length at least 80 mm, width 4-10 px, distal 70 mm valley depth median at least 8 grey levels with at least 85% of points deeper than 3.

**Shape comparison** (`calibration/shapeError.ts`): distal window 60 mm of projected arclength (40 mm also reported); in-plane rotation at most 30°, 3° grid refined to 0.75°; 8 ICP translation updates; 2 mm sampling; symmetric mean closest-point distance; per-run best over the engaged states of the allowed catheters; no scale fitted.

**Catheter shapes** (`catheterShapes.ts`):
- `SHAPE_PARAMS.jl` and `.jr` hold the wave-1 Judkins values unchanged (tip 3 mm, primary 9 mm/90°, arm 10·n − 6 mm, secondary radius 5.5·n mm/180°; Judkins right 3 mm, 10 mm/60°, 10·n − 12 mm, 22 mm/30°). The coordinate search found no change that held on held-out starts (wave2/I3.md).
- `SHAPE_PARAMS.jlBySize["5"]`: Judkins left 5 secondary curve 22 mm, 185°, primary 95° (chosen by a scan over all validated configurations for engagement in the dilated root; see wave2/I3.md).
- `SHAPE_PARAMS.al`: Amplatz left tip 4 mm, reverse tip bend 6 mm/−50°, hook radius 12 + 6·size mm (AL1 18 mm, AL2 24 mm: the sinus radius of the normal and the dilated model root), hook turn 130° (chosen by a scan for engagement with the Amplatz technique).

**Classifier** (`engagementClassifier.ts`): `PROLAPSE_EXCESS_DEG` = 90°: a fold counts as prolapse when the catheter's total turning from the tip to the shaft 10 mm above the fold exceeds the free shape's total turning over the same arclength by more than 90°. Measured populations (all six solver configurations): Judkins right 4 pushed in the left sinus 127-185° beyond its free shape; Amplatz hooks seated in a cusp −9 to 66°. Current AL1 (normal root) and AL2 (dilated root) on the fixture: pushed 48 mm from the root, at most 68°; pushed 120 mm, 107-212° (a loop on the valve).

**Operator procedures** (`manoeuvres.ts`, test protocol, not device or anatomy constants):
- Amplatz technique: every 0.5 s, pull back 3 mm if the tip is in the ventricle; re-seat with a 2 mm advance when the curve's lowest point (distal 80 mm) is more than 12 mm above the valve plane; otherwise advance 1.5 mm below the expected ostial level (left main height − 1 mm) and withdraw 1 mm at or above it; turn 0.1 rad towards the expected left main direction beyond 15°; at most 30 corrections.
- `STARTS12`: 12 parking positions, 45-100 mm, for reliability comparisons.
- `protocol.jlTurn` 0.1 rad and `jlSteps` 24 (wave-1 values, now named); `jlJiggle` 0 and `jlFacing` null (switches used in the sensitivity runs; off by default).

**Budget** (`bench.ts`, `host.ts`): full-frame bench frames at 15 frames/s, 8 warm-up frames; "auto" measures each validated configuration (finest first) as the median of three 0.8 s repeats of each load on the scene's own geometry and keeps the first whose worst-case median is at most `AUTO_MARGIN` = 0.85 of the budget (model parameter: headroom for the difference between the short init measurement and a sustained run, observed at 2-7%); host cost median over the last 15 frames; budget 2 ms per 1/15 s of patient time.
