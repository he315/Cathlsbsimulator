## 4. Clinical text added to cases and quiz options

The rewritten quiz options keep each item's correct answer and explanation; distractors were lengthened
or the correct option shortened without adding numeric claims beyond those already in the item, its
explanation or other Academy content. The 116 consequence steps describe what follows a non-best choice;
scenario numbers in them (blood pressures, times, volumes in the narrative) are teaching scenario values,
not reference facts. Treatment statements in them repeat guidance already present in the Academy (for
example IM adrenaline 0.5 mg repeated at 5 minutes, low-dose dobutamine to 20 micrograms/kg/min for flow
reserve, a 250 mL fluid bolus in RV infarction, culprit-only PCI in shock after CULPRIT-SHOCK, routine
IABP without survival benefit after IABP-SHOCK II). Branches added to published cases are marked in their
text as teaching branches that are not part of the published report.
