# S9 facts: patient engine, haemodynamics, 12-lead ECG and transducer

This file lists every constant in `src/simulation/patient/**`, `src/simulation/ecg12.ts` and the S9 additions to `circulation.ts`, `physiology.ts` and `pressure.ts` that comes from literature or data. Each source was opened and read in this stream, and quotes are short and verbatim.

Constants not listed here fall into two groups:
- physics (hydrostatics, Boyle's law, lumped hydraulics, unit conversions);
- MODEL PARAMETERS, named `MP_*` or marked "MODEL PARAMETER" in a code comment.

Several full texts were read through the Europe PMC REST service (`https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/<PMCID>/fullTextXML`). For those, the DOI is given as the canonical link.

## Heparin and ACT

**FDA label.** "HEPARIN SODIUM, for intravenous use", https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2017/017029s140lbl.pdf (Reference ID 4144240). The text was extracted with `pdftotext`.

| Use in code | Quoted value |
|---|---|
| `HEPARIN_VD_L_PER_KG = 0.07` | "The volume of distribution is 0.07 L/kg." |
| `heparinHalfLifeS()` range | "The plasma half-life is dose-dependent and it ranges from 0.5 to 2 h." The clearance is described as "biphasic … rapid saturable clearance … and … slower first order elimination". The level-to-half-life mapping inside 0.5-2 h is a model choice. |
| `PROTAMINE_U_PER_MG = 100` | "Each mg of protamine sulfate neutralizes approximately 100 USP units." |
| immediate onset | "Peak plasma concentration and the onset of action are achieved immediately after intravenous administration." |

**ACT targets** (`ACT_TARGET`). Kim MH, Lee YS, Lee MS. ASEAN Heart J 2014, https://doi.org/10.7603/s40602-014-0007-z, which reproduces the 2011 ACCF/AHA/SCAI PCI guideline table:
- "Heparin 70 to 100 units/kg IV bolus, titrate to ACT 250 to 300 sec (50 to 70 units/kg IV if GP IIb/IIIa used, titrate to ACT 200 to 250 sec)".
- In the text: "250-300 seconds for Hemotec and 300-350 seconds for Hemochron systems".

**ACT slope calibration** (`MP_ACT_SLOPE_S_PER_U_ML = 160`). Soleimannejad M et al. J Cardiovasc Thorac Res 2014, https://doi.org/10.5681/jcvtr.2014.021.
- Method: "ACT levels 10 minutes after administration of 100U/kg IV heparin", measured on a Hemochron device.
- Result: "The range of ACT was between 165 to 750 seconds (mean 319.8 seconds)".
- How it is used: the slope is chosen so that 100 U/kg with a 120 s baseline gives about 320 s at 10 minutes (the test gives 290-350 s).
- The 120 s baseline ACT and the log-normal sensitivity spread (SD 0.3, chosen to cover the wide reported range) are MODEL PARAMETERS.

## Arrest management

## 12-lead ECG

**Lead vectors** (`DOWER` in `ecg12.ts`). Vondrak J, Penhaker M. Review of Processing Pathological Vectorcardiographic Records for the Detection of Heart Disease. Front Physiol 2022, https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2022.856590 (full text at https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2022.856590/full, reopened on 2026-10-05). Table 3 is "Leading vectors for deriving a 12-lead electrocardiogram from the Frank XYZ signal" (Dower). 34 of the 36 coefficients are used as printed, for example V1 = −0.515 X + 0.157 Y − 0.917 Z.

**Two typographical errors in Table 3, corrected.** Table 3 prints V4 Z as −0.604 and V5 X as 2.125. The same paper's Table 4, "Transformation matrix for Inverse Dower transformation (IDT)", is the least-squares inverse of the 8 independent leads (I, II, V1-V6). Its printed rows are:
- X: 0.156, −0.010, −0.172, −0.074, 0.122, 0.231, 0.239, 0.194
- Y: −0.227, 0.887, 0.057, −0.019, −0.106, −0.022, 0.041, 0.048
- Z: 0.022, 0.102, −0.229, −0.310, −0.246, −0.063, 0.055, 0.108

The pseudo-inverse (AᵀA)⁻¹Aᵀ of the Table 3 rows for those 8 leads matches Table 4 with a largest error of:
- 0.097 with the printed V4 Z = −0.604 and V5 X = 2.125 (for example the X row comes out 0.095 … 0.270 … 0.113 against the printed 0.156 … 0.239 … 0.194);
- 0.0008 with V5 X = 1.125 only;
- 0.0005 with V5 X = 1.125 and V4 Z = −0.601, which is exact to the 3 printed decimals.

The code therefore uses V4 = [1.213, 0.127, −0.601] and V5 = [1.125, 0.127, −0.086]. The cross-check is a test in `patient.test.ts` (largest error below 0.0006), and `INVERSE_DOWER` in `ecg12.ts` holds Table 4 as printed. With the printed V5 X, the V5 R wave was about twice V4 and V6 (1.79 mV against 0.87 and 0.80) and every V5 ST deviation was about 1.9 times too large; with the correction the normal beat gives V4 0.98, V5 1.09, V6 0.90 mV.

The same authors' IEEE JTEHM 2022 paper (https://doi.org/10.1109/jtehm.2022.3167009, Table 2) gives the inverse Dower matrix. It was used only to confirm the axis convention.

**ST-elevation criteria** (`stemiCriteria()`). Mokhtar WSW et al. BMC Med Educ 2026, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09632-7 (via Europe PMC PMC13463640). Quotes:
- "ST-segment elevation at the J-point, ≥ 1 mm in ≥ 2 adjacent leads (other than leads V2 and V3, where elevation of ≥ 2 mm in men or ≥ 1.5 mm in women is considered significant)".
- The Fourth Universal Definition "specifies slightly different thresholds for leads V2-V3 in young men (≥ 2.5 mm)".
- This is a secondary source; the UDMI document itself was not opened.
- The source gives no age for "young men". The code uses < 40 y as a named MODEL PARAMETER (`MP_YOUNG_MAN_AGE`), not as a sourced value.

**Dipole model.** The P, QRS, T, VT, paced, AF and VF waveforms and the ST and T gains are MODEL PARAMETERS. They were tuned until the normal tracing had rS in V1, qR in V5-V6, upright T in I, II and V2-V6, and an inverted aVR. `patient.test.ts` checks this.

## Coronary flow distribution

**Huo-Kassab exponent** (`HK_EXPONENT = 7/3`). Taylor DJ et al. Invasive validation of novel 1D models for computation of coronary fractional flow reserve. Cardiovasc Res 2025, https://doi.org/10.1093/cvr/cvaf168 (via Europe PMC PMC12638729).
- Quote: "the Huo-Kassab law of vascular scaling … Inlet flow = Outlet flow ( R in R out ) 7 3" (equation as rendered in the XML).
- The same paper states that the law is "supported by observational data from coronary arteries".

**Model parameters.**
- `REF_FLOW_ML_S` is 1.0 mL/s at 3.0 mm. It is the existing `RESTING_FLOW` model parameter of `physiology.ts`.
- `DEFAULT_CFR = 3.5` is in the range the existing physiology cases use (3.2-3.6).
- `CTO_COLLATERAL_HYP` (0.35 of normal maximal flow) and the region size are MODEL PARAMETERS.
- Chronic lesions and collaterals (all MODEL PARAMETERS, no source opened): collateral recruitment starts at 70% diameter stenosis (`MP_COLLATERAL_START`) and reaches `CTO_COLLATERAL_HYP` at 95% (`MP_COLLATERAL_FULL`); chronic disease never leaves a region with less than 1.3 times its resting demand as maximal capacity (`MP_CHRONIC_REST_RESERVE`); hyperaemia removes up to half of the collateral-dependent capacity (`MP_COLLATERAL_STEAL`, coronary steal). The design rule behind them is the clinical definition of stable angina (no ischaemia at rest), not a measured collateral flow.
- Only the culprit total occlusion of an acute coronary syndrome is thrombotic (no flow, no collaterals); every other total occlusion is taken as a chronic total occlusion. This is a modelling rule: AngioCAD records grades, not lesion age.
- Transmurality of ischaemia is judged on the supply failure that remains at resting demand (`RegionSupply.restDeficit`), so demand ischaemia behind an intact resting supply stays subendocardial (ST depression). The 0.45-0.80 smoothstep of that deficit is a MODEL PARAMETER.
- Default (tree-less) network: a D2 region was added (demand 0.12 mL/s, D1 reduced from 0.28 to 0.20) and the apical LAD direction was changed from [0.5, 0.65, −0.45] to [0.55, 0.3, −0.78] (normalised) so that a mid LAD occlusion gives anterior elevation without inferior elevation. Both are MODEL PARAMETERS.
- On the CASE_LIBRARY[0] tree, the computed total resting coronary flow is 4.2 mL/s (about 250 mL/min). This number is an output, not an input.

## Transducer chain

**Deranged Physiology, "Arterial line dynamic response testing"**, https://derangedphysiology.com/main/cicm-primary-exam/cardiovascular-system/Chapter-759/arterial-line-dynamic-response-testing:
- "a natural frequency in excess of 24 Hz in order to resolve fine features of the arterial line trace". This is used in `Transducer.dynamic().verdict`.
- After a fast flush, an adequate system shows at least one and no more than two oscillations. Used for teaching text only.

**Deranged Physiology, "Resonance, damping and frequency response"**, https://derangedphysiology.com/main/cicm-primary-exam/cardiovascular-system/Chapter-734/resonance-damping-and-frequency-response:
- Optimal damping "0.64-0.7". Used in the verdict.
- Typical commercial systems run at "35-40" Hz; older systems at "10-25 Hz" (Schwid et al 1988).
- Increasing tubing length increases damping.
- These are used to check that the simulated cath-lab line (100 cm catheter plus 150 cm line; fn about 24 Hz, ζ about 0.25 without air) is in a plausible range. The dimensions, the transducer compliance (3.0 × 10⁻⁶ mL/mmHg) and the damping gain (4) are MODEL PARAMETERS.

**Phlebostatic axis.** Oh C et al. BMC Anesthesiol 2023, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12871-023-01968-7 (via Europe PMC PMC9824971). Quote: "the transducer should be placed at an anatomical point close to the level of the right atrium (phlebostatic axis), in the fourth intercostal space at the midaxillary line".

**Physics, not literature.**
- Hydrostatic offset ρgh with saline at 1005 kg/m³ and g = 9.81 m/s². This gives 0.7395 mmHg/cm, so 10 cm is 7.4 mmHg, matching the plan's figure.
- Air compliance V/P_atm (Boyle, isothermal).
- Inertance ρℓ/(πr²), Poiseuille resistance 8μℓ/(πr⁴), ωn = 1/√(LC), ζ = (R/2)√(C/L).
- The amplitude ratio of a second-order system, exp(−πζ/√(1−ζ²)).

## Haemodynamic baseline

The generated blood pressures are anchored on `docs/SIM_FACTS.md` item 18 (systemic BP 120/80 mmHg, ESC/ERS 2022 Table 11). The spread, and its age, hypertension and LVEF dependence, are MODEL PARAMETERS.

These model parameters (`initialCirculation`, `MP_ENGINE`) were tuned until a normal adult gave a CO of 4-8 L/min (SIM_FACTS item 18):
- the incisura parameters (aortic valve inertance 0.0003 mmHg·s²/mL, closing volume 1.0 mL);
- the pericardial pressure-volume curve;
- the baroreflex gains;
- the CPR amplitude.

## Not used (could not be confirmed at source)

- A sourced normal baseline ACT. Item 18 of SIM_FACTS already lists normal LVEDP as unverified, and the baseline ACT is a MODEL PARAMETER.
- The atropine dose regimen and IV-bolus adenosine AV-block incidence. The ERC 2021 ALS guideline returned 403 and its PDF link now redirects, so the doses are user-entered and the response curves are MODEL PARAMETERS.
- A resting total coronary flow figure. The StatPearls page returned a CAPTCHA, so the total is derived from the tree as above.
- VF incidence per mL of contrast in the conus or with a damped RCA. These are MODEL PARAMETERS chosen so the relative risk ordering is right: conus > damped RCA > wedged > normal.

### MODEL PARAMETERS (no source opened)

- **Half-lives and effect-site lags** of every new drug in `DRUGS`. For oral antiplatelets the half-life is the offset of the platelet effect: 4 days for aspirin, clopidogrel and prasugrel, 12 h for ticagrelor. Cangrelor is 5 min (the module says only "short half-life").
- **Onsets** (`ORAL_ONSET_S`): aspirin 60 min whole and 30 min crushed; prasugrel 2 h and 1 h; ticagrelor crushed 1 h; clopidogrel crushed 4 h. Absorption uses a time constant of onset / 3.
- **Effect gains and ED50s** (`MP_FORMULARY`):
  - reversal Ki: flumazenil 0.08 mg, naloxone 0.06 mg;
  - inotrope, chronotrope and pressor gains;
  - antiarrhythmic ED50s;
  - furosemide venodilation and diuresis (0.25 mL/s at full effect);
  - bronchodilation;
  - the fraction of an IC microvascular dose that acts systemically (0.35);
  - antiplatelet ED50s;
  - heparin and bivalirudin anticoagulant ED50s;
  - bivalirudin ACT slope (190 s per mg/kg), calibrated to the sourced regimen and goal above;
  - andexanet and PCC reversal (no dose in the module);
  - DOAC effect half-life (12 h);
  - hazard reductions: thrombus 0.6 antiplatelet and 0.5 anticoagulant, stent thrombosis 0.8 and 0.3.
- **Interactions** (`MP_INTERACTION`): PDE-5 nitrate gain 2.5; nitrate tolerance at most 0.6, half at 100 µg; prolonged QTc 0.47 s and poor LVEF 40% (the module gives no numbers); amiodarone pro-arrhythmia chance 0.25, drawn once for each amiodarone bolus or infusion start that raises the long-QT or pre-excitation flag.
- **Heparin half-life mapping** (`MP_HEPARIN_HALF_LIFE`): h(c) = 19 + 512·c/(c + 11.8) min, with c the plasma level in U/mL. The constants are fitted so the time for the level to halve after 25, 100 and 400 U/kg is 30, 60 and 150 min. The volume of distribution 0.07 L/kg is from the FDA label above. The ACT 10 minutes after 100 U/kg is unchanged (about 320 s).
- **Engine** (`MP_FORMULARY_ENGINE`):
  - FiO2 0.04 per L/min of oxygen, capped at 0.6 (a model conversion);
  - oxygen at that ceiling relieves 75% of a hypoventilation or airway desaturation and 35% of a shunt (oedema) desaturation;
  - over-sedation starts at a respiratory depression of 0.45 and ends below 0.3;
  - IV adrenaline: VT is drawn once, with chance 0.5, when the pro-arrhythmic drive exceeds 0.5. The drive is half-maximal at 400 µg IV. A dose given during an arrest is marked and left out of this drive and of its hypertension term, so that return of circulation does not count it as a dose "with a pulse".
- **Dynamic LVOT obstruction** (`MP_LVOT`): resting obstruction 0.3, exponents inotropy 2, heart rate 1, preload 1.5 and afterload 1, maximum 0.85. Contractility is scaled by (1 − O)/(1 − 0.3), so the patient is at rest on arrival.
- **Thrombus hazard** (`MP_THROMBUS`): 1/900 per second per thrombotic lesion, times a seeded log-normal patient factor (log-SD 0.3), times the drug multiplier. Labs draw from the rate.
- **Presets** (`presetPatient`):
  - "vasodilated" loses 40% of vascular tone at rest;
  - "hcm-obstructive" has no coronary disease and an LVEF of at least 60%;
  - "lcx-stemi" copies the lcx-stemi scenario of `src/simulation/angioPci.ts` (54-year-old man, thrombotic 99% LCx at 0.42, 16 mm). The drawn AngioCAD grades are cleared and Prox LCx is set to grade 5, so the grades, the culprit and the lesion agree; the STEMI baseline rhythm is kept.
- **Team query thresholds** (`QUERY_ABOVE` in `src/simulation/team/params.ts`) for the new drugs. Each is set at or above the dose statement in `FORMULARY_REFERENCE`, so that the nurse queries only a slip of unit or decimal: flumazenil 1 mg, naloxone 2 mg, diazepam 20 mg, propofol 200 mg, dobutamine 1000 µg, dopamine 1000 µg, vasopressin 5 U (these three are infusion-only, so a bolus is refused first), isoprenaline 200 µg, furosemide 250 mg, salbutamol 10 mg, amiodarone 450 mg, lidocaine 200 mg, metoprolol 15 mg, flecainide 200 mg, aminophylline 500 mg, nicardipine 1000 µg, nitroprusside 1000 µg, eptifibatide 40000 µg, tirofiban 5000 µg, cangrelor 5000 µg, bivalirudin 150 mg, idarucizumab 5000 mg, andexanet 2000 mg, PCC 10000 U, aspirin 600 mg, clopidogrel 600 mg, prasugrel 60 mg, ticagrelor 180 mg.
- **Reaction components** (`responses.ts`): bronchodilation relieves up to 70% of the airway component, and an antiarrhythmic removes up to 50% of the ischaemic VF hazard. With neither drug given the engine behaves as before.

### Contrast ceiling (`contrastBudget.ts`)

| Rule | Module text | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Brilakis general | complication-management/05-patient-complications.ts:698 (Brilakis 2.6, 1.1) | stop before contrast (mL) reaches 3.7 × GFR (mL/min); count recent contrast such as a CT |
| Brilakis CTO | cath-handbook/03-safe-practice.ts:861 | contrast above 3.7 × creatinine clearance |
| Oxford | complication-management/05-patient-complications.ts:710 (Oxford ch 4) | no more than 5–7 mL/kg, depending on the agent |
| Advanced CKD advisory | complication-management/05-patient-complications.ts:704 (Brilakis 28.3) | eGFR below 30: ideally less contrast than the creatinine clearance; a ratio above 2 predicts injury |

MODEL PARAMETERS (`CONTRAST_BUDGET_DEFAULTS`):
- the warning threshold is 80% of the stricter ceiling, and the announcements are at 50% and 100% (LAB_BUILD_PLAN section 4);
- the Oxford figure is 5 mL/kg unless the agent's own figure is given, the stricter end of the range;
- earlier contrast counts within 7 days. The module says that "how long to wait after a CT is not stated in either book" (05-patient-complications.ts:735).

The engine emits `contrast.budget` events at each point once, in ascending order, from `crossedPoints()` (the helper the labs use), as well as the older MACD and contrast/CrCl `renal.threshold` events. When recent contrast has already used the whole ceiling, the first injection announces every point.
