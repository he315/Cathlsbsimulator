# pci-patient-coupling: model parameters

Patient coupling of the PCI lab: the engine's coronary network follows the treated lumen (`src/simulation/pci/actions/patient.ts`), the contrast transport follows the engine's live coronary flow (`src/simulation/pci/patientBridge.ts`, `src/render/xray/contrastTransport.ts`, `src/simulation/patient/engine.ts`), the PCI lab's hypotension causes (`src/simulation/pci/hypotension.ts`), arrhythmias, the defibrillator, CPR and the compression device (`src/ui/pci/PciLabSession.ts`, `src/ui/pci/consoles/DefibConsole.tsx`, `src/ui/pci/render/cpr.ts`).

Sourced values carry their location in the code and are not repeated as model parameters: the five measurement faults and the guide's true causes (Brilakis 28.1.1.1 and 28.1: pm-monitoring-pressure, pci-manual/02-monitoring.ts:270-297; cm-patient-complications-hypotension, complication-management/05-patient-complications.ts, the hypotension table and pitfalls; Brilakis 6.5 for catheter-induced ischaemia); the defibrillator energies (VF 150-200 J biphasic or 200-360 J monophasic, cp-complex-patients-lab-arrest and tip pci-arrest-defib-settings; stable arrhythmias 150 J biphasic or 200 J monophasic and poorly tolerated VT 200-360 J, cm-patient-complications-arrhythmia), offered as the dial `DEFIB_ENERGIES_J` [50, 100, 150, 200, 360] J (50 and 100 J are the dial's lower steps, not recommendations); a synchronised defibrillator that does not discharge in VF (the check in cp-complex-patients-lab-arrest, complex-pci/08-complex-patients.ts:633). Every value below is a MODEL PARAMETER: a setting of the simulator, not a clinical value.

## The engine (src/simulation/patient/engine.ts `MP_COUPLING`)

Every one is inert unless used: no effusion, no regurgitation, no refractory shocks, and the pulsus swing only where a lab turns it on (`setPulsusParadoxus`, the PCI lab). A lab that does not use them behaves as before.

| Constant | File | Value | Unit | Rests on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pulsusShare` | src/simulation/patient/engine.ts | 0.6 | share of the pericardial pressure | MODEL PARAMETER: with an effusion, breathing swings the pericardial constraint this share above the pericardial pressure at full inspiration and below it at full expiration (symmetric over a breath). It stands for ventricular interdependence, as the circulation model has one pericardial pressure for both ventricles. Gives about 15-30 mmHg of systolic swing at 120-155 mL of effusion and 1-3 mmHg without one (patientCoupling.test.ts, the hypotension tests) |
| `pulsusTidal` | src/simulation/patient/engine.ts | 0.22 | respiration signal (0..1) | the cardiac clock's default tidal excursion (src/simulation/clock/cardiacClock.ts `tidal`), so the swing spans a quiet breath |
| `pulsusBeats` | src/simulation/patient/engine.ts | 10 | beats | MODEL PARAMETER: the pulsus read-out takes the systolic swing over this many beats (at least one breath) |
| `arConductanceMlSPerMmHg` | src/simulation/patient/engine.ts | 8 | mL/s per mmHg at severity 1 | MODEL PARAMETER: acute aortic regurgitation through a cusp held open by a guide, root to LV in diastole; at the cause's severity 1 the lad-focal patient's guide trace goes from about 137/71 (mean 105) to 139/30 (mean 80), the aortic diastolic pressure meeting an LVEDP of about 33 mmHg: a wide pulse pressure and a fall of about 25 mmHg in the mean, the size of the module's example (128/70 to 94/48, mean about 89 to 63), which recovers at once when the guide comes back. The circulation model keeps the systolic pressure up (the LV ejects the regurgitant volume again), so the fall is in the diastolic pressure; raised from 3.5 after the review, when the fall (mean 105 to 88) was too small to be recognised |
| `displayedDampedPulseShare` | src/simulation/patient/engine.ts | 0.5 | share of the tip's pulse | MODEL PARAMETER: with `setDisplayedDampingClass` on (the PCI lab only), an aortic tip whose displayed pulse (the mean of the last four beats, not during a flush) is below half the pulse at the tip is classed 'damped', so contrast or a plug in the guide, a bulky device or air in the line gives the damped trace the operator reads (and the lab's 'pressure-damped'); the injection hazards read the tip's own class (`physicalTipState`) |
| `fluid()` with a negative volume | src/simulation/patient/engine.ts | ml / overS | mL/s | the fix of the build run 3 report: a negative entry is removed at its own rate, like a positive one, never below the stressed-volume floor of 20 mL the other losses use |
| `coronaryFlowScale(vessel)` | src/simulation/patient/engine.ts | live / rest | ratio | the engine's live ostial flow of a vessel over the flow the same network would carry at the resting perfusion pressure and demand, without hyperaemia or a catheter in the ostium (no new constant); cached per supply update, the resting flows until the network, occlusions, spasm or microvascular state change |
| `setCoronaryTree(..., { keepOcclusions })` | src/simulation/patient/engine.ts | option | | no constant: the PCI lab's sync after stenting rebuilds the network and keeps the occlusions and microvascular state that other callers set (the default still clears them) |

## The contrast transport (src/simulation/pci/patientBridge.ts, src/render/xray/contrastTransport.ts)

| Constant | File | Value | Unit | Rests on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MP_FLOW_SCALE_STEP` | src/simulation/pci/patientBridge.ts | 0.05 | ratio | MODEL PARAMETER: the live flow scale is passed in steps of this size, so the transport's cache stays warm |
| `MP_FLOW_SCALE_DEADBAND` | src/simulation/pci/patientBridge.ts | 0.1 | ratio | MODEL PARAMETER: a trunk already passed is dropped when its flow comes within this of rest (the engine's resting flow wanders a few per cent with the rate-pressure product), so at rest the transport runs exactly as before |
| `MP_FLOW_SCALE_ENTER` | src/simulation/pci/patientBridge.ts | 0.15 (the deadband and one step) | ratio | MODEL PARAMETER: hysteresis after the in-app review. A trunk not yet passed is passed only once its flow differs from rest by this much; while passed, its value is held until the flow moves a whole step (0.05) from it. The lcx-stemi patient's resting flow wanders between about 1.08 and 1.14 of rest with breathing, which without hysteresis flipped the record between 1.1 and 1.15 each second and ran a resting patient's contrast 10-15 % fast; with it nothing is passed (patientCoupling.test.ts: at most one change over 20 s of steady sinus rhythm) |
| `MP_FLOW_SCALE_START_S` | src/simulation/pci/patientBridge.ts | 1.5 | s of lab time | MODEL PARAMETER: no flow scale is passed in the first 1.5 s of a run. In lad-focal at wire-across the guide settles in the ostium over the first second and the engine's left ostial flow reads 0 from about 0.3 s to 1.1 s (a brief wedge of the start, not of the patient), which otherwise left an early injection unfilled; the margin covers the settle (patientCoupling.test.ts: no flow scale over the first 2 s) |
| `TransportMods.flowScale` | src/render/xray/contrastTransport.ts | per vessel | ratio | no constant: a region's flow is scaled by the nearest vessel on its path with a scale, so its transit times grow in proportion |
