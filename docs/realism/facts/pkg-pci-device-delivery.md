# pci-device-delivery: model parameters

Device delivery (`src/simulation/pci/actions/devices.ts` `advanceDevice`) gained the shaft travel down the guide from the valve (`deviceShaftMm`, the 90 and 100 cm shaft marks on the table in `src/ui/lab/TableView.tsx`), screening as the device leaves the guide tip (`counters.unscreened`), the pins as the technique (the wire pinned for the two-hand technique, the guide held as well for the independent-hand technique), the wire pulled back by a guide back-out, the guide drawn in by a withdrawal against friction, a stent deformed when it is pulled back into a guide that is not coaxial or forced against a guide extension's collar, a guide extension delivered stepwise (`moveExtension`: bare wire, over a device, inchworming, balloon-assisted tracking), the support options (`recordSupport`, `deepBreath`, `setAnchorBalloon`, `setSupportCatheter`; buddy wire and guide exchange are the existing actions) and a start state with a device already stopped at the lesion (`applyStartSetup`). The guide (`src/simulation/pci/guide.ts`) gained the back-up of the left hand, an anchor balloon and a support catheter, a held deep breath, the device running inside an extension, and an extension's edge catching on the bare wire. Device dimensions come from the catalogue (`src/simulation/pci/equipment.ts`: the stents, balloons and extensions, their profiles, stiffness, sizes and cylinder lengths), which is already sourced. Every force and share here is a MODEL PARAMETER chosen so the behaviour matches what the module text describes; none is a clinical measurement. Where the plan asks for calibration against the rod solver's guideBackup manoeuvre, that was not done in this package (the rod guide is off by default); the values are tuned to the analytic delivery model of `guide.ts`.

## MODEL PARAMETERS

| Parameter | File | Value | Unit | Rests on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `DEFAULT_GUIDE_LENGTH_CM` | `actions/devices.ts` | 100 | cm | The usual guide length (guide.ts: an absent `lengthCm` means 100 cm) |
| Marks at exactly the guide lengths | `actions/devices.ts` `SHAFT_MARKS_CM`, `deviceShaftMm` | Y-connector length ignored | - | The module text says the device is then "close to" the guide tip; the shaft beyond the valve is the guide length plus the distal end's route position |
| Introduction point | `actions/devices.ts` `advanceDevice` (`fromValve`) | 0 mm of shaft (hands), or 40 mm short of the guide tip (headless default, unchanged) | mm | The hands push the whole guide length; scripts keep the earlier position so older tests are unchanged |
| `MP_BACKOUT_MM` | `actions/devices.ts` | 0.3 | mm | The engine's long-standing back-out threshold (and `PCI_REACTION_SNAP_MM`) |
| `MP_WIRE_RETREAT_SHARE` | `actions/devices.ts` | 0.5 | share of the back-out | An unpinned wire tip slips back with a guide that is pushed out (pm-monitoring#3 a); half of the guide's travel |
| `MP_SUCK_SHARE`, `MP_SUCK_MAX_MM`, `MP_SUCK_LOG_MM` | `actions/devices.ts` | 0.4; 1.5; 0.3 | share of the pull times friction/back-up; mm per pull; mm | A device pulled back against friction draws an unheld guide in (design: "withdrawal against friction pulls the guide in"); the pull before deflation is pci-indeflator's `withdrawBeforeDeflation` |
| `MP_HELD_SEAT_SHARE`, `MP_HELD_SEAT_MAX_MM`, `MP_HELD_SEAT_DEPTH_MAX` | `actions/devices.ts` | 0.1; 0.5; 2.5 | share of the part of each push beyond the guide tip; mm per push; mm | The independent-hand technique: "part of the push seats the guide" (plan design). Travel inside the guide shaft seats nothing; the depth cap is below `GUIDE_TOO_DEEP_MM` (3 mm, guide.ts) and inside the 2-3 mm seat pm-wiring-guide-seat calls optimal, so the left hand alone never raises catheter-too-deep |
| `MP_GUIDE_HAND_N` | `guide.ts` | 0.08 | N | The back-up the left hand adds holding and gently pushing the guide; about half a 6 Fr Judkins right's back-up in this model |
| `MP_HELD_BACK_SHARE` | `guide.ts` | 0.5 | share | The left hand on the guide takes half of a stalled push's back-out |
| `MP_BACKED_OUT_FLOOR_MM` | `guide.ts` | -15 | mm | A guide pushed out of the ostium falls back into the root, no further: initialGuide's out-of-ostium depth (repeated pushes against a disengaged guide no longer drive its depth without limit) |
| `MP_SHAFT_SLACK_MM` | `guide.ts` `deliver` (`DeliveryInput.slackMm`, set by `advanceDevice`/`moveExtension` with `byHand`, which the 'delivery' binding passes) | 1 | mm | A stalled push by the lab's hands backs the guide out by at most the hub travel the device did not take up plus this stored slack (and never more than the excess force over the guide's stiffness, nor 8 mm), so small bites back the guide out progressively and the learner sees it start to back out before it leaves the ostium (`MP_DISENGAGE_MM` -2 mm): from the ostium, one 1 mm bite takes it to -2 mm at most, still engaged. Headless scripts without `byHand` keep the engine's unbounded back-out (the default, unchanged) |
| Over-stent limit of a guide extension | `actions/devices.ts` `extensionMode` | the crimped (undeployed) stent's proximal edge minus `MP_EXT_MARGIN_MM` (2 mm); a balloon or microcatheter as before | mm | The stent can catch the extension's edge or be deformed at its tip (complication-management/04-equipment-loss, guide extension row, Brilakis Fig. 30.34); an advance stopped there is counted in `DeliveryRecord.extensionBlocked` |
| Nodal branches not offered as anchors | `actions/devices.ts` `NODAL_BRANCH`, `anchorBranches` | SAN, AVN left out | rule | The module text names the conus or an acute marginal for the RCA and a diagonal for the LAD (complex-pci/13-uncrossable, 'Side-branch anchor'; pci-manual/12-prep-and-stent), never a nodal branch; the console lists the rest largest first by full name |

| `MP_ANCHOR_OVERSIZE` | `actions/devices.ts` | 1.1 | ratio to the branch reference | Tolerance on the module's 1:1 sizing before an anchor counts as oversized |
| `MP_SUPPORT_CATHETER_BACKUP` | `guide.ts` | scaffold 1.8, strut 1.6, anchoring balloon 1.5 | multiplier | Support catheters as back-up multipliers labelled as model parameters (plan design); ordering by how firmly each grips the wall in the module's description |
| `MP_BREATH_BEND_SCALE`, `MP_BREATH_HOLD_S` | `guide.ts`; `actions/devices.ts` | 0.75; 10 | scale of the bend load; s | "Deep breath (team breath hold scales bend integrals)" (plan design); a small extra in the module's support menu |
| `MP_IN_EXTENSION_N` | `guide.ts` | 0.01 | N | A device inside an extension in the vessel meets only the guide's curves and this |
