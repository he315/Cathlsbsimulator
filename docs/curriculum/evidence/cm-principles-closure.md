## Possible issues in existing Academy lessons (for the fixer)

- `comp-dissection-ostial-sidebranch` pitfalls: "avoid jailing hydrophilic polymer-jacketed wires". Brilakis p.299 describes this as controversial and cites a microscopy study in which only 2 of 115 polymer-coated jailed wires showed mild damage versus 55% (63 of 120) of non-polymer wires; he lists polymer-jacketed wires among those that can be jailed. The Academy statement is stronger than the source.
- `comp-dissection-nhlbi` steps: "Stent: ... usually working distal to proximal so that blood in the false lumen is not pushed further downstream". In Brilakis (p.437, p.445) distal-first stenting is the treatment for intramural haematoma, not a general rule for dissections. Its "low-pressure prolonged inflation to tack the flap, then stent" is also not how Brilakis frames it (p.439: prolonged balloon angioplasty alone may sometimes suffice when a stent cannot be delivered; stenting preferred). Worth checking against a reference.
- `comp-dissection-nhlbi` table, abrupt closure row "Air": "forceful flush" is not in Brilakis 25.2.3.3 or Oxford p.327 (both say aspirate; Brilakis advances a workhorse wire to break up bubbles). Belongs to cm-flow-embolism's checkers.
- `comp-principles-doctrine` table "ACT checks every 30 minutes in long cases": Brilakis 21.3 (p.390) gives every 20–30 minutes once therapeutic, for CTO PCI.
- `comp-dissection-nhlbi` steps, 'If the wire is lost' (complications.ts line 781): recommends confirming the true lumen by 'a microcatheter tip injection'. Brilakis p.438 warns that this enlarges the subintimal space if the wire is subintimal and gives the microcatheter-plus-workhorse-wire test as safer; this module teaches the latter (returned as an Academy fix in round 1).
- `comp-dissection-nhlbi` NHLBI table (complications.ts lines 744–745): A 'little or no contrast persistence', B 'minimal persistence'. Brilakis Fig. 6.5 (p.132) has 'no persistence' for both; Oxford p.321 describes persistence in both. Returned as an Academy fix in round 1.
