## Lesson cp-complex-patients-shock-setup

- Four challenges (identify early, understand cause, stabilise haemodynamics, revascularise if indicated); shock team and standardised algorithms: brilakis p.428
- Early identification before refractory and metabolic shock improves survival; local algorithms and dedicated shock team: brilakis p.428 (24.2.1)
- Low EF raises risk of cardiogenic shock in ACS; prior revascularisation, comorbidities (renal failure, diabetes, future surgery), medications given: brilakis p.377 (20.1)
- Tehrani criteria: SBP <90 mmHg for >30 min or drugs to maintain; end-organ hypoperfusion; lactate >2 mmol/L: brilakis p.429 (Fig 24.7); ref tehrani-shock-team-2019
- SCAI stages A–E names and summaries; re-stage; arrest raises risk: ref scai-shock-2022 (as taught in Academy mcs-shock-definitions)
- Oxford ACC/AHA class 1: angiography as prelude to revascularisation in cardiogenic shock within 18 hours of onset of shock: oxford p.27
- One-call line to interventional cardiology, cardiac surgery, advanced heart failure, critical care; transfer to lab or CICU; ADHF-CS: RHC and echo; AMI-CS: RHC, coronary angiography and revascularisation, assessment of peripheral vascular anatomy: brilakis p.429 (Fig 24.7)
- Consent problematic if moribund or after opiates; best interests and/or consent by proxy: oxford p.249
- Resuscitation status settled before procedure: as taught in pm-planning (tip diag-planning-resuscitation-status; brilakis p.31 checklist DNR/DNI)
- Radial default for primary PCI (MATRIX, RIFLE, RIVAL): oxford p.249
- Femoral may be preferred in shock (frequent need for MCS and rapid delineation of anatomy): brilakis p.429 (24.2.4)
- Radial spasm risk factors include IV vasopressors or cardiac arrest; treat with vasodilators, switch to ipsilateral ulnar, contralateral radial or femoral: brilakis p.87 (4.3.5.3)
- Impella CP 14 Fr sheath; iliofemoral >5 mm without severe calcification, tortuosity or obstruction: brilakis p.251 (14.1.4)
- NCSI: femoral arterial access via direct visualisation with ultrasound and fluoroscopy; venous femoral or internal jugular: brilakis p.430 (Fig 24.8)
- Prompt conversion to femoral when radial engagement delays reperfusion, "time = muscle": brilakis p.377–378 (20.4)
- Check 1: brilakis p.429, p.430, p.87; oxford p.249
- Serial lactate and invasive haemodynamics; RV assessment (RA, PAPI); CPO >0.6 W while limiting escalating vasopressors and inotropes: brilakis p.428 (24.2.2)
- Wedge >18 mmHg LV failure; PAPi <0.9 RV failure; wedge >25 with SvO2 <50% high risk of periprocedural pulmonary oedema and shock; Swan Ganz left in place: brilakis p.249 (14.1.1)
- False hypotension list (open Y-connector, open or air-filled transducer connection, damping, catheter obstruction by air/thrombus/contrast, bulky equipment); remove without injection if aspiration fails: brilakis p.485 (28.1.1.1)
- Amplatz and deeply curved EBU can push aortic cusp open causing acute AR; repositioning corrects: brilakis p.487 (Fig 28.2)
- True hypotension: hypovolaemia, cardiac failure, peripheral vasodilation; RHC profile (LV filling pressure low/high/low; CO low/low/high; LV filling pressure can be low in RV failure): brilakis p.485–486 (Fig 28.1)
- Check 2 arithmetic: CPO = MAP × CO ÷ 451, PAPi = (sPAP − dPAP) ÷ RA: brilakis p.429–430; LV-dominant → microaxial pump: brilakis p.252 (14.1.9); NCSI pre-PCI pump thresholds: brilakis p.430
- Vasopressors and inotropes increase myocardial oxygen consumption, may cause arrhythmias and reduce recovery; Brilakis gives 1,679 shock patients (19% cardiogenic), dopamine more arrhythmias than norepinephrine; avoid alpha agonists (no inotropy, add afterload); early MCS reduces need: brilakis p.428–429 (24.2.3). SOAP II abstract (PMID 20200382, re-read round 1): 1,679 randomised; arrhythmias 24.1% vs 12.4%; 280 with cardiogenic shock (16.7%, taught as 'about 17%'), higher 28-day mortality with dopamine in that subgroup (P = 0.03, Kaplan-Meier subgroup analysis): ref debacker-soap2-2010. The module states both figures and that the trial report gives 280.
- Norepinephrine preferred vasopressor for cardiogenic and septic shock: brilakis p.56 (3.6.1.2)
- Pitfalls and pearl: summarise the above (pearl is teaching advice built on brilakis p.428 serial measurement)

## Lesson cp-complex-patients-shock-culprit

- Five ingredients: early diagnosis; Swan Ganz; culprit-only revascularisation; MCS before vs after PCI with device by haemodynamic need and local availability/expertise; subsequent care (dedicated team, haemodynamic monitoring, adjust support, access to advanced therapies): brilakis p.428 (Fig 24.6); ref brilakis-eckman-shock-2018
- Revascularisation should be provided; early revascularisation improves mortality: brilakis p.431 (24.2.7)
- SHOCK: 302 patients, LV failure, ERV by CABG or angioplasty vs initial medical stabilisation; 30-day 46.7% vs 56.0% (NS); 6-month 50.3% vs 63.1%: ref shock-1999 (PMID 10460813); 6-year survival 32.8% vs 19.6%: ref hochman-shock-longterm-2006
- Engagement in STEMI: RCA PCI without left anatomy may be suboptimal (LM/MVD may need support or CABG; balloon angioplasty only may be preferable); immediate culprit engagement shortens door-to-balloon by 8–13 min; Fig 20.1 sequence: brilakis p.378 (20.5)
- Culprit clues (ECG, slow flow, haziness, collaterals, distal branch occlusion, LV gram, OCT, TTE incl. mechanical complications: ventricular rupture, VSD, papillary muscle rupture): brilakis p.378–379 (20.7)
- Wire fails because lesion is not the culprit (may be CTO); review films, prior films, ECG; OCT; thrombus or ulceration suggests culprit: brilakis p.380 (20.8.4)
- About two thirds multivessel; few have more than one culprit, which should be treated; culprit-only at presentation (CULPRIT-SHOCK), staged revascularisation: brilakis p.431 (24.2.7); also brilakis p.379 (20.7)
- CULPRIT-SHOCK table: 706 patients; 30-day 45.9% vs 55.4% (RR 0.83); death RR 0.84: ref culprit-shock-2017; 1-year death 50.0% vs 56.9% (RR 0.88, CI 0.76–1.01); repeat revascularisation 32.3% vs 9.4%; HF rehospitalisation 5.2% vs 1.2%: ref thiele-culprit-shock-1y-2018
- Oxford: CULPRIT-SHOCK lower 30-day mortality and severe renal failure; guidelines recommend culprit-only: oxford p.252
- 2023 ESC: culprit-only class I; staged non-IRA IIa C: ref licordari-esc-acs-2024; no window after shock: as taught and verified in pm-target (licordari)
- Check 1: refs above; FFR in shock brilakis p.431 (24.2.12); CTO not culprit brilakis p.380, NCSI p.430
- Support decision informed by RHC and team (HF, surgery, intensivists); limited data: brilakis p.431 (24.2.14)
- Oxford: routine IABP not recommended after CRISP-AMI and IABP-SHOCK; MCS no 30-day survival improvement vs IABP; case-by-case on age, comorbidities, neurological function, prospects for long-term survival, predicted QoL: oxford p.252
- NCSI pre-PCI pump (LVEDP >15 or CI <2.2, suitable anatomy); door-to-support <90 min: brilakis p.430; single-arm, 171 patients, 72% survival to discharge: ref basir-ncsi-2019
- DanGer Shock: pump before PCI, lower 180-day mortality, more complications, comatose OHCA excluded: ref danger-shock-2024 (as verified in pm-support-choice)
- IABP-SHOCK II and ECLS-SHOCK no 30-day mortality reduction: refs iabp-shock-ii-2012, ecls-shock-2023
- TIMI III flow; intracoronary vasodilators if not achieved: brilakis p.430
- FFR affected by coronary afterload; may be inaccurate in shock: brilakis p.431
- Post-PCI CPO and PAPi; vascular assessment (femoral angiogram, Doppler, external bypass); ICU daily haemodynamic and vascular assessment, haemolysis, Impella position; explant criteria; 3–5 days to LVAD/transplant centre or palliative care: brilakis p.430
- Check 3 arithmetic and NCSI branch (CPO >0.6 and PAPI <0.9 → right-sided support if clinical suspicion of RV dysfunction): brilakis p.430
- Drill (angio-pci LCx STEMI scenario), sim (impella), videos x9bB5Hab9A0, k_WRU_1jU60, 4HsjKW4hA1c (titles only)

## Lesson cp-complex-patients-tavr-before

- High prevalence of CAD in severe AS: ref yudi-tavr-reaccess-2018 (abstract); diagnostic angiography routine in symptomatic patients assessed for valve surgery because non-invasive diagnosis of stenoses is difficult with valve disease: oxford p.27
- Work-up: systematic coronary, aortic and peripheral assessment; aortography always for valve–ascending aorta angulation; haemodynamics depending on reliability of non-invasive studies (gradients, RHC); transfemoral commonest and preferable; size, tortuosity, calcification; axillary and subclavian if marked iliofemoral disease: oxford p.131
- AS or AR with dilated ascending aorta may need larger catheters: brilakis p.34 (1.5)
- CT list (femoral and axillary arteries; annulus diameter, area, perimeter; root and STJ for self-expandable; whole aorta; valve calcification; coronary ostia height; working view angles): oxford p.30
- AS waveform may appear "dampened" but is parvus and tardus; side holes mask damping: brilakis p.111 (5.6.2)
- High coronary flow in severe AS among causes of failure to fill; larger lumen, avoid side holes, stronger injection; declare nondiagnostic: brilakis p.127 (6.6.3.1)
- Crossing AV rarely performed, hazardous, silent emboli on MRI; 5 F AL1 and hydrophilic straight-tipped wire; exchange for pigtail with exchange length J wire; controlled manipulation; Prussian helmet sign: oxford p.129–130
- Check 1: brilakis p.111, p.127
- Iliofemoral angiography: graded pigtail just above distal aortic bifurcation; table elevated; pan to femoral heads both sides; pump injection e.g. 20 mL at 20 mL/s; grading for diameter; invasive angiography identifies puncture site and whether tortuous arteries straighten with extra-stiff wire: oxford p.131
- FFR decreases after TAVR (hyperaemic flow increase), iFR unchanged; FFR with caution in severe AS: brilakis p.427 (24.1.12); ref ahmad-tavr-physiology-2018; limited validation in AS: oxford p.234; one 28-patient study: ref ahmad note (taught in pm-physiology)
- Timing table (controversial, increased risk, may decrease TAVR complications; same time convenient but complexity and contrast; deferring allows symptom attribution but engagement challenging): brilakis p.426 (24.1.7)
- NOTION-3 details: ref lonborg-notion3-2024 (abstract re-read)
