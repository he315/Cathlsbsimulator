## Lesson pm-planning-steps

- PCI goal is to restore unimpeded epicardial flow without complications; 14 steps; planning is first and previews the others; plans can and should change with new information but a plan is invaluable: brilakis p.25–26 (Introduction), p.29
- Steps always done (planning, monitoring, medications, access, engagement, angiography except zero-contrast PCI which still needs a prior angiogram, target selection, wiring, closure) and not always (lesion preparation, sometimes direct stenting though generally discouraged; stenting, sometimes balloon including DCB or thrombectomy only; physiology; imaging; haemodynamic support): brilakis p.25–26
- Most problems averted by careful selection and preparation: oxford p.229
- Monitoring: baseline ECG and heart rate; vital signs and pulse oximetry: brilakis p.33 (1.2); baseline so later changes can be identified: brilakis p.37 (2.2)
- Medications items (allergies, aspirin given, aspirin allergy desensitised, contrast allergy premedicated, P2Y12 for planned PCI or STEMI, metformin, insulin, warfarin, DOAC): brilakis p.33 (1.3)
- Access items (radial harvested for CABG, AV fistula avoid arm, previous sites and closure device, Angio-Seal within 90 days → contralateral femoral or radial, previous complication → avoid same site, PAD → avoid severely diseased or occluded iliofemoral or subclavian, STEMI/warfarin/DOAC/high bleeding risk favour radial, patient preference for femoral in heavy hand/arm users, morbid obesity favours radial, high INR and low platelets favour radial, prior films, chest CT incl arteria lusoria, abdominal CT for CFA bifurcation, ultrasound, outcome = site and sheath size/length): brilakis p.33–34 (1.4)
- Engagement items (CABG anatomy from surgical report and films; catheters used; switch access site if engagement was difficult; aortic CT for dilatation and anomalous coronaries; AS or AR with dilated ascending aorta may need larger catheters): brilakis p.34 (1.5)
- Angiography in renal failure (biplane, fewer cine projections, IVUS, contrast-saving systems, iso-osmolar, hydration 1–3 mL/kg/h normal saline pre and post); prior radiation skin injury (limit cine runs, keep area out of beam): brilakis p.35 (1.6)
- Target: symptoms help determine need; noninvasive tests identify culprits; prior angiograms for interval change; recent angiography or CTA allows target determination beforehand: brilakis p.35 (1.7), p.33
- Wiring, lesion preparation (prior failure to expand; severe calcium → atherectomy, laser, IVL; large thrombus → antithrombotic treatment and thrombectomy), stenting (DAPT ability), closure (active infection or immunocompromised → avoid closure devices), physiology (equivocal symptoms without noninvasive ischaemia; prior reaction or contraindication to adenosine), imaging (prior PCI of target strongly favours imaging): brilakis p.35 (1.8–1.13)
- Haemodynamic support items (HF symptoms, low EF, JVP, oedema, crackles, pulses, BNP, lactate in shock, echo, access imaging, RHC values incl CPO and PAPI; consider support with reduced EF, poor haemodynamics, complex high-risk PCI): brilakis p.36 (1.14)
- Prior angiogram review list (femoral bifurcation, PAD, calcification, prior stents, untreated old lesions, crossover, groin scar, kissing iliac stents or stenosis, radial loops, subclavian stenosis or tortuosity, long sheath, anomalous origin, LIMA/graft engagement, catheters and support, guidewires, stent delivery, atherectomy): brilakis p.32 (Table 1.1)
- Check 1 (Angio-Seal within 90 days): brilakis p.33
- Check 2: guide support ranking for RCA (JR4 +, AR1/2 ++, hockey stick/AL1/2 +++): oxford p.214 (Table 8.4); anticipate larger guide for rotational atherectomy to avoid switching mid-PCI: oxford p.214
- Pitfalls: radiation skin injury on back may need postponement or modification: brilakis p.33; CABG report rather than assumed anatomy: brilakis p.335
- Tips: pci-planning-fourteen-steps (p.25–26, p.29); pci-planning-old-films (p.32, p.35); access-planning-site-history (p.33); pci-planning-imaging-restenosis (p.35); pci-planning-adenosine-history (p.35); access-planning-closure-infection (p.35 1.11; p.31 Table 1.1 immunosuppressants → avoid closure devices)
- Video ELu-yJ7USOU: note restates the registry title only

## Lesson pm-planning-bailout

- Four areas of risk and benefit (CAD impact, operator competence including rested staff, lesion complexity, comorbidities): brilakis p.140 (7.1.8), p.142 (Fig 7.3)
- Complication lists acute (acute vessel closure, perforation, equipment loss, MI, emergent CABG, tamponade, arrhythmias, access, thromboembolic, contrast, radiation injury, shock, death) and long-term (ISR, stent thrombosis, DAPT-related): brilakis p.142 (Fig 7.3)
- Aorto-ostial: preloaded wire, hit and run, or wire with non-selective engagement; fibrotic, calcified, rigid, recoil, may need atherectomy; damping common: brilakis p.271 (15.1.1, 15.1.2)
- Bifurcation: most techniques through 6 Fr; V-stenting and SKS need at least 7 Fr; easier to pass two pieces of equipment through a larger guide: brilakis p.295
- Left main: review prior films (RCA disease or occlusion, collaterals from RCA, LM protected by graft, severe calcium needing atherectomy); LV function, haemodynamics, presentation, comorbidities; RHC for filling pressures and output and support decision; defibrillation pads; early vasopressors such as norepinephrine: brilakis p.329 (17.1, 17.2)
- Calcium: large guides, support techniques, imaging, atherectomy, IVL: brilakis p.355 (19.1); radial usually possible ideally with 7 Fr low-profile sheaths or sheathless guides, femoral superior support for particularly complex: p.355 (19.4); long duration so anticoagulation carefully monitored: p.355 (19.3)
- Previous CABG: review surgical report, prior angiograms, CT; in non-urgent cases may delay to obtain report; mistakes frequent if relying on presumed anatomy: brilakis p.335 (18.1.1)
- CTO: ad hoc discouraged in most cases; time for planning, counselling, viability; CCTA for segment and projections: brilakis p.389 (21.1); readiness for perforation: p.389 (21.2)
- STEMI: limited time but know prior revascularisation (stent thrombosis), graft anatomy, cardiac function (shock risk), comorbidities (renal failure, diabetes, future surgery), medications given; postpartum → SCAD; stressful event → takotsubo but angiography often needed: brilakis p.377 (20.1)
- Shock: early identification before refractory shock; local algorithms and shock team: brilakis p.428 (24.2.1)
- After TAVR: CT to predict reaccess; limitations (not in urgent/emergent, extra contrast, motion artefact); femoral may be preferred: brilakis p.423 (24.1.1, 24.1.4)
- Check (calcified LAD radial 6 Fr): oxford p.214; brilakis p.355
- Contrast: stop before ≥3.7 × GFR, lower threshold in CKD or single kidney; recent contrast (e.g. CT) considered: brilakis p.39 (2.6)
- Radiation: cumulative air kerma and DAP monitored; procedure usually stopped if air kerma exceeds 5–7 Gy; >15 Gy sentinel event: brilakis p.39 (2.5); radiographer warns operator at 1 Gy; operator records exposure in report and communicates to patient: oxford p.39
- Previous skin injury: limit runs, keep area out of beam; may postpone or modify: brilakis p.35, p.33
- Operator experience and staff condition (sleep deprivation) affect outcome: brilakis p.140; operator or patient fatigue among reasons for delayed PCI and staging: brilakis p.146 (7.3.1, 7.3.2)
- Multivessel: increased hypotension risk if target vessel occlusion occurs with stenoses elsewhere: oxford p.255
- CTO-complex PCI cart with covered stents and coils, readily available, restocked, known to staff: brilakis p.597 (30.23)
- Operator verifies team availability and equipment; leads briefing (cases, intra- and post-procedural care plan, technical or staffing issues, list coordinator, roles in arrest or complications): oxford p.47
- Left main elective IABP with impaired LV function, short inflations: oxford p.261
- Check (contrast plan for eGFR 40 with recent CT): brilakis p.39
- Tips: pci-planning-guide-size (p.295, p.355, oxford p.214); pci-planning-left-main (p.329); pci-planning-contrast-ceiling (p.39, p.33); pci-planning-radiation-limit (p.39, oxford p.39); pci-planning-bailout-kit (p.597, p.142, oxford p.47)
- Videos 6TrmPYWK0io, cY_nRMiZ2ss, maPX2si7W8k: notes restate registry titles only

## Lesson pm-planning-assessment

- Indication and responsible cardiologist documented; record events or symptom change since decision; prior procedures reviewed for access sites, catheters, complications, outcomes; CABG operation note; echo results; drug history; allergies or their absence; pre-admission clinic or morning of admission; fasting confirmed: oxford p.41
- Presentation, appropriateness of indication (appropriate use criteria), ongoing pain, prior catheterisation images and reports, CABG report, comorbidity list, non-adherence or noncardiac surgery within 6–12 months → PCI may be best avoided (stent thrombosis from surgery and early DAPT discontinuation), medical therapy or CABG preferred; renal failure or anticoagulated → stage non-emergent PCI; ultra-low or zero contrast in advanced CKD; contrast or latex allergy: brilakis p.29
- H&P within 30 days (outpatient) or 24 h of admission (inpatient); candidacy for stenting (Hct <30 with type and cross-match; major surgery recent or planned for next year; overt bleeding; chronic anticoagulation; non-adherence): brilakis p.30 (Table 1.1)
- Check (bowel surgery in 4 months): brilakis p.29, p.35 (1.10)
- Examination: pulse, BP, peripheral pulses, JVP, basal crackles: oxford p.41; radiation skin injury on back, all pulses, HF signs (rales, JVP, oedema): brilakis p.33; checklist (abnormal cardiac exam, CHF, cardiogenic shock, decreased distal pulses, hostile groin: massive obesity, scar, infection, ulcer; AV fistula; severe arm tremor or involuntary movements; prior radiation skin injury): brilakis p.31 (Table 1.1)
- Excessive movements may hinder the procedure: brilakis p.37 (2.1)
- Back pain affecting lying flat; HF relevant to support: brilakis p.29, p.36
- Tests: Hb, WBC, platelets, INR, potassium, creatinine + eGFR, pregnancy test: brilakis p.33; CBC and electrolytes within 14 days (outpatient) or 24 h (inpatient); pre-procedure hydration needed?; INR within 24 h for warfarin; serum beta hCG; ECG; echo; coronary CTA; prior angiograms: brilakis p.32 (Table 1.1); adequate IV access: brilakis p.31 (Table 1.1); blood count, electrolytes, renal function, glucose, lipid status, anticoagulation status on warfarin, 12-lead ECG baseline, venous cannula preferably left arm for sedation, IV fluids, emergency drugs: oxford p.41; pregnancy confirmation on arrival: oxford p.43
- Check (bloods 20 days old): brilakis p.32, p.30
- Only absolute contraindication refusal of consent; relative contraindications (AKI, pulmonary oedema, known contrast allergy, uncontrolled hypertension, active GI haemorrhage, acute stroke, untreated coagulopathy, untreated or unexplained febrile illness): oxford p.25
- Baseline patient items (comfort, pain anticipated vs unexpected, consciousness and breathing, moving all extremities, allergic signs): brilakis p.37 (2.1)
- Tips: diag-planning-check-in (oxford p.43, p.41); pci-planning-stent-candidacy (brilakis p.29, p.30, p.35); access-planning-hostile-groin (brilakis p.31, p.34, p.32)

## Lesson pm-planning-preparation

- Fasting times (2 h clear liquids incl clear/hard candies and drinks without pulp or dairy; 6 h light solids incl toast/oatmeal, liquids with dairy, pulp, infant formula; 8 h regular diet; chewing tobacco 6 h; per institutional protocol), from ASA: brilakis p.30 (Table 1.1), p.36 ref 1; ref asa-fasting-guideline-2017
- Aspirin received; aspirin allergy desensitisation; P2Y12 for planned PCI or STEMI: brilakis p.33; most patients on antiplatelet therapy; preloaded clopidogrel 300–600 mg per local policy: oxford p.41
- 2023 ESC ACS: routine P2Y12 pretreatment not recommended in NSTE-ACS with unknown anatomy and early invasive (<24 h) management: ref esc-acs-2023
- Metformin: brilakis p.33 (CKD hold day of, not before ≥48 h; without CKD check renal function and withhold if deteriorates); oxford p.42 (very low lactic acidosis risk with normal renal function; withhold day before and 2 days after)
- Insulin: brilakis p.33 (reduce for fasting); oxford p.42 (early on list, withhold morning insulin and short-acting oral hypoglycaemics, close CBG monitoring, eat and drink when recovered and resume regimen)
- Warfarin: brilakis p.33 (discontinue 5 days, INR on day, radial preferred); oxford p.42 (3–5 days, INR <2, mechanical mitral valve IV UFH stopped 2 h before, LMWH morning dose omitted)
- LMWH within 24 h dose and time; sildenafil within 24 h; immunosuppressants → avoid arterial closure devices; chronic opioids → sedation challenging: brilakis p.31 (Table 1.1)
- PDE-5 inhibitors: no nitroglycerin within 24 h of avanafil, sildenafil, vardenafil, 48 h of tadalafil: brilakis p.42 (3.2), also p.124
- DOAC table: brilakis p.34 (Table 1.2), confirmed on page image
- Check (dabigatran CrCl 42 → 4 days): brilakis p.34
- Allergy checklist (contrast pretreated; aspirin desensitisation; heparin HIT → alternative antithrombotics; latex → remove latex products; multiple allergies → consider prednisone pretreatment): brilakis p.30 (Table 1.1)
- Brilakis premedication (prednisone 50 mg at 13, 7, 1 h before or methylprednisolone 32 mg at 12 and 2 h; diphenhydramine 50 mg 1 h; cimetidine or ranitidine 1 h; emergency hydrocortisone 100 mg IV asap): brilakis p.47 (3.3). - Sedation checklist (ASA and Mallampati documented, contraindication, prior adverse reaction, IV access): brilakis p.31 (Table 1.1)
- Shave both groins if femoral planned: oxford p.41

- Sign-in whiteboard items: oxford p.55

- Consent after discussion of risks and benefits per local guidelines: oxford p.41; form checked and signature confirmed on arrival: oxford p.43
- Only absolute contraindication refusal of consent: oxford p.25
- Diagnostic risks table (death 0.1, MI 0.1, pulmonary oedema 0.1, stroke 0.1, tamponade 0.1, contrast reaction 0.4, vasovagal 2–3, arrhythmia 0.4 with VF, VT, AF, SVT, heart block, vascular 1–2 with haemorrhage, pseudoaneurysm, AV fistula, infection, dissection 0.1, air embolism 0.4, renal failure 0.7, cholesterol embolisation 1.4%); risk increases with age and comorbidity: oxford p.299

## Lesson pm-planning-classification

- PCI angiography defines length, reference diameter either side, complexity features (side branches, tortuosity, calcification, thrombus); at least two orthogonal views, avoid overlap, least foreshortening (foreshortened lesion appears shorter and more severe); separate views for proximal and distal limits sometimes: oxford p.209
- ACC/AHA published 1988, revised 1990; up to 11 features; A, B, C; B1 one B feature, B2 two or more: oxford p.211; ref ryan-ptca-guideline-1988; ref ellis-modified-acc-aha-1990
- Feature table: oxford p.212 (Table 8.2, read from page image)
- Check (16 mm eccentric irregular → B2): oxford p.212
- POBA-era estimates >85%, 60–85%, <60%; current success 99%, 92%, 90%; MACE <1%, 2–5%, 5–7%; devised in POBA era; newer systems predict better in stent era; multivariate predictors of failure (CTO, unprotected bifurcation, long lesions, obvious thrombus): oxford p.211
- SCAI 2000, simplified, divides by type C criteria and patency; success 98.3% type I to 80.2% type IV; complications ~2.3% type I, >10% type IV: oxford p.211; Table 8.3 p.212; ref krone-scai-classification-2000
- NCDR comparison numbers: ref krone-lesion-classification-stent-era-2003
- Check (occlusion ≥1 year → SCAI IV): oxford p.212
- SYNTAX score is the usual complexity measure: brilakis p.140 (7.1.6); elements (dominance with no codominance; segment weights 0.5 to 6; ×2 for 50–99%, ×5 for occlusion; occlusion extras incl side branch rules; trifurcation +3 to +6; bifurcation +1/+2 by Medina, +1 for angle <70°; aorto-ostial +1; severe tortuosity +2; length >20 mm +1; heavy calcification +2; thrombus +1; diffuse disease/small vessels +1 per segment): brilakis p.141 (Fig 7.2); ref sianos-syntax-score-2005
- CT-based CTO scores (CT-RECTOR, Korean registry): brilakis p.389; Medina and J-CTO pointers to other modules
- Lesion complexity list (CTO, bifurcation, LM, SVG, thrombus, calcification, ostial, multivessel, small vessel, diffuse): brilakis p.142 (Fig 7.3)
- Tip: pci-planning-classify-lesion (oxford p.211–212)
