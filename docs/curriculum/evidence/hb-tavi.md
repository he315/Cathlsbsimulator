# Evidence: hb-tavi (TAVI)

Module file: `src/content/manual/cath-handbook/07-tavi.ts`. Page numbers are PDF pages (Brilakis as in the
extracts; Oxford PDF page = printed page + 21). References are by registry id; new ones are in the module's
`refs` export with PMIDs.

How the references were read:
- `esc-vhd-2025`: publisher HTML (academic.oup.com, Eur Heart J 46:4635). The HTML body text available covered the
  front matter to section 9.2; recommendation tables are images and were not readable. Only sentences read in that
  text are used: sections 6.1, 8.2.3, 8.5, 8.5.1, 8.5.1.1, 8.5.1.2, 8.5.1.3, the bioprosthesis points list and the
  mode-of-intervention paragraph in section 8 (just before section 8.6), the valve-in-valve paragraphs in section 8,
  and section 8.6 (surveillance). Round 1 re-read: the age-70 paragraph, the neo-skirt / TAV-in-SAV / TAV-in-TAV
  paragraphs, the small-annulus text, the explant mortality and the bicuspid and iliofemoral sentences were checked
  again on the publisher page. No class or level of recommendation is quoted from it; section 14 (prosthetic valves)
  was not readable.
- `esc-vhd-2021` section 11.3.2.1 (antithrombotic therapy after TAVI) body text read in round 1: meta-analysis of more
  major or life-threatening bleeding with DAPT than aspirin; POPular TAVI cohort A less bleeding with aspirin.
- `esc-pacing-2021`: the TAVI pacing section could not be read on the publisher page (the fetched text ends in section
  7). Its two statements used here (pre-existing RBBB with a new conduction disturbance: early permanent pacing should

  are attributed to that lesson in the text.

  423–427, 497–502.

- `esc-achd-2020`: publisher HTML (Eur Heart J 42:563), sections 4.8 (RVOTO) and 4.10 (tetralogy) text and the
  Figure 7 caption; the 4.8 recommendation table itself is an image.
- PMC full text read: `francone-escr-tavi-imaging-2020` (PMC7160220), `lederman-coronary-obstruction-2019`

## Points where sources differ or are dated

- ESCR 2019 advised against routine coronary CT before TAVI; the 2025 ESC/EACTS guideline says omitting invasive
  angiography should be considered after a good-quality CT. Both are given, dated.
- Coronary compression in TPVI: 5% on test balloon (Morray, Brighenti) against "under 2%" in the 2020 ESC ACHD
  guideline, which counts clinical complications; the lesson gives both and says what each measures.
- Tamburino 2020 states that European guidelines recommend 72 hours of monitoring after TAVI before pacing for complete
  heart block; this predates the 2021 ESC pacing guideline and is not taught; the lesson points to sh-tavi-pvl-conduction.
  Its statement that new LBBB alone (without pre-existing RBBB) is not a reason to prolong the stay is now paired with the
  2021 pacing guideline's caution for broad LBBB or a long PR.
- Oxford's sign-out wording speaks of dual antiplatelet therapy after a TAVI valve; the 2021 ESC/EACTS text and POPular
  TAVI cohort A favour aspirin alone. Both are given.
- The 2025 bioprosthesis points list says "aortic root enlargement"; the 2025 text on small annuli says "annular
  enlargement". The lesson gives both, attributed.
- The ESCR text describes the coplanar view with the left coronary cusp on the left and the non-coronary cusp on the
  right, the reverse of the Academy lesson sh-tavi-deploy (NCC left, RCC middle, LCC right). The lesson states only
  "RCC in the middle, cusp bases evenly spaced" and leaves the side convention to sh-tavi-deploy.
